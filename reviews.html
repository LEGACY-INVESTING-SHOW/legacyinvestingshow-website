Legacy Investing Show reviews: common questions

Is Legacy Investing Show legit?

Yes. Legacy Investing Show, LLC is a registered company based in Herriman, Utah that sells paid courses and coaching. The Better Business Bureau lists it under Real Estate School, and the company is not BBB accredited. Its main Trustpilot profile, Legacy Investing Show By Preston Seo, carries a 4.2 score across 66 reviews.

What do Legacy Investing Show reviews say?

On Trustpilot, 77% of the 66 reviews are five star, 11% four star, 3% three star and 9% one star. The positive reviews name the tax strategies, the community and the step-by-step plan. The one-star reviews repeat four things: refunds, slow replies, a price that moved during the sales call, and the fact that most video testimonials are interviews Preston Seo records himself.
