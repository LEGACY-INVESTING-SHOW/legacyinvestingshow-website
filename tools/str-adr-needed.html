What this calculates

Solve for the average daily rate an STR needs after platform fees, given occupancy, monthly costs, and a profit target.

How to use it

  1. Set target profit to 0 to see pure break-even ADR.
  2. If comps cannot print this ADR, cut costs or skip the lease.
  3. Run a winter occupancy, not just summer.

Common mistakes

  • Using 80% occupancy in a market that prints 55%.
  • Forgetting owner stays, which are occupancy without ADR.
  • Treating a listing's 'similar listings' map as a comp set.

Formula

ADR = ((monthly costs + target profit) ÷ occupied nights) ÷ (1 − fee%).

How the STR ADR needed calculator works

Works backwards from what you must clear each month to the nightly rate the listing has to average at a given occupancy.

Method

Occupied nights per month = occupancy % × (365 ÷ 12).

Net needed per night = (fixed monthly costs + target monthly profit) ÷ occupied nights.

Nightly rate needed = net needed per night ÷ (1 − platform and merchant fee %). This is the headline.

Assumptions

  • Target profit is cash after the costs you entered, before income tax.
  • Occupancy is a full-year average; dynamic pricing, weekly discounts, and seasonality collapse into one blended rate.
  • Guest-paid cleaning is excluded from the rate.

Limitations

  • The answer is only useful if you compare it to what comparable listings actually average. The tool does not know your market.
  • Variable per-booking costs are not separated out.
  • Not investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What nightly rate should I charge on Airbnb?+

This tool gives you the floor: the average rate your costs and profit target require at a realistic occupancy. Price against the market from there. If the required rate sits above what comparable listings achieve, the fix is lower costs or a different property, not a higher price.

Is ADR the same as my listed price?+

No. ADR is the average of what guests actually paid per booked night after discounts and before cleaning fees. Enter the blended number, not the headline price.

How do occupancy and rate trade off?+

Lowering the occupancy input raises the rate needed. Run it at your market's slow-season occupancy to see the rate you would need to survive on, and at peak to see your margin.

Does higher profit change my taxes?+

Cash profit and taxable profit differ. Depreciation on the property and furnishings can leave a profitable STR with a tax loss on paper, which under the short-term rental rules a material participant can use against other income. The tax side is not in this tool.
