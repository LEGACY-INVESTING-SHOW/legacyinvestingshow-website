What this calculates

Estimates the workspace share of eligible home costs using floor area. Eligibility rules are strict and this page does not test them, so treat the output as arithmetic rather than permission.

How to use it

  1. Start with office square footage and work down the form, or load an example to begin from a realistic case.
  2. Read the headline result alongside the supporting rows, which show the intermediate figures behind it.
  3. Check the assumptions. They decide what the number includes and, more importantly, what it leaves out.
  4. Sweep office square footage on the response curve to see how much it actually moves the answer.
  5. Run a cautious case as well as an optimistic one before using the estimate in a decision.

Common mistakes

  • Counting a corner of a room that is also family space.
  • Including the entire mortgage payment rather than just the interest portion.
  • Claiming a share larger than the space genuinely justifies, which is a common audit trigger.

Formula

Estimate = eligible annual costs × (office square footage ÷ home square footage)

FAQ

Can employees claim this?+

Under current US rules, generally no for W-2 employees. The deduction is aimed at the self-employed. Rules differ by country and change over time, so check current guidance for your situation.

What counts as an eligible cost?+

Typically rent or mortgage interest, property tax, utilities, insurance, and general repairs for the whole home. Costs that apply only to the office itself are usually deductible in full rather than apportioned.

What is the simplified method?+

The US also allows a flat rate per square foot up to a capped area, which requires no expense tracking. It is often smaller than the actual-cost method this page models, but far less work.

Does claiming this affect selling my home?+

It can, through depreciation recapture on the business-use portion. That is one reason to raise it with a tax professional rather than deciding from a calculator.

Are these numbers financial advice?+

No. They are educational estimates based on the inputs and assumptions on this page. Confirm important decisions with a qualified professional and your own documents.
