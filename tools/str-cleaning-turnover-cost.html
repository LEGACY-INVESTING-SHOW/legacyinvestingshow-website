What this calculates

Multiply monthly turnovers by cleaner, supplies, and laundry to see the operating cost most STR spreadsheets understate.

How to use it

  1. Count actual turnovers from the last 90 days, not a hope.
  2. If you self-clean, still enter a wage you would pay someone else.
  3. High turnover with thin ADR is how arbitrage dies.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming the cleaning fee is profit.
  • Ignoring laundry on back-to-back nights.
  • Underwriting 15 turnovers at a 2-hour self-clean.

Formula

Monthly cost = turnovers × (cleaner + supplies + laundry).

FAQ

Should the guest cleaning fee be income?+

Usually it is an offset to this cost, not extra profit.

How do I estimate turnovers?+

Booked nights ÷ average stay length. A 2-night average stay at 60% occupancy is a lot of cleans.

Midterm stays help this number?+

Yes. Fewer turnovers is the whole point of the midterm comparison.
