What this calculates

Estimate debt-service coverage ratio from annual NOI and an amortizing loan's principal-and-interest payment.

How to use it

  1. Many DSCR programs want 1.0 to 1.25. If you are at 0.95, the file is fragile.
  2. Rebuild NOI from leases, not the broker OM.
  3. Raise rate 100 bps and see if the ratio still clears.

Common mistakes

  • Using gross rent instead of NOI.
  • Comparing to a 1.25 requirement with an interest-only quote you will not keep.
  • Ignoring impounds that do not sit in NOI.

Formula

DSCR = annual NOI ÷ (monthly P&I × 12).

FAQ

What DSCR do lenders want?+

It varies. 1.0 is a common floor; 1.25 is a healthier file. Ask the actual program.

Does this include taxes and insurance?+

They should already be in NOI as expenses. Do not subtract them again from debt service unless your lender uses PITIA in the denominator.

STR income?+

Lenders often haircut it. Use a lower NOI.

How the DSCR calculator works

Debt-service coverage ratio: how many times annual net operating income covers the annual principal-and-interest payment on an amortizing loan.

Method

Monthly P&I is computed from loan amount, interest rate, and amortizing term using the standard mortgage payment formula.

Annual debt service = monthly P&I × 12.

DSCR = annual NOI ÷ annual debt service. This is the headline.

NOI cushion above debt = NOI − annual debt service, the dollars of room before the property stops covering its loan.

Assumptions

  • NOI already has taxes, insurance, management, maintenance, and vacancy expensed out of it. Do not subtract them again.
  • Fully amortizing loan. An interest-only quote would show a higher ratio the tool does not model.
  • No lender haircut to income; DSCR lenders often discount projected rent or short-term rental revenue.

Limitations

  • Lender programs differ on the denominator: some use PITIA (principal, interest, taxes, insurance, association dues) rather than P&I alone, which produces a lower ratio.
  • Rate and term at closing may differ from the quote you enter, and a rate cap or balloon changes the picture.
  • It does not test whether the NOI is realistic; it only divides.
  • Not lending or investment advice.

Frequently asked questions

What DSCR do lenders require for a rental property loan?+

It varies by program. Many use 1.0 as a floor and treat 1.25 as a healthier file, but the exact threshold, the rent source they accept, and whether they use P&I or full PITIA differ. Ask the specific lender and match this tool's denominator to theirs.

Can I qualify for a DSCR loan with Airbnb income?+

Some lenders accept short-term rental income, usually with a history of receipts or a third-party projection, and often with a haircut. Run this tool with a discounted NOI to see whether the ratio still clears their floor.

Is a DSCR loan the same as a conventional investor loan?+

No. DSCR loans qualify the property on its own income rather than your personal debt-to-income ratio, which is why W-2 earners with high personal debt or self-employed borrowers use them. They typically cost more in rate and points.

What DSCR should I target as an investor, not just for the lender?+

Higher than the floor. A ratio near 1.0 means one vacancy or repair pushes the property into a deficit. The NOI cushion row shows how many dollars of bad news you can absorb in a year.
