What this calculates

Subtract effective rent from housemates or extra units from PITI and owner-occupied operating costs to see what housing actually costs.

How to use it

  1. Use PITI from the mortgage tool, not a round number from memory.
  2. If you rent rooms, vacancy is roommate turnover.
  3. A negative net housing cost means the property pays you to live there — until a void month.

Common mistakes

  • Ignoring that you still need a bedroom when the tenant leaves.
  • Underwriting 100% occupancy on three rooms in a house you also occupy.
  • Forgetting utilities you agreed to cover.

Formula

Net housing = PITI + owner ops − rent × (1 − vacancy%).

FAQ

Is house hacking the same as a duplex rental?+

You occupy part of it. Financing and tax treatment differ from a pure rental.

Can this be an STR on the other unit?+

Yes, but then use STR occupancy and ops, not a simple vacancy percent.

Does this help with DTI?+

Some lenders count a fraction of rental income. Do not assume 100% of this rent lands on the application.
