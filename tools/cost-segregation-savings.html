What this calculates

Estimate first-year tax savings from reclassifying rental basis into short-life property, bonus depreciation, and a study fee.

How to use it

  1. Start with purchase basis and a conservative land percentage.
  2. Enter the share a study would likely move into personal property or land improvements.
  3. Stress the tax rate. If a few points of rate flip the net, get a real quote.

Common mistakes

  • Modeling bonus you cannot use because of passive or taxable-income limits.
  • Ignoring recapture when you sell.
  • Treating the study cost as optional documentation.

Formula

Extra dep ≈ bonus on reclassified basis + remaining short-life dep − 27.5-year straight line. Savings = extra dep × tax rate − study cost.

FAQ

Is this a cost segregation study?+

No. It is a planning screen so you can decide whether to price a study. The study creates the allocations your CPA files.

What bonus rate should I use in 2026?+

Qualifying property acquired after January 19, 2025 can use 100% bonus. Confirm placed-in-service dates with your advisor.

Do I need to be a real estate professional?+

Not always. Short-term rental material participation can matter more for W-2 owners. This calculator does not test those hours.

How the cost segregation savings calculator works

A planning screen for whether a cost segregation study is worth pricing on one rental. It compares year-one depreciation with a study against plain 27.5-year straight line, then subtracts the study fee.

Method

Depreciable building basis = purchase basis × (1 − land share). Land is never depreciated.

Basis moved to short-life property = building basis × the share you expect a study to reclassify into 5-, 7-, and 15-year property.

Bonus depreciation = reclassified basis × the bonus percentage. 100% is the 2026 default for qualifying property acquired after January 19, 2025.

Year-one depreciation with a study = bonus + (building basis − reclassified basis) ÷ 27.5 + (reclassified basis − bonus) ÷ 5.

Extra year-one depreciation = that figure minus plain straight line (building basis ÷ 27.5), floored at zero.

Year-one tax savings = extra depreciation × your combined marginal rate, minus the study and filing cost. That net figure is the headline.

Assumptions

  • Tax year 2026 planning defaults: 100% bonus on qualifying property acquired after January 19, 2025. Property under a binding contract on or before that date stays on the old phase-down (20% in 2026), so change the bonus input if that is you.
  • The building is residential rental on 27.5-year straight line with no mid-month convention. Commercial 39-year property would show a smaller straight-line baseline.
  • Everything reclassified that bonus does not cover is treated as 5-year property. A real study spreads it across 5-, 7-, and 15-year classes.
  • Combined marginal rate is whatever you enter. The tool adds no state tax and no net investment income tax on its own.
  • Savings are year one only. It does not show the smaller deductions you give up in later years.

Limitations

  • It does not test whether you can use the loss. Rental losses are passive unless you qualify as a real estate professional or the property is a short-term rental you materially participate in. Suspended losses carry forward instead of cutting this year's tax.
  • The excess business loss cap in Section 461(l) and the at-risk rules can also limit how much of a large depreciation loss offsets W-2 or other income in the same year.
  • Depreciation recapture on sale is ignored. Reclassified components come back mostly as Section 1245 recapture at ordinary rates, and the building share as unrecaptured Section 1250 gain at up to 25%.
  • The reclassification share is your guess, not an engineering result. Only a study by a qualified provider produces allocations a CPA can file.
  • Not tax advice. Federal rules only.

Frequently asked questions

Does cost segregation work for an Airbnb?+

Yes. A short-term rental is depreciable real estate, so a study can move furniture-grade components, land improvements, and similar assets into 5-, 7-, and 15-year classes that qualify for bonus depreciation. Whether the resulting loss offsets your W-2 income depends on the passive loss rules, not on the study.

Can cost segregation reduce my W-2 taxes?+

Only if the loss is non-passive. The two common routes are real estate professional status (more than 750 hours and more than half of your working time in real property trades, plus material participation) or a short-term rental with an average guest stay of seven days or less where you materially participate. Otherwise the loss is suspended and carries forward.

Does bonus depreciation apply in 2026?+

Yes. Qualified property acquired after January 19, 2025 gets 100% bonus depreciation, made permanent by the 2025 tax law in Section 168(k). Property under a binding contract on or before January 19, 2025 stays on the old phase-down and gets 20% in 2026. The building shell never qualifies; only the short-life components a study identifies do.

Why does the result go negative on smaller properties?+

Because the study fee is fixed while the extra depreciation scales with basis and tax rate. If the net is negative or thin, a study may still make sense across a portfolio or on a higher-basis property, but on this property the tool is telling you to get a real quote before committing.

What happens when I sell the property?+

Accelerated depreciation lowers your basis, so more of the sale is taxable gain. The building share is unrecaptured Section 1250 gain at up to 25%; the reclassified components are mostly Section 1245 recapture at ordinary rates. A 1031 exchange can defer it. Run the depreciation recapture calculator with your expected exit numbers.
