What this calculates

Estimate realized gain, boot, and deferred gain on a like-kind rental exchange using sale price, basis, and replacement price.

How to use it

  1. Use the actual sale price and a realistic cost of sale.
  2. Enter adjusted basis after depreciation, not original purchase price.
  3. If you keep cash or buy down, put it in boot.

Common mistakes

  • Using original purchase price as basis.
  • Forgetting selling costs, which reduce realized gain.
  • Treating a vacation home as automatically like-kind.

Formula

Realized gain = sale price − selling costs − adjusted basis. Recognized gain = min(realized gain, boot). Deferred = realized − recognized.

FAQ

What is boot?+

Value you take out of the exchange: cash, debt relief you do not replace, or a cheaper replacement. Boot can make part of the gain taxable now.

Does deferred gain disappear?+

No. It usually reduces basis in the replacement property and shows up later on sale unless you exchange again.

Can I 1031 a short-term rental?+

It depends on use, holding, and whether it is investment property. Confirm with a QI and a CPA before you list.
