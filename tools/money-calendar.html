Money Calendar

A simple monthly routine for your investing, taxes, benefits and spending.

Import into Apple, Google or Outlook Calendar. Adds 12 all-day reviews on your chosen day, including past months. These are planning reminders, not official deadlines. Set alerts in your calendar app.

2026 federal dates to check

  • April 15, 2026: usual deadline for 2025 individual federal returns, tax payments and eligible 2025 IRA/HSA contributions.
  • 2026 estimated income tax installments: April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027, if required.
  • December 31: review year-end actions in advance. Payroll and provider cutoffs may be earlier; not every retirement contribution has this deadline.

These are general federal dates, not a determination that you owe an installment. Exceptions and disaster relief can change them. Sources: IRS filing dates and IRS estimated tax guidance.

January

Reset your automatic investing

  • Review take-home pay, essential spending and your savings goal.
  • Set automatic transfers that fit your cash flow; check the new year's contribution limits.

February

Gather your tax documents

  • Collect income forms, deductible expense records and HSA receipts.
  • Compare actual spending with your January plan.

March

Check last year's contribution room

  • Review prior-year IRA and HSA eligibility and unused contribution room before the usual April tax-filing deadline.
  • If contributing for last year, select that tax year with your provider. A filing extension generally does not extend this contribution deadline.

April

Use your return to plan ahead

  • Once your return is filed, review what caused a refund or balance due.
  • Adjust current-year withholding or estimated payments with your tax professional if needed.

May

Review your cash cushion

  • Compare emergency savings with essential monthly expenses.
  • Plan ahead for summer travel and other irregular bills.

June

Check your midyear progress

  • Compare contributions and account balances with your annual goals.
  • Adjust automatic transfers for the remaining months if your income or expenses changed.

July

Review debt and recurring costs

  • Review loan rates, balances and your next payoff priority.
  • Cancel subscriptions you no longer use and compare recurring bills.

August

Review who and what is protected

  • Check beneficiaries and important account information.
  • Review insurance coverage after major life changes.

September

Forecast income and taxes

  • Estimate full-year income, including bonuses and side income.
  • Check withholding and estimated payments before year-end with your tax professional.

October

Set your holiday budget

  • Choose spending amounts for gifts, travel and celebrations.
  • Move money into a dedicated spending category before shopping.

November

Review employer benefits

  • Compare next year's health plan, HSA or FSA options and retirement benefits.
  • Confirm your employer's actual enrollment dates; they vary by plan.

December

Finish your year-end review

  • Check remaining workplace retirement contributions and payroll cutoffs; review planned giving.
  • Review realized gains and losses with a tax professional before trading. Check wash-sale rules and each action's deadline.

Use your own deadlines

This is a personal planning calendar. Confirm federal and state tax dates, disaster extensions, employer enrollment windows and provider cutoffs separately. Prior-year contributions depend on eligibility; IRA contribution eligibility and deductibility are different questions. Rules were reviewed in September 2026; recheck them for 2027.

Sources: IRS IRA reminders, IRS HSA contribution rules.
