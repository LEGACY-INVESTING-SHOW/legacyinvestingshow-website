Who is Airbnb Ascension for, and who is it not for?

It is for someone who wants monthly cash flow from a side business alongside their job, has startup capital for a deposit and furnishing, and is willing to research markets and call landlords with scripts, roleplay, and a coach behind them. It is not for people who want to test the idea cheaply, people without a reserve of working capital on top of the program, people who want to own the property (that is a different path), or people who cannot give it real hours in the first 60 days.

How long does it take?

The standard program runs six months and is scoped to one property, with a 12-month Scale version for clients who want to keep going. Launch is front-loaded: market research, landlord calls, the lease, furnishing, and the listing. Clients on the reviews page report anywhere from 30 days to about five months from joining to a signed lease or first booking, and once your systems are automated the ongoing time commitment is about 4 hours a week.

Do I need to own real estate?

No. Airbnb Ascension is rental arbitrage: you sign a 12-month lease on a property in an area where short-term rentals are allowed, furnish it, list it on Airbnb, VRBO, and Booking.com, and target cash flow of roughly two to three times the monthly lease cost. Your coaches vouch for you with landlords as references. If you want to own the asset for equity and tax benefits, that is a different program.
