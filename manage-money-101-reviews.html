The short answer

Manage Money 101 is not a separate paid program, so there is no separate set of Manage Money 101 reviews. It is the free masterclass and registration funnel Legacy Investing Show runs at join.managemoney101.com: the tax-strategy training that introduces the material and leads to a strategy call about the paid programs.

The Trustpilot profile for Legacy Investing Show By Preston Seo lists managemoney101.com as the business website, so the reviews you are looking for are the company's reviews: a 4.2 TrustScore from 66 reviews, plus the Legacy Wealth Blueprint case studies on the reviews page.

What Manage Money 101 actually is

  • The domain. managemoney101.com is where Legacy Investing Show hosts registration for its free Tax Strategy Masterclass, at join.managemoney101.com/tax-strategies. Every masterclass button on this site points there.
  • The masterclass. Tax-focused training for high earners, business owners, and investors, taught live by Preston Seo with a Q&A at the end. It introduces the material and leads to a strategy call.
  • The Trustpilot listing. The profile Legacy Investing Show By Preston Seo, at trustpilot.com/review/firstairbnb.com, lists managemoney101.com as the business website. Reviews left there are reviews of the company.
  • The slides. Presentation slides transcribed on the reviews page carry the banner GO TO MANAGEMONEY101.COM, which is how attendees are sent to register.
  • What it is not. The site publishes no separate product page, curriculum, or price under the Manage Money 101 name. If someone described a paid program to you under that name, ask on the call which program it maps to.

How it relates to the Legacy Wealth Blueprint

The sequence is: masterclass, then strategy call, then a program matched to your situation. The site's own decision tree has three branches.

  • You want to optimise what you already have (taxes, debt, accounts, protection): the Legacy Wealth Blueprint, a twelve-month program with a written plan, weekly coaching, and a dedicated guide.
  • You want monthly cash flow from a side business without buying property: Airbnb Ascension, built on the Airbnb Arbitrage Roadmap curriculum.
  • You have the capital to buy a short-term rental and want it done for you: STR Concierge.

Who the masterclass is for, and who it is not for

The site describes the masterclass as tax-focused training for high earners, business owners, and investors. The people the brand serves typically earn $75K or more and fall into four groups: high earners paying more tax than they expected, business owners deciding how to structure and pay themselves, real estate investors sizing up depreciation and entity choices, and professionals building a first wealth system.

  • Not for you if you want a review of a product called Manage Money 101. There is none; the reviews are of Legacy Investing Show.
  • Not for you if you want a published price for the paid programs before any conversation. Pricing is only discussed on the strategy call.
  • Not for you if you want passive, index-fund-only advice. The material is about tax, entity, debt, and account decisions that require implementation.

Pricing

The masterclass itself is free. The paid programs it leads to are a different matter:

Legacy Investing Show does not publish a price for the Legacy Wealth Blueprint, and this page does not either. The site's stated position is that programs are matched to different situations and goals rather than sold off a single published menu, that custom options, payment plans, and financing exist, and that what someone invests depends on their picture and how they choose to pay.

The number is discussed on the strategy call. Before that call, decide what a written plan, twelve months of coaching, a dedicated guide, and a CPA brief would need to be worth to you, and read the documented client results with the disclaimer attached. Then compare what you hear against that, not against the sales pitch.

Refund terms

The site's terms of service say this and only this: access to paid products and services is governed by the offer terms shown at checkout, including any refund or review policies presented there.

The program page adds one instruction: ask on the call about the No Client Left Behind Guarantee and get its current written terms, including what actions it requires, how a return on your program investment is measured, and when continued support applies.

No other refund terms are published on this site. Do not rely on anything you have not seen in writing at checkout.
