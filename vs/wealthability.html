Quick verdict

These two solve different problems. WealthAbility is built around Tom Wheelwright's Tax-Free Wealth framework, and its main product is a match with a CPA firm in its network that plans and, in most engagements, files. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is a 12-month coached education program: you learn the strategies across five pillars, get a written plan and a 1-on-1 guide, and hand a CPA Implementation Brief to your own CPA, who decides what applies and files.

Pick WealthAbility if you want a vetted CPA to own the tax work and you already run a business or hold investments that give a planner real levers. Pick Legacy Wealth Blueprint if you want to understand your own money well enough to direct your CPA, if you are a W-2 earner whose problem is wider than taxes, or if you want to keep the CPA you have. Many people should do both, in that order.

What WealthAbility is

The education and advisory business founded by Tom Wheelwright, CPA, author of Tax-Free Wealth and a long-time Rich Dad Advisor. Its central idea is that the tax code is a set of incentives for business owners and investors, and its main offer is matching clients with network CPA firms trained to plan around them.

Format. Books, live trainings and events on the education side; a consultation, then a match with a network CPA firm on the advisory side. Advisory pricing is not published; it is quoted after the consultation and varies by firm and scope. Verify the current offering on its site.

Known for

  • Tax-Free Wealth and the Rich Dad lineage, with a strong business and real estate emphasis
  • A network of CPA firms trained in one consistent planning method
  • Live trainings for people who want the framework before or instead of hiring

Best for

  • Business owners and real estate investors who want a CPA relationship where planning, not just filing, is the point
  • People who have read Tax-Free Wealth, agree with it, and want a CPA who practises it
  • Clients willing to switch CPAs to get the work done by a professional

Not ideal for

  • W-2 earners with no business and no rentals, who give a planner fewer levers
  • People whose problem is wider than taxes: debt, credit, retirement accounts, insurance, estate, a first side income
  • Anyone who wants to keep their current CPA and direct them better

Why people compare WealthAbility and Legacy Wealth Blueprint

  • They read Tax-Free Wealth, loved it, then realised the advisory path means changing CPAs and paying for a full planning engagement.
  • They are W-2 employees and the framework keeps pointing at business owners and investors.
  • They want to understand the strategies rather than outsource the whole thing.
  • Their problem is not only tax: debt, retirement accounts, insurance and a first side income need the same plan.

Where Legacy Wealth Blueprint fits

Legacy Wealth Blueprint fits when you want to understand your own money and run the plan with support rather than delegate it. You get a custom 12-month Wealth Strategy Snapshot, a 1-on-1 guide who checks in every two weeks, weekly live coaching with replays, 100+ lessons across five pillars, and a partner network with a tax strategist and an estate attorney at client pricing. Near year end you receive a CPA Implementation Brief for the CPA you already use. Details are on the program page.

Where Legacy Wealth Blueprint does not fit

Legacy Wealth Blueprint is not a CPA firm. Preston is a personal finance educator, not a CPA or an attorney. The team does not file returns, give legal advice or contact your CPA; you hand over the brief yourself. If you want a professional who owns the tax work end to end, WealthAbility's network or a good local planning CPA is the right buy and we are not.

It is also the wrong fit if your income is well under six figures, if paying would come out of an emergency fund, or if you want a real-estate-only program. Real estate is one pillar of five here; WealthAbility's framework goes deeper on it.

How to decide

  1. Write down what you own: W-2 only, a business, rentals, or a mix. Owners and investors give a CPA network the most to plan around; W-2-only earners gain more from a whole-picture plan.

  2. Decide learn or delegate. If you never want to understand the strategy, hire a firm. If you want to direct your CPA, join a program.

  3. Decide whether you will change CPAs. WealthAbility generally means moving the work to a network firm; Legacy Wealth Blueprint means keeping yours.

  4. List your non-tax problems. Debt, retirement accounts, insurance or a first side income are untouched by a tax-only engagement.

  5. Ask WealthAbility which firm you would be matched with, who there does your work, whether filing is included, the fee structure and the guarantee. Ask Legacy Wealth Blueprint what the Wealth Strategy Snapshot contains, who your guide is, how the CPA brief works with your CPA, and what the No Client Left Behind Guarantee requires in writing.

Questions people ask

Is WealthAbility worth it?

For a business owner or investor who wants a CPA firm trained in one planning method and will move their tax work there, it can be. For a W-2 earner with no business or property, a planner has fewer levers, and a broader education program may return more.

Does WealthAbility do your taxes?

WealthAbility itself is the education and matching business. The planning and, in most engagements, the filing are done by the network CPA firm you are matched with. Confirm scope with that specific firm before you engage.

Is Legacy Wealth Blueprint a CPA firm?

No. It is a coached education program with a written plan, a 1-on-1 guide, weekly coaching and a CPA Implementation Brief for the CPA you already use. The partner network includes a tax strategist and an estate attorney at client pricing, but the program does not file returns or give legal advice.

Which is better for W-2 earners, WealthAbility or Legacy Wealth Blueprint?

For most W-2-only earners, Legacy Wealth Blueprint: it is built for that profile, covers debt, accounts and protection alongside tax, and needs no business to be useful. If a W-2 earner also owns rentals or a side business, WealthAbility's network becomes stronger and the two can work together.

Can I use WealthAbility and Legacy Wealth Blueprint together?

Yes, and order matters. Learn the strategies and get your written plan first, then take the CPA Implementation Brief to a planning CPA, from the WealthAbility network or elsewhere. You will ask better questions.

How much does WealthAbility cost?

Advisory pricing is not published; it is quoted after the consultation and depends on the firm and scope. Legacy Wealth Blueprint does not publish a price either; it is discussed on the strategy call along with what is included.
