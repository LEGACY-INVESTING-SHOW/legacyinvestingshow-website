Questions people ask first

How is this different from YouTube videos and finance books?

Videos and books give you general knowledge, not a system built around your own numbers. The program adds step-by-step implementation, direct coaching, and accountability.

Can I still join if I don't own a business yet?

Yes. Many students start without one. We work on building income streams and optimizing the finances you already have, whether you are a W-2 employee or an entrepreneur.

What if my partner or spouse isn't on board yet?

That is common. Start with the free resources and go through them together. The strategies and examples are concrete enough to discuss as a decision rather than a leap of faith.

I don't have time for this right now

Most students work full-time. The strategies are designed to run alongside a normal schedule, and two to three hours a week is enough to keep moving.

How do I know if this is for me?

If you make good money, pay more tax than you would like, or want income that does not depend on your next paycheck, it is for you. Students typically earn $75K+.

What if I'm not good at any of this finance stuff?

You don't need to be. Every strategy breaks into steps you follow in order, and you can start with one.
