What Tax Alchemy actually is

Two different things share the Karlton Dennis name, and people mix them up.

Tax Alchemy is the firm. Its site says Karlton Dennis is CEO and lists tax strategy analysis, entity structuring, implementation help, real estate tax planning, cost segregation, and 1031 exchange work. It also offers tax preparation, but only to advisory clients. On pricing the site says the flat fee "can range from as little as $32k up to $50k."

Tax Strategy Accelerator is the education product. The page lists video courses, a community, recordings of weekly calls with the private client group, and AMA sessions with tax advisors. The price on the page is "$97 Per Month" with a 14 day risk-free window.

So if you saw a $32,000 quote and went looking for options, you may only need the $97 membership. And if you wanted someone to do the work, a $97 membership will not do it. Get clear on which one you were shopping for first.

How to choose

Answer four questions in order. They knock out most of the list fast.

1. Do you want to learn it or hand it off?

If you want to hand it off, you are shopping for a firm. That means Tax Alchemy, Tax Goddess, or a local CPA who does planning. Expect four or five figures and expect to be quoted on a call.

If you want to learn it, you are shopping for education. Tax Strategy Accelerator, Navi Maraj's course, Business Owner HQ, and Legacy Wealth Blueprint sit here.

2. Do you already have a business or a rental?

This is the question that decides how much any program can do for you. Entity planning needs a business. Depreciation and cost segregation need property. If you are a W-2 earner with neither, a program cannot invent deductions for you. It can only help you build the thing that creates them.

3. Do you need answers or do you need a plan?

Anderson Platinum and Business Owner HQ are built for questions. You bring a situation, you get an answer, you move on. That works when you already know your direction.

A plan is different. You want someone to look at your whole picture once and tell you the order of operations. That is what a written wealth plan or a paid planning engagement gives you.

4. What is the total, in writing, before the call?

Every seller in this category sells on a call. Ask for the full price, the payment terms, the refund window, and what happens when the term ends. Get it in an email. If the number only appears after a 45 minute pitch, that is information too.

Where Legacy Wealth Blueprint fits, honestly

Legacy Wealth Blueprint is our program, so read this section with that in mind.

The price is $9,800 paid by cash or card. It is $10,800 if you finance through Splitit. There is a smaller option, LWB Course plus AI, at $1,500 for applicants earning under $75,000.

What you get: lifetime access to a 100+ video curriculum across five pillars (tax, debt and credit, income, investing, protection), 12 months in the private community, weekly live coaching calls with replays, a custom 12-month wealth plan, an optional review of your prior year return, a 1-on-1 guide with biweekly check-ins, an AI advisor trained on the system, and access to a partner network of tax, estate, credit, and entity specialists.

Who it is not for. Be blunt with yourself here.

  • It is not for you if you want someone to file your return. We do not file. Near the end of the year you get a CPA implementation brief that you forward to your own CPA. Nobody on the team talks to your CPA directly.
  • It is not for you if $9,800 comes out of your emergency fund or goes onto a card you cannot clear. Pay off expensive debt first.
  • It is not for you if you earn under about $75,000. The $1,500 course option exists for that reason. The full program assumes enough income for the strategies to move real money.
  • It is not for you if all you want is a cheap library of tax videos. Tax Strategy Accelerator is $97 a month and it is good at that job.
  • It is not for you if you only want real estate deal coaching. That is a different program.

What to check before you pay anyone

  1. Find the price on the seller's own page, not on a review site.
  2. Ask what happens at the end of the term. Does access stop?
  3. Ask who answers your questions. The founder, a team, or a forum?
  4. Ask whether tax filing is included. Usually it is not.
  5. Read the refund policy before the call, not after.
  6. Search the name plus "refund" and read what comes back with a cool head.

A program is worth its price when it changes what you actually do. Nothing on this list works if you buy it and never open it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Tax Alchemy cost?

Tax Alchemy says on its own site that it charges a flat fee based on the strategies you need, and that the fee "can range from as little as $32k up to $50k." That is a service fee, not a course price.

Is Tax Strategy Accelerator the same thing as Tax Alchemy?

No. Both are run by Karlton Dennis, but Tax Strategy Accelerator is a $97 per month education membership with courses, a community, and call recordings. Tax Alchemy is the done-for-you planning firm.

What is the cheapest real alternative to Tax Alchemy?

The lowest published price in this comparison is Tax Strategy Accelerator at $97 per month. Navi Maraj CPA's self-paced course is listed at $1,498. Both are education, so you still do the work yourself or hire a CPA to file.

Do I need a business or rental property for these programs to help?

Most advanced strategies need one or the other. Entity planning needs a business. Depreciation and cost segregation need property. A W-2 earner with neither has a much shorter list of moves.

What does Legacy Wealth Blueprint cost?

Legacy Wealth Blueprint is $9,800 paid by cash or card, or $10,800 if you finance it through Splitit. A smaller option, LWB Course plus AI, is $1,500 for applicants earning under $75,000.

Does any program in this list file my tax return?

Tax Alchemy offers tax preparation to its advisory clients. Tax Goddess and LYFE Accounting are firms. The education programs, Legacy Wealth Blueprint included, do not file returns for you.

How do I check a price before I buy?

Open the seller's own page, screenshot the price and the refund terms, and ask for the total in writing before any call. Prices and discounts in this category change often.
