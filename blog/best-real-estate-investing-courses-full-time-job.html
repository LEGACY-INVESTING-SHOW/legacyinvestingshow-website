Do the free stuff first

Reddit threads about real estate coaching almost always have the same top comment. It says the information is free. That comment is partly right. The information is free. Structure, feedback, and other people's mistakes are not.

So start here, at no cost:

  • BiggerPockets forums and podcasts. The BiggerPockets Real Estate Podcast is free, hosted by Dave Meyer and Henry Washington, with more than 1,400 episodes and new ones twice a week. Real Estate Rookie is also free, hosted by Ashley Kehr and Tony J. Robinson, with around two episodes a week.
  • Ken McElroy's free channels. His podcast and YouTube channel are linked from kenmcelroy.com, along with a free multifamily deal checklist.
  • Free live sessions. Wealthy Investor runs a free Cashflow 2.0 masterclass. Infinity Investing runs free virtual workshops.
  • Pace Morby's free content. SubTo links its public YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram channels from subto.com.

Give this 30 to 60 days. If you still cannot say which model you want and which city you want it in, a paid program will not fix that. It will just cost you money while you keep deciding.

How to choose when your time is the scarce part

Money is not your tightest limit. Hours are. Use these four filters in order.

1. Pick the model your calendar can carry. Buy-and-hold rentals need a few hard weeks and then light upkeep. Flipping and creative finance need constant calls. Short-term rentals need daily attention at launch and then systems. If you have 5 hours a week, do not pick the model that needs 20.

2. Match the format to how you actually learn. Live weekly calls only help if you attend. If your job runs late, a self-paced library with an active chat beats a call you keep missing. Wealthy Investor's value is the call volume. KenPro's value is that you can watch at 11pm.

3. Decide what you are buying: information or review. A $399 membership gives you information. A high-ticket program should give you someone who reads your actual lease, your actual numbers, and tells you no. If a $9,800 program does not include that, you are paying course prices for a course.

4. Ask what happens when you stall. Everyone with a job stalls. Ask the seller what the program does in month three when you have not made an offer. If the answer is "watch module 4 again," keep looking.

Legacy Wealth Blueprint: the honest version

Legacy Wealth Blueprint is $9,800. That covers lifetime access to the curriculum and 12 months of community and live support. You get weekly live coaching calls with replays, a custom 12-month Wealth Strategy Snapshot, a review of your prior year tax return, a CPA implementation brief near tax season, and a 1-on-1 guide with bi-weekly check-ins. There is a smaller version at $1,500 for applicants earning under $75,000. That one is the video curriculum plus the AI advisor, with no calls.

There is no published money-back guarantee. If that matters to you, ask for the refund terms in writing before you pay.

Who Legacy Wealth Blueprint is not for:

  • People who want a deal-finding course. It is a wealth program covering taxes, debt and credit, income, investing, and protection. If you only want to learn how to find and underwrite rentals, a BiggerPockets bootcamp or KenPro is a better fit and far cheaper.
  • People earning under about $75,000. The math on tax strategy does not carry a $9,800 fee at that income. The $1,500 version exists for this reason.
  • People who would borrow to pay for it. If the fee needs a card you cannot clear, the program is not the problem you should be solving first.
  • People who want short-term rental operations. That is a different program. Airbnb Ascension is $9,800 for 6 months and one property, and it is about acquiring and running the property, not about your whole balance sheet.

Before you pay anyone

Ask these five questions and get the answers in writing:

  1. What is the total price, including any second payment or upsell?
  2. What is the refund policy?
  3. How long is my access, and what happens when it ends?
  4. Who exactly reviews my deals, and how fast do they reply?
  5. Can I speak to two people who joined in the last six months and are not in your ads?

A seller who answers all five plainly is worth a second call. A seller who gets vague on price or refunds has told you something useful.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a paid course to start investing in real estate?

No. The BiggerPockets forums and the free BiggerPockets and Real Estate Rookie podcasts cover the basics at no cost. Pay for a program when you want structure, deal review, or access to people who have done the model you picked.

How much do real estate investing programs cost in 2026?

Published prices vary a lot. KenPro lists $39.99 a month or $399 a year. Wealthy Investor lists $1,000 a month for its All Star tier. The Grant Cardone Real Estate Master Class lists $10,000. SubTo and Infinity Investing do not publish a price.

What can I do with 5 to 10 hours a week?

Enough to learn one model and underwrite deals. Pick one strategy, one market, and one weekly block of time. Trying three models at once is the most common reason busy people stall.

Is a cheap membership as good as coaching?

They do different jobs. A membership gives you courses and a community. Coaching gives you a person who reviews your numbers and answers you quickly. Buy coaching only when a specific decision is costing you money or time.

Why do some programs hide their price?

High-ticket programs often sell on an application and a call. SubTo and Infinity Investing both route you to a form rather than a checkout. Ask for the full price and the payment terms in writing before the call ends.

How much does Legacy Wealth Blueprint cost?

Legacy Wealth Blueprint is $9,800. It covers lifetime access to the curriculum plus 12 months of community and live support. There is a $1,500 course and AI version for applicants earning under $75,000, and it does not include calls.

Who should not buy a real estate coaching program?

Anyone who would need debt to pay for it, anyone who has not read a single lease or run a single deal analysis yet, and anyone who cannot protect 5 hours a week for the next six months.
