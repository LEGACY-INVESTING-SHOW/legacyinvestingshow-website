Common Success Patterns

What successful students have in common:

1. They start imperfectly Every successful student had doubts. They started anyway.

2. They follow the scripts Students who "improve" the scripts fail. Students who use them exactly succeed.

3. They message 20+ landlords The magic number seems to be 20. Message fewer, no unit. Message 20+, get a yes.

4. They use the community Students who ask questions, get answers. Students who lurk, struggle.

5. They stay consistent Not every month is great. They keep going through the dips.

Common Failure Patterns

What struggling students have in common:

1. They overthink Analysis paralysis. Never actually message a landlord.

2. They give up too early Message 5 landlords, get 5 nos, quit. Need to message 20.

3. They skip the training Think they can figure it out. Waste time and money.

4. They don't use the community Try to do everything alone. Fail unnecessarily.

5. They expect overnight success Get discouraged when not profitable in week 1. This is a business, not a lottery ticket.
