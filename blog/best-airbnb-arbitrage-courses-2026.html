Is Airbnb arbitrage still profitable in 2026?

Short answer: the demand side is holding up and new supply has slowed, which helps. Your own margin still depends on three things nobody in a course controls, and we cover those next.

Here is what the published data says.

Demand is still growing. Airbnb reported Nights and Seats Booked up 10% year over year in the second quarter of 2026, with gross booking value of $27.2 billion, up 16% (Airbnb, 6 August 2026).

New supply has slowed a lot. AirDNA's midyear outlook, published 8 July 2026, forecast available US listings growing just 2.7% in 2026. That is a cut from its December 2025 forecast of 4.6%, which AirDNA itself described as well below the roughly 20% peak expansion of 2021 and 2022 (AirDNA midyear outlook, AirDNA 2026 outlook).

Occupancy and revenue per listing are up slightly. AirDNA forecast average US occupancy of 57.4% in 2026, a little above the pre-pandemic average of 57.0%, with RevPAR up 2.9% driven by nightly rates. Rate growth picked up from 0.7% year over year in January to around 3% by spring.

So the market is not collapsing. It is normalising. Slower supply growth with steady demand is a better setup for an operator than the 2021 land grab was.

One caveat on a number you will see quoted. AirDNA's Best Places to Invest 2026 list, published 27 January 2026, showed its top 10 markets with an average home price of $296,000, annual revenue potential of $40,500, and yields near 14% (AirDNA). Those are ownership yields. They are not arbitrage margins. Arbitrage earns the spread between rent and nightly revenue, and it earns no equity at all.

The three risks that decide your outcome

1. Landlord consent. This is the whole business. If your lease does not allow subletting for short stays in writing, you do not have a company, you have a clause waiting to be enforced. Get it written into the lease, not agreed on a call. Any program that skips this is selling you a fantasy.

2. Regulation. Rules are changing constantly, and mostly in one direction. The independent tracker STR Rule Watch logged 100 verified US regulatory changes across cities, counties, and states between 13 March and 15 August 2026 (shortrentalrules.com). A February 2026 guide from Minut records the shape of it: Atlanta requires hosts to keep the property as a primary residence and caps them at two properties, Los Angeles caps unhosted nights at 120 a year, and San Francisco caps them at 90 (Minut). Primary-residence rules make arbitrage impossible, not just harder. Check before you lease.

3. Saturation and rent. Supply growth has slowed nationally, but national numbers do not pay your rent. Some neighbourhoods are full. Run your model at 50% occupancy, not at the number in the sales deck. If it only works at 80%, it is not a business. Our guide on evaluating an Airbnb market sets out how to check a city properly, and the mistakes that quietly kill arbitrage profit covers the rest.

How to choose a program

  1. Confirm your city allows it. Free, and it removes most bad purchases.
  2. Buy a cheap course first. Spend $180 to $800, then call 20 landlords. Most people quit at the calls. Better to learn that for a few hundred dollars.
  3. Only then pay for coaching, and only if the fee buys people. Ask exactly who reviews your lease and your numbers, and how fast they reply.
  4. Get the refund policy in writing. Rakidzich publishes one. Most do not, including us.
  5. Keep your capital separate from the fee. If the program price eats the money you need for deposits and furniture, you have bought a course instead of a business.

Frequently asked questions

Is Airbnb arbitrage still profitable in 2026?

The demand side looks steady. AirDNA forecast US occupancy averaging 57.4% in 2026 and RevPAR up 2.9%, with new listings growing only 2.7%. Airbnb reported Nights and Seats Booked up 10% year over year in Q2 2026. Your margin still depends on your rent, your city's rules, and your landlord.

What is the cheapest credible arbitrage course?

Sean Rakidzich publishes self-paced courses from $180 to $800 with a 30-day money-back guarantee. That is the lowest-risk way to find out whether you like the daily work.

Why do most arbitrage programs hide their price?

They sell on a call. BNB Formula, 10XBNB, and High Performance Hosts all route you to a form or a consultation rather than a checkout. You can ask for the number by email before you agree to a call.

What is the biggest risk in arbitrage?

Landlord consent. If subletting is not allowed in writing, your whole business sits on a lease clause that can end it. Regulation is second, and rent that is too high for the nightly rate is third.

Do I need a licence or permit?

Often yes. Many cities require registration, a licence number on the listing, and lodging tax. Some require the host to live in the property, which rules out arbitrage entirely.

How much cash do I need to start one unit?

Plan for the deposit, furniture, and a few months of rent in reserve. Our guidance for Airbnb Ascension clients is $10,000 to $25,000 of working capital, separate from any program fee.

Is a coached Airbnb program worth it?

Only if the fee buys people rather than videos. A coached program should give you deal review before you sign a lease, same-day access to a coach, and setup help. If it is only a video library, it is overpriced.
