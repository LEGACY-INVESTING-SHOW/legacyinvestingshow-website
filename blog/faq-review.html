Legacy Investing Show FAQ: 27 Questions Answered Honestly

I get the same questions every day.

Let me answer them all in one place.

No BS. Just honest answers.

About the Program

1. What exactly is Legacy Investing Show?

A step-by-step system for building an Airbnb business without buying property.

We call it rental arbitrage. You lease apartments, furnish them, list on Airbnb, profit from the spread.

2. How is this different from other real estate courses?

Three things:

Specificity: Most courses teach concepts. We teach exact scripts, spreadsheets, and systems.

Current: I still operate 47 units. Not teaching outdated theory from 2015.

Filter: We turn away people who won't execute. Quality over quantity.

3. What's included in the program?

  • 120+ training videos
  • Deal analysis spreadsheets
  • Landlord outreach scripts
  • Furniture checklists
  • Private community (1,400+ members)
  • Weekly live coaching calls
  • Lease templates
  • Guest communication templates
  • Revenue management training
  • Scaling systems

4. How long do I have access?

Lifetime. Pay once, access forever. Including all updates.

5. Is there a payment plan?

Yes. $2,497 one-time or 6 payments of $497.

Investment & ROI

6. How much does the program cost?

$2,497 or 6 x $497.

Compare to one semester of community college. Or a used Honda Civic.

7. How much money do I need to start?

$8,000 to $15,000 for your first unit.

Breakdown:

  • First month rent: $1,500-$2,500
  • Security deposit: $1,500-$2,500
  • Furniture: $4,000-$6,000
  • Supplies/setup: $1,000-$2,000
  • Buffer: $1,000-$2,000

8. What's the typical ROI?

Most students see 100% return on the program cost within 60-90 days.

That's not hype. That's math.

If you make $3,000 profit per month (typical for one unit), you pay back the $2,497 program fee in 25 days.

9. Can I really make money in my market?

Depends on your market.

Good markets: Austin, Nashville, Phoenix, Tampa, Atlanta, Dallas

Harder markets: San Francisco, NYC, areas with strict STR regulations

We teach you how to analyze your specific market before you start.

10. How long until I'm profitable?

Most students are profitable by month 2-3.

Month 1: Setup and first bookings Month 2: Finding rhythm, building reviews Month 3+: Consistent profit

Getting Started

11. Do I need real estate experience?

No.

Most students start with zero experience.

Marcus was in IT. Jennifer was a nurse. Sarah was a teacher.

The system is built for beginners.

12. Can I do this with a full-time job?

Yes.

Most students start while working. 10-15 hours per week is plenty at first.

You can transition full-time once you have 2-3 units.

13. Do I need good credit?

Not necessarily.

You need to qualify for an apartment lease. Requirements vary by landlord.

If your credit is poor, you might need a co-signer or larger deposit.

14. Do I need an LLC?

Not to start.

Many students begin as sole proprietors. Scale to LLC once you have 2+ units.

We cover entity structure in the training.

15. What if I don't have $8K saved?

A few options:

  1. Save up first (recommended)
  2. Partner with someone who has capital
  3. Start with a smaller market where costs are lower

Don't go into debt to start. That's dumb.

The Business Model

16. Is rental arbitrage legal?

Yes, in most places.

Some cities have restrictions:

  • San Francisco: 90-day annual cap
  • New York: Strict regulations
  • Some HOAs prohibit short-term rentals

We teach you how to research local laws before you start.

17. What if the landlord says no?

Most will.

That's normal.

You might message 20 landlords to get 1 yes. That's a 5% success rate.

Our scripts improve your odds, but rejection is part of the game.

18. How do I find landlords who allow Airbnb?

You don't find them. You convince them.

Most landlords have never considered Airbnb. You educate them on the benefits:

  • Guaranteed rent (you pay even if unit is empty)
  • Professional management (you treat it like a business)
  • Higher quality tenants (you have incentive to maintain the unit)

19. What about damages and problems?

Part of the business.

Airbnb has host protection insurance. You collect security deposits. You screen guests. You have cleaning protocols.

Most issues are minor. Broken glass. Stained carpet. Normal wear and tear.

20. How much time does this take?

Per unit, per week:

Setup phase: 10-15 hours Operational phase: 2-3 hours

With systems and automation, you can manage multiple units in 10 hours/week total.

Support & Community

21. What if I get stuck?

Three support channels:

  1. Private community: Post questions, get answers from me and 1,400+ students
  2. Weekly coaching calls: Live Q&A every week
  3. Email support: Direct line to our team

You're not alone.

22. Do I get 1-on-1 coaching with Preston?

Not included in the base program.

Group coaching calls every week where I answer questions live.

1-on-1 coaching is available as an upgrade for serious students.

23. What if I have questions about my specific market?

Post in the community.

Chances are, someone in your city is already in the program. They can share local intel.

Results & Expectations

24. What's a realistic first-year income?

One unit, realistic numbers:

Revenue: $3,000-$5,000/month Expenses: $2,000-$3,000/month Profit: $1,000-$2,000/month

Scale to 3 units: $3,000-$6,000/month profit

Scale to 5 units: $5,000-$10,000/month profit

Your results will vary based on market, execution, and luck.

25. What percentage of students succeed?

Here's the honest answer:

Of students who actually complete the training and message 20+ landlords, about 80% get their first unit.

Of those, about 70% scale to multiple units.

The students who don't succeed usually:

  • Never start (watch videos but don't take action)
  • Give up after first few rejections
  • Pick terrible markets
  • Run out of capital

Success correlates directly with execution.

26. Is there a guarantee?

30-day action-based guarantee.

Requirements:

  • Watch the core training modules
  • Message at least 20 landlords using our scripts
  • Attend at least one coaching call

If you do those things and don't think it's for you, full refund.

No questions asked.

But you have to actually try. No refunds for people who don't take action.

27. How do I know this isn't a scam?

Fair question.

Here's proof we're legit:

Proof of concept: I operate 47 units. $20M+ portfolio. Not theory.

Student results: 1,400+ members. Hundreds of success stories. Real names, real numbers.

Public presence: Active YouTube channel with 200K+ subscribers. Daily content.

Transparency: We show the hard parts. The rejections. The problems. Scams only show wins.

Guarantee: 30-day refund policy. Scams don't offer refunds.

Accessibility: I'm in the community every day. You can message me directly. Scams hide behind VA teams.

Use your judgment. Do your research. Talk to students in our community.
