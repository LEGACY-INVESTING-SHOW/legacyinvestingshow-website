Renters insurance cost in Mississippi

Mississippi averages about $190 a year, $20 above the US average.

Mississippi renters paid about $190 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $20 more than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline. Your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

What is different about Mississippi

Mississippi's $190 average is one of the highest in the country, $20 above the US figure, because storm claims and a tougher litigation climate outweigh low replacement costs. Gulfport is not Oxford. The statewide mean is a warning, not a coupon.

Most shopping conversations in Mississippi start in Jackson, Gulfport and Oxford.

Leases and landlord rules in Mississippi

Gulf Coast leases often require coverage because storm years are not theoretical. Jackson managed properties usually do too. Oxford student housing puts it in the packet.

What to actually schedule

Hurricane and tornado contents claims are the weather pair. Flood on the coast is the coverage people mix up with wind. Schedule electronics.

A Gulfport example

Example: a Gulfport renter with $15,000 contents can still quote near or above $190. Cheap furniture does not repeal a difficult storm market.

What drives Mississippi renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: hurricanes, tornadoes, and severe storms are all part of the risk profile.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: higher claim frequency and a more active litigation climate push costs up.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are low, which offsets some of the pressure, and some carriers limit Gulf exposure, which reduces competition and helps explain one of the highest averages in the country.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Mississippi Insurance Department (www.mid.ms.gov/).

Questions people ask about Mississippi renters insurance

What the state average does and does not tell you.

Why is Mississippi renters insurance so expensive?

The $190 NAIC 2021 average reflects storms and claims more than high-end sofas.

Do Gulfport landlords require coverage?

Many do.

Does a Mississippi renters policy cover hurricanes?

Wind to your stuff may be included. Flood is usually not.

Should Oxford students expect Gulf Coast prices?

Not automatically. Quote the ZIP. The statewide $190 is a blend.
