Renters insurance cost in Iowa

Iowa renters paid about $115 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $55 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline; your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

$115 Iowa average a year, $55 below the US average

What is different about Iowa

Iowa's $115 average is low because replacement costs are modest and the market is competitive, not because hail forgot the state. Des Moines and Iowa City still see weather claims that make a $1,000 deductible feel real.

Most shopping conversations in Iowa start in Des Moines, Cedar Rapids and Iowa City.

Leases and landlord rules in Iowa

Des Moines communities usually require coverage. Iowa City student rentals often put it in the lease packet.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics and instruments. Hail and wind are the everyday weather pair. Flood along rivers is separate.

A Des Moines example

Example: a Des Moines renter with $16,000 contents can sit near $115. After a hail week, the deductible is the number that matters.

What drives Iowa renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: tornadoes, hail, and severe storms are the main claim triggers.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is moderate, mostly weather-driven.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are modest, and a competitive regional carrier market keeps rates below average.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Iowa Insurance Division (iid.iowa.gov/).
