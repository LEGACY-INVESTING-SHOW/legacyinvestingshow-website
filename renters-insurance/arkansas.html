Renters insurance cost in Arkansas

Arkansas renters paid about $151 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $19 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline; your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

$151 Arkansas average a year, $19 below the US average

What is different about Arkansas

Arkansas prices around $151, close to the national average, because tornado and hail claims are common even when replacement costs in Little Rock are not coastal-expensive. Northwest Arkansas growth around Fayetteville is pulling some ZIP codes above the old statewide feel.

Most shopping conversations in Arkansas start in Little Rock, Fayetteville and Fort Smith.

Leases and landlord rules in Arkansas

Little Rock complexes usually want $100,000 liability. Fayetteville student housing near campus is stricter about proof-before-keys than a private landlord in Fort Smith.

What to actually schedule

Schedule hunting gear, instruments, and electronics. Tornado claims are about your stuff inside a damaged building, not about rebuilding the landlord's walls.

A Little Rock example

Example: a Little Rock renter with $18,000 contents and a $500 deductible can sit near $151. A Fayetteville house share with four laptops and bikes should inventory first, then quote.

What drives Arkansas renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: tornado and severe-storm exposure runs across much of the state.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claims are mostly weather-driven, with litigation less of a factor.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are modest, and plenty of national insurers compete, keeping quotes near the national average.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Arkansas Insurance Department (insurance.arkansas.gov/).

Questions people ask about Arkansas renters insurance

What the state average does and does not tell you.

What is the Arkansas renters insurance average?
About $151 a year in the NAIC 2021 baseline, $19 under the US average.
Do Arkansas landlords require coverage?
Many managed properties do. Private landlords vary. The lease wins.
Does renters insurance pay after a tornado in Arkansas?
It can pay for your contents and additional living expenses if the form includes those perils. The house is the owner's policy.
Why would a Fayetteville quote beat Little Rock?
More student density, more electronics, sometimes more theft pressure. Limits explain more than city pride.
