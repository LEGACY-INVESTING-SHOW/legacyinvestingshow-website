Renters insurance cost in Arizona

Arizona averages about $149 a year, $21 below the US average.

Arizona renters paid about $149 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $21 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline. Your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

What is different about Arizona

Arizona's $149 average hides a Phoenix-versus-everywhere split. Monsoon storms, theft in some urban ZIP codes, and higher replacement costs in the Valley pull quotes up even though the statewide number sits $21 under the US average.

Most shopping conversations in Arizona start in Phoenix, Tucson and Mesa.

Leases and landlord rules in Arizona

Phoenix and Scottsdale apartment communities almost always want liability proof at move-in. Tucson student rentals near the university are picky about roommate policies: one named insured does not always cover the other three.

What to actually schedule

Heat does not usually steal a sofa. Theft, water from a swamp cooler, and monsoon wind do. Schedule bikes and cameras. Flood from a wash overflowing is still a flood conversation.

A Phoenix example

Example: a Phoenix renter with $20,000 contents, $100,000 liability, and a $500 deductible might land near $149. A downtown high-rise with valet theft exposure and a $50,000 contents limit will not.

What drives Arizona renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: summer monsoon storms and heat-related wear shape loss patterns.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is moderate and litigation is not a dominant driver.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs in Phoenix and Tucson run above the state norm, and a healthy number of national carriers compete for renters business.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Arizona Department of Insurance and Financial Institutions (difi.az.gov/).

Questions people ask about Arizona renters insurance

What the state average does and does not tell you.

How much is renters insurance in Arizona?

About $149 a year in the NAIC 2021 baseline, below the $170 US average. Phoenix quotes still vary hard by ZIP.

Do Phoenix apartments require renters insurance?

Most professionally managed communities do, and they want to be listed on the policy.

Does Arizona renters insurance cover monsoon damage?

Wind and some water from wind-driven rain can be in the form. Flood from a wash is usually not.

Should Tucson students share one policy?

Only if the carrier and lease allow it. Roommate assumptions are a common claim fight.
