Renters insurance cost in Maine

Maine averages about $109 a year, $61 below the US average.

Maine renters paid about $109 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $61 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline. Your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

What is different about Maine

Maine's $109 average is among the lowest. Winter storms and older housing still create water and freeze claims in Portland. The cheap average reflects a smaller, lower-limit market, not an absence of ice dams.

Most shopping conversations in Maine start in Portland, Lewiston and Bangor.

Leases and landlord rules in Maine

Portland buildings often require coverage. Bangor landlords vary. Student housing near Orono may put it in the lease.

What to actually schedule

Schedule outdoor gear. Freeze and water backup are the unglamorous claims. Coastal flood is separate.

A Portland example

Example: a Portland renter with $18,000 contents can sit near $109. A waterfront unit should not treat that as flood coverage.

What drives Maine renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: winter storms and cold are the main claim drivers.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is low.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are moderate, and a small but stable carrier market keeps the average among the lowest in the country.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Maine Bureau of Insurance (www.maine.gov/pfr/insurance/).

Questions people ask about Maine renters insurance

What the state average does and does not tell you.

How much is renters insurance in Maine?

About $109 a year in the NAIC 2021 baseline, $61 under the US average.

Do Portland landlords require it?

Many managed properties do.

Does a Maine policy cover frozen-pipe damage to my stuff?

Often yes for sudden water, subject to the form. Neglect is the usual fight.

Should I buy flood coverage on the coast?

Ask. The $109 average does not include flood.
