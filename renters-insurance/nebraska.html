Renters insurance cost in Nebraska

Nebraska renters paid about $150 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $20 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline; your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

$150 Nebraska average a year, $20 below the US average

What is different about Nebraska

Nebraska's $150 average sits $20 under the US figure. Hail and winter do the weather work. Omaha replacement costs run above western counties. Lincoln student density adds a roommate layer.

Most shopping conversations in Nebraska start in Omaha, Lincoln and Bellevue.

Leases and landlord rules in Nebraska

Omaha and Lincoln complexes require proof. UNL-area leases often include a coverage clause.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics. Hail deductibles on renters still need to be payable. Tornado contents claims are not rare.

A Omaha example

Example: an Omaha renter with $18,000 contents may see a quote near $150. A Lincoln house with five laptops should inventory.

What drives Nebraska renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: severe storms, hail, and winter weather are common.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is moderate, mostly weather-driven.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are modest, and a competitive carrier market keeps rates near the national average.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Nebraska Department of Insurance (doi.nebraska.gov/).
