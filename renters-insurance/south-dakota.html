Renters insurance cost in South Dakota

South Dakota averages about $130 a year, $40 below the US average.

South Dakota renters paid about $130 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $40 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline. Your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

What is different about South Dakota

South Dakota's $130 average sits under the US figure. Hail and winter do the work. Rapid City is not Sioux Falls. Western wildfire-adjacent living expenses are a local question.

Most shopping conversations in South Dakota start in Sioux Falls, Rapid City and Aberdeen.

Leases and landlord rules in South Dakota

Sioux Falls complexes usually require proof. Rapid City managed properties often do too.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics and outdoor gear. Hail deductibles need to be payable. Flood is still flood.

A Sioux Falls example

Example: a Sioux Falls renter with $16,000 contents may sit near $130. After hail, the deductible is the story.

What drives South Dakota renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: severe storms, hail, and winter weather are common.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is moderate, mostly weather-driven.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are modest, and a thin carrier market keeps the average near the national figure.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the South Dakota Division of Insurance (dlr.sd.gov/insurance/).
