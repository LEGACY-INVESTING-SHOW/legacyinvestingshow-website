Renters insurance cost in Maryland

Maryland averages about $131 a year, $39 below the US average.

Maryland renters paid about $131 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $39 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline. Your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

What is different about Maryland

Maryland's $131 average sits under the US figure. Silver Spring and the DC suburbs have higher replacement costs than the Eastern Shore. Baltimore theft and water in older rowhomes are a different file than an Annapolis marina-adjacent apartment.

Most shopping conversations in Maryland start in Baltimore, Silver Spring and Annapolis.

Leases and landlord rules in Maryland

DC-suburb buildings require certificates. Baltimore professionally managed properties usually do too.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics and bikes. Basement units need a water-backup conversation. Coastal flood near the Bay is separate.

A Baltimore example

Example: a Baltimore renter with $20,000 contents may see a quote near $131. A Bethesda high-rise with nicer furniture will not.

What drives Maryland renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: coastal storms, nor'easters, and winter weather shape claims.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is low to moderate.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs near Washington, DC and Baltimore are above the state norm, and a competitive mid-Atlantic carrier market keeps prices below the national average.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Maryland Insurance Administration (insurance.maryland.gov/).
