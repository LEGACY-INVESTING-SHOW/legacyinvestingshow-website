Renters insurance cost in Ohio

Ohio renters paid about $125 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $45 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline; your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

Ohio average
$125 a year
US average
$170 a year
Source
NAIC 2021 baseline

What is different about Ohio

Ohio's $125 average is well under the US figure thanks to a competitive market and moderate replacement costs. Columbus student and young-professional density still creates theft and water claims that a statewide mean cannot see.

Most shopping conversations in Ohio start in Columbus, Cleveland and Cincinnati.

Leases and landlord rules in Ohio

Columbus, Cleveland, and Cincinnati complexes require proof. Campus landlords around OSU are used to certificates.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics. Winter freeze and upstairs water leaks are ordinary. Lake flood is a Cleveland-edge conversation.

A Columbus example

Example: a Columbus renter in Short North with $20,000 contents may sit near $125. Four roommates with four bikes should schedule the bikes.

What drives Ohio renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: severe storms and winter weather are the main triggers.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is moderate.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are moderate, and a highly competitive carrier market keeps the average well below the national figure.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Ohio Department of Insurance (insurance.ohio.gov/).
