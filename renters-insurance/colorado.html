Renters insurance cost in Colorado

Colorado renters paid about $147 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $23 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline; your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average

Source
NAIC 2021 baseline

What is different about Colorado

Colorado's $147 average is pulled around by Front Range hail. Denver replacement costs sit above rural counties. Wildfire at the urban fringe is a contents and living-expense story, not a reason the whole state prices like Texas.

Most shopping conversations in Colorado start in Denver, Colorado Springs and Boulder.

Leases and landlord rules in Colorado

Denver and Boulder communities usually require liability proof at move-in. Ski-town rentals can be casual on paper and brutal after a hail claim if you were uninsured.

What to actually schedule

Hail can wreck cars and outdoor gear. Schedule bikes. Flood from a burn scar is still flood. Earthquake is uncommon compared with hail.

A Denver example

Example: a Denver renter with $22,000 contents and a $500 deductible might sit near $147. After a neighborhood hailstorm, the deductible you chose matters more than the statewide average.

What drives Colorado renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: hail, wildfires, and winter storms all contribute to loss patterns.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency runs moderate, with hailstorms the most common trigger.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement values along the Front Range are above the national norm, and many carriers compete, especially in the Denver market.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Colorado Division of Insurance (doi.colorado.gov/).

Questions people ask about Colorado renters insurance

How much is renters insurance in Colorado?
About $147 a year in the NAIC 2021 baseline, $23 under the US average.
Do Denver apartments require it?
Most managed properties do.
Does Colorado renters insurance cover hail damage to my belongings?
Often yes for personal property, subject to the deductible. The roof is the landlord's policy.
Should Boulder students buy a higher contents limit?
If the apartment is a laptop warehouse, yes. Inventory beats guessing from $147.
