Renters insurance cost in South Carolina

South Carolina averages about $124 a year, $46 below the US average.

South Carolina renters paid about $124 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $46 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline. Your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

What is different about South Carolina

South Carolina's $124 average sits well under the US figure. Charleston wind and humidity claims are not a Greenville story. Columbia student housing adds a roommate layer. The cheap mean is a blend.

Most shopping conversations in South Carolina start in Charleston, Columbia and Greenville.

Leases and landlord rules in South Carolina

Charleston historic and peninsula rentals often require coverage. Greenville complexes usually do. Columbia campus leases often include it.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics. Coastal flood and wind are two questions in Charleston. Hail inland is less of a Texas-style story and still real.

A Charleston example

Example: a Charleston renter in a peninsula apartment should not treat $124 as a hurricane plan. A Greenville renter with modest furniture might sit near the average.

What drives South Carolina renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: hurricane and severe-storm exposure is significant, especially near the coast.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is moderate.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are moderate, and a competitive southeast carrier market keeps the average below the national figure.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the South Carolina Department of Insurance (doi.sc.gov/).

Questions people ask about South Carolina renters insurance

What the state average does and does not tell you.

How much is renters insurance in South Carolina?

About $124 a year in the NAIC 2021 baseline.

Do Charleston landlords require it?

Many do, especially professionally managed properties.

Does South Carolina renters insurance cover hurricanes?

Wind may be included. Flood from surge is usually not.

Should Columbia students share a policy?

Only if listed.
