Renters insurance cost in District of Columbia

District of Columbia renters paid about $147 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $23 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline; your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

$147 District of Columbia average a year, $23 below the US average

What is different about District of Columbia

DC's $147 average is below the US figure despite high replacement costs because competition is thick and many policies are written on modest contents limits. A Capitol Hill row-house basement is not the same risk as a new Navy Yard tower.

Most shopping conversations in District of Columbia start in Washington.

Leases and landlord rules in District of Columbia

Almost every professionally managed DC building requires renters insurance and a certificate before keys. Roommate riders matter in group houses.

What to actually schedule

Schedule bikes stolen off yards and electronics. Flood in a basement unit is a flood conversation. Civil unrest and theft are contents issues that still need a police report habit.

A Washington example

Example: a Navy Yard renter with $30,000 contents and $300,000 liability can price above $147. The average is not a Dupont Circle quote.

What drives District of Columbia renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: winter storms and occasional coastal weather events shape claim patterns.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is low and disputes are uncommon.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are high because the metro is one of the most expensive in the country, and a competitive carrier market keeps prices below what the high cost of living might suggest.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the District of Columbia Department of Insurance, Securities and Banking (disb.dc.gov/).

Questions people ask about District of Columbia renters insurance

What the state average does and does not tell you.

How much is renters insurance in DC?
About $147 a year in the NAIC 2021 baseline, $23 under the US average.
Do DC landlords require renters insurance?
Most managed buildings do, and they are not flexible at move-in.
Does a DC policy cover a flooded basement?
Flood is usually excluded. Backup of sewers may need an endorsement.
Should I raise liability in a group house?
If people come and go, yes. Liability is the part landlords actually care about.
