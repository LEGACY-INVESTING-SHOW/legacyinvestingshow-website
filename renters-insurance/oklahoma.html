Renters insurance cost in Oklahoma

Oklahoma averages about $163 a year, $7 below the US average.

Oklahoma renters paid about $163 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $7 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline. Your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

What is different about Oklahoma

Oklahoma's $163 average sits close to the US figure because tornado and hail claims are not a joke. Oklahoma City and Tulsa replacement costs are moderate. The premium is a weather bill.

Most shopping conversations in Oklahoma start in Oklahoma City, Tulsa and Norman.

Leases and landlord rules in Oklahoma

OKC and Tulsa complexes require proof. Norman student housing usually does too.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics. Tornado contents claims are why a deductible you cannot pay is foolish. Safe rooms do not replace a policy.

A Oklahoma City example

Example: an OKC renter with $18,000 contents can still sit near $163 because the pool pays a lot of storm claims. Cheap furniture does not repeal hail.

What drives Oklahoma renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: the state is among the most severe-weather exposed in the country, with tornadoes and hail common.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: weather claims and a more active litigation climate push costs up.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are moderate, and competition softens some of the weather-driven pressure, but the average still runs high.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Oklahoma Insurance Department (www.oid.ok.gov/).

Questions people ask about Oklahoma renters insurance

What the state average does and does not tell you.

Why is Oklahoma renters insurance higher than Kansas?

The $163 NAIC 2021 average reflects a hard storm and litigation mix.

Do Tulsa landlords require coverage?

Managed properties usually do.

Does renters insurance cover tornado damage to my belongings?

Often yes for personal property. The house is the owner's policy.

Should Norman students buy higher contents limits?

If the house is a laptop stack, yes.
