Renters insurance cost in Indiana

Indiana renters paid about $127 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $43 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline; your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

$127 a year
US average
$170 a year
Source
NAIC 2021 baseline

What is different about Indiana

Indiana's $127 average sits well under the US number. Tornado and hail still write the weather story. Indianapolis replacement costs are moderate. Bloomington student density creates a different theft and roommate pattern.

Most shopping conversations in Indiana start in Indianapolis, Fort Wayne and Bloomington.

Leases and landlord rules in Indiana

Indianapolis complexes want proof. IU-area leases in Bloomington almost always do. Private landlords in smaller counties vary.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics. Tornado contents claims are common enough that a deductible you cannot pay is a bad personality trait.

A Indianapolis example

Example: an Indianapolis renter with $18,000 contents may see a quote near $127. A Bloomington house with four gaming PCs should inventory first.

What drives Indiana renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: tornado and severe-storm exposure is present across the state.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is moderate and litigation is not a major driver.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are moderate, and steady carrier competition keeps prices near the national average.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Indiana Department of Insurance (www.in.gov/idoi/).
