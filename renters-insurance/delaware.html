Renters insurance cost in Delaware

Delaware averages about $124 a year, $46 below the US average.

Delaware renters paid about $124 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $46 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline. Your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

What is different about Delaware

Delaware's $124 average is low for the Mid-Atlantic because the market is small and replacement costs outside Wilmington are not Manhattan. Coastal storm exposure still exists. Do not read $124 as 'nothing happens here.'

Most shopping conversations in Delaware start in Wilmington, Dover and Newark.

Leases and landlord rules in Delaware

Wilmington apartment communities usually want proof of insurance. Newark student rentals near UD often require it in the lease packet.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics. Flood near inland bays is a separate product. Wind from a nor'easter can still be in the renters form for your stuff.

A Wilmington example

Example: a Wilmington renter with $20,000 contents and $100,000 liability can sit near $124. A waterfront unit should not use that average as a flood substitute.

What drives Delaware renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: coastal storms and nor'easters are the main weather exposure.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is low.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs sit close to the mid-Atlantic norm, and the small market size means a handful of carriers dominate pricing.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Delaware Department of Insurance (insurance.delaware.gov/).

Questions people ask about Delaware renters insurance

What the state average does and does not tell you.

How much is renters insurance in Delaware?

About $124 a year in the NAIC 2021 baseline, $46 under the US average.

Do Delaware landlords require it?

Managed buildings in Wilmington usually do.

Does the $124 average include flood?

No. Flood is separate.

Should UD students in Newark share a policy?

Only if the carrier allows listed roommates. Ask before move-in.
