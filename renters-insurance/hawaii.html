Renters insurance cost in Hawaii

Hawaii averages about $151 a year, $19 below the US average.

Hawaii renters paid about $151 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $19 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline. Your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

What is different about Hawaii

Hawaii's $151 average is below the US figure even though almost everything is shipped in. The statewide number is not a promise that replacing a mattress in Honolulu is cheap. Hurricane and lava sound dramatic. Theft from a lanai and water leaks in older walk-ups do more everyday work.

Most shopping conversations in Hawaii start in Honolulu, Hilo and Kahului.

Leases and landlord rules in Hawaii

Honolulu high-rises require certificates. Neighbor-island landlords vary. Military housing has its own proof rules.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics, dive gear, and instruments. Hurricane deductibles and flood near the coast are separate reads. Volcanic damage is not a free extra.

A Honolulu example

Example: a Honolulu renter with $30,000 contents can price above $151 because shipping-driven replacement costs do not care about the statewide mean.

What drives Hawaii renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: hurricane and volcanic hazards are the state's headline exposures.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is low given the limited number of renters policies.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are among the highest in the country because most goods are shipped in, and a thin carrier market with limited competition keeps the average below the national baseline.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Hawaii Insurance Division (cca.hawaii.gov/ins/).

Questions people ask about Hawaii renters insurance

What the state average does and does not tell you.

Why is Hawaii below the US renters average?

The $151 NAIC 2021 average is a blend. Thin limits and a unique market structure sit beside very expensive replacements.

Do Honolulu buildings require renters insurance?

Most do, especially high-rises.

Is lava or hurricane in a standard Hawaii renters form?

Do not assume either. Read endorsements.

Should I raise contents limits because of shipping costs?

Yes if you would have to replace locally at island prices.
