Renters insurance cost in Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania renters paid about $124 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $46 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline; your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

Pennsylvania average
$124 a year
US average
$170 a year
Source
NAIC 2021 baseline

What is different about Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania's $124 average is well under the US figure. Philadelphia replacement costs and row-home water claims differ from Pittsburgh. The statewide mean includes a lot of smaller cities.

Most shopping conversations in Pennsylvania start in Philadelphia, Pittsburgh and Allentown.

Leases and landlord rules in Pennsylvania

Philly professionally managed buildings require certificates. Pittsburgh complexes usually do too. Student housing near Temple or Pitt expects proof.

What to actually schedule

Schedule electronics. Basement units need water-backup talk. Winter freeze is ordinary.

A Philadelphia example

Example: a Philadelphia renter in a Fishtown walk-up with $25,000 contents can price above $124. A Pittsburgh one-bedroom might sit nearer the average.

What drives Pennsylvania renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: winter storms and severe weather are the main exposures.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is moderate, with litigation a factor in the Philadelphia metro.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are moderate overall, and higher in the metros, and many carriers compete, keeping the average below the national figure.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Pennsylvania Insurance Department (www.insurance.pa.gov/).
