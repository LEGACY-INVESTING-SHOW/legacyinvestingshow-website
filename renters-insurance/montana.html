Renters insurance cost in Montana

Montana renters paid about $130 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $40 less than the $170 US average. The state average is a baseline; your quote depends on your city, coverage limits, deductible, and claims history.

US average: $170 a year, on the same NAIC 2021 baseline.

$130 Montana average a year, $40 below the US average

What is different about Montana

Montana's $130 average sits under the US number. Winter, wildfire at the wildland edge, and a thin carrier market all show up. Bozeman replacement costs have climbed with in-migration even if the statewide mean still looks calm.

Most shopping conversations in Montana start in Billings, Missoula and Bozeman.

Leases and landlord rules in Montana

Missoula and Bozeman rentals near campus often require coverage. Billings complexes usually do. Rural landlords vary.

What to actually schedule

Schedule outdoor gear and tools. Wildfire living expenses matter if you are against the trees. Earthquake is less of a headline than winter and fire.

A Bozeman example

Example: a Bozeman renter with $25,000 of ski and electronics gear can price above $130. The average includes thinner rural limits.

What drives Montana renters insurance costs

Weather exposure is a major driver here: winter storms and wildfire season are the main concerns.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency is low.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement costs are moderate, and a thin carrier market keeps the average below the national figure.

Questions about carriers or complaints go to the Montana Commissioner of Securities and Insurance (csimt.gov/).

Questions people ask about Montana renters insurance
