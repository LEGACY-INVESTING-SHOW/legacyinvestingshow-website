Questions people ask before the call.

I earn a W-2 salary. Is this for me?

Yes. The program is built for people earning $100K or more, whether that comes from a salary, self-employment, or a business. Which strategies apply depends on your income, accounts, debt, and goals.

What happens with my CPA?

You keep your CPA. The program gives you education and a written plan, plus a CPA Implementation Brief you hand over before filing season. Your tax professional decides what applies and handles your filings.

Do I have to start a business or buy property?

No. You can focus on your current income, debt, and accounts. The curriculum also covers other income and investment options, so you can look at them when they fit your plan.

How long do I have access?

The video curriculum is yours for life. The Wealth Circle community, weekly coaching, and your guide run for 12 months.

Is there a guarantee?

Ask on the call about the No Client Left Behind Guarantee and get its current written terms. Confirm what actions it requires, how a return on your program investment is measured, and when continued support applies before you enroll.

How do I get ready for the call?

Watch the walkthrough at the top of this page. Then write down your main financial goal, the decisions you are weighing right now, and any questions. Your confirmation email has the details for joining the call.
