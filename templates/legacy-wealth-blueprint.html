Before your call.

Is this a fit if I earn a W-2 salary?+

Yes. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is designed for high earners, including W-2 employees, self-employed people, and business owners. The strategies that apply will depend on your income, accounts, debt, and goals.

How does this work with my CPA?+

You keep your CPA. The program provides education and a personal action plan, including a CPA Implementation Brief for you to share. Your tax professional reviews what applies to you and handles your filings.

Do I have to start a business or buy property?+

No. You can focus on your current income, debt, and accounts. The curriculum also covers other income and investment options so you can evaluate them when they fit your plans.

How long do I have access?+

The video curriculum comes with lifetime access. Your Wealth Circle community membership and live support last 12 months.

What should I ask about the guarantee?+

Ask the team to explain the No Client Left Behind Guarantee and provide its current written terms. Confirm the required actions, how a return on your program investment is measured, and when continued support applies before you enroll.

How should I prepare for my strategy call?+

Watch the overview and write down your questions. Think about your main financial goal, the decisions you're weighing, and where you want more clarity. Use the appointment details in your confirmation email to join your scheduled call.
