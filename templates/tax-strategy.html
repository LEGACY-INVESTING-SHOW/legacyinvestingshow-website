How to put it in place

  1. Check that you qualify. Confirm the facts of your own situation meet the test before you plan around the deduction.
  2. Model the numbers. Work out what the strategy returns at your marginal rate, net of what it costs to run.
  3. Build the file as you go. Contemporaneous records defend the position; reconstructed ones rarely do.
  4. Review it each year. Limits, rates and your own facts move. A strategy that fit last year may not fit this one.
