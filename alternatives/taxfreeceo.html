Quick verdict

We can describe it only in general terms: when we last checked, the site did not load for us, and the public footprint is a book of the same name and social content. If you are considering it, verify the current offering, the format, who teaches, and the terms directly before you pay. The alternatives split by what you want. A coached plan that covers W-2 income and real estate as well as business: Legacy Wealth Blueprint, which we publish and offer. A cheap library to learn from first: Tax Strategy Accelerator, or a fixed-scope course. Live time with a licensed CPA and attorney: Mark J. Kohler's Business Owner HQ. And the option people skip because it is not exciting: a local CPA who actually does proactive planning.

What TaxFreeCEO is

TaxFreeCEO markets tax-planning education and coaching to business owners and high earners. The brand is sold under the name of Brandon A. Madden, MBA, EA, and shares its name with his book. Beyond that, we will not describe pages we could not read: when we tried to open the site in September 2026 it did not load, so treat every detail about current format, community, and support as something to confirm with them directly. The book's title promises a large percentage cut in your tax bill. Treat that as a marketing claim, not a planning number: what you can actually cut depends on whether you have a business, whether you own property, and which state you live in.

Format. As far as public listings show, a course and community built around the founder's system. Ask for the current syllabus, the live-call schedule, who answers questions, and whether any licensed professional works on your file.

Known for

  • A book and social content on reducing tax for business owners by building the business around the tax code
  • Positioning aimed at entrepreneurs rather than W-2 employees
  • An EA credential listed with the founder's name

Best for

  • Business owners who follow the founder's content and want his system specifically
  • People who prefer one creator's framework to a broader curriculum

Not ideal for

  • W-2 earners with no business; most business-owner strategies do not apply
  • Anyone who needs to confirm the offering exists and what it includes before committing; do that first
  • People who want the tax plan coordinated with debt, retirement accounts, real estate, and protection

Why people look for TaxFreeCEO alternatives

  • They could not find a clear description of what the program currently includes, or the site would not load, and they want something they can verify.
  • They earn most of their income on a W-2 and the business-owner framing does not fit yet.
  • They own or want to own real estate and want the STR loophole, cost segregation, and REPS covered properly, not as an afterthought.
  • They want a licensed professional accountable for the advice, or a firm that files.
  • They want someone to check in with them, not only a library and a group.
  • Their books are a mess and they suspect, correctly, that no strategy program works on top of bad records.

TaxFreeCEO alternatives, one by one

1. Legacy Wealth Blueprint

Type: Coaching program. Best for: W-2 earners and business owners earning roughly six figures and up who want tax strategy, real estate, debt, accounts, and protection in one written plan with a guide checking in.

Strengths

  • Covers W-2 tax strategies and business strategies in the same pillar, so the plan still works if your income is mostly salary
  • Real estate taught step by step as an investment vehicle, with the tax rules that decide whether a rental helps your return
  • A custom 12-month Wealth Strategy Snapshot, an optional Prior Year Tax Return Diagnostic in the first weeks, and a CPA Implementation Brief before filing season for your own CPA
  • Weekly live coaching with replays, a 1-on-1 Legacy Wealth Guide with bi-weekly check-ins, 12 months of community, an AI advisor, and a partner network of tax, estate, credit, and entity specialists

Tradeoffs

  • Does not file returns and does not contact your CPA; you carry the brief
  • Coaches are educators, not licensed professionals; licensed advice comes through the partner network
  • Pricing is not published; it is discussed on the strategy call
  • We publish this page, so weigh our description accordingly

2. Tax Strategy Accelerator (Karlton Dennis)

Type: Course. Best for: Self-starters who want a large video library and a community before they hire anyone.

Strengths

  • Video courses, a private community, recordings of the weekly calls with the firm's private client group, and AMA sessions with tax advisors
  • The same founder and the same thinking as Tax Alchemy, at a membership commitment rather than a firm engagement
  • A fair way to learn what a business or a rental would actually change on your return before you commit to either

Tradeoffs

  • A membership does not know your name; nobody checks whether you implemented anything
  • It teaches; it does not file, structure entities, or run cost segregation for you
  • If you have only a W-2 and no plans to change that, the library will be mostly aspirational

3. Navi Maraj CPA small business tax course

Type: Course. Best for: Owners who want the S-corp and deduction basics as a fixed-scope, self-paced course with no calls.

Strengths

  • A self-paced course with modules and software walkthroughs, taught by a CPA
  • Fixed scope, so you know exactly what you are buying before you buy it
  • Covers the questions new owners actually have: entity choice, reasonable salary, bookkeeping basics

Tradeoffs

  • No live support and no community to speak of; it is a course
  • Small-business focus; real estate and W-2 strategies are not the point
  • You still need a CPA to file and a plan to know what to do after the course ends

4. Business Owner HQ (Mark J. Kohler)

Type: Coaching program. Best for: Small business owners who want live time with a licensed CPA and attorney on a schedule, with legal services available from his firms.

Strengths

  • Mark J. Kohler is a licensed attorney and CPA; the membership includes biweekly live masterminds with him, an app, on-demand videos, an AI assistant, and a forum
  • KKOS Lawyers and Directed IRA sit in the same group, so entity setup, legal work, and self-directed accounts can be done in one place
  • The trifecta structure is a clear framework for owners with assets to protect

Tradeoffs

  • Built for owners; a W-2 earner with no entity gets less from it
  • Group calls and a forum, not a dedicated 1-on-1 guide
  • The education points toward his firms; get legal quotes separately. See the Mark J. Kohler alternatives guide

5. Anderson Business Advisors Platinum

Type: Community. Best for: Investors with several entities who want ongoing legal and tax answers on demand rather than a course or a one-off plan.

Strengths

  • A membership built around questions: strategy sessions, document review, a forms library, and a weekday Q&A room
  • Legal, tax, and asset protection under one brand, which suits people with multiple LLCs and rentals
  • A published support schedule, so you know when a person is available

Tradeoffs

  • Answers, not a plan; you bring the situation and get a response, which only works if you already know your direction
  • Heavier on entity and asset protection than most W-2 earners need
  • An enrollment plus ongoing membership model; confirm the current terms and what stops when you leave

  • A network of advisor firms trained in that method, which solves the hardest problem in this category: finding a planner, not a preparer

9. WCG CPAs & Advisors

Type: Firm or professional service. Best for: S-corp owners and rental owners who want a CPA firm that publishes its thinking and handles planning and preparation together.

Strengths

  • Known for long, detailed public guides on S corporations, reasonable salary, and rental property taxation, so you can read how they think before you engage
  • Preparation, planning, and small-business services under one roof
  • A firm, so licensed professionals do the work and sign the return

Tradeoffs

  • A firm engagement, not coaching; nobody checks whether you followed through on the non-tax parts of your finances
  • Capacity and minimums change; verify current intake rules and services directly

10. LYFE Accounting (Sherman the CPA)

Type: Firm or professional service. Best for: Owners whose books are not clean enough for any strategy to work yet, and who want accounting, preparation, and planning from one month-to-month firm.

Strengths

  • Bookkeeping, tax preparation and planning, and CPA and CFO services under one roof
  • Described as month to month with no long contract, which lowers the risk of trying it
  • Solves the problem that stops most strategy programs from working: bad records

Tradeoffs

  • An accounting firm first; strategy depth depends on the engagement you choose
  • Not a coaching program and not built for W-2 earners
  • Verify current services and intake rules directly

Visit LYFE Accounting (Sherman the CPA) (opens their site)

11. A local CPA who does proactive planning

Type: Firm or professional service. Best for: Anyone whose current CPA is a preparer and who wants a planner in the same city, without a program wrapped around it.

Strengths

  • Local knowledge of state rules, and a person you can sit across from
  • Planning plus preparation from the same person removes the hand-off risk
  • Often the least expensive route to a specific strategy modelled with your real numbers

Tradeoffs

  • Most local CPAs are compliance-first; ask what strategies they proposed to clients last year and how many S-corp or cost segregation returns they handle
  • No curriculum, no community, no accountability between meetings
  • You still need to know enough to ask the right questions, which is where free content or a program earns its keep

Where Legacy Wealth Blueprint fits

Legacy Wealth Blueprint is built for people earning roughly six figures and up who want to optimise the income they already have, including people who are not yet interested in a business or real estate. That is the main reason it tends to fit the people who bounce off business-owner-first programs: the Tax Optimisation pillar covers W-2 strategies, tax-advantaged accounts, deductions, and entity structures for when a business does arrive, and the Investment Vehicles pillar teaches real estate step by step next to retirement accounts.

The deliverables are concrete. Lifetime access to the 100+ video curriculum. Twelve months of weekly live coaching with replays and the Wealth Circle community. Where Legacy Wealth Blueprint does not fit

It is not a firm. If you want the return filed and the entities set up for you, choose Tax Alchemy, WCG, or a local CPA. It is not a business-operations program; it will not teach you to run payroll or bookkeeping, and if your books are a mess, fix that first. It is not for someone early in their career with a modest income, and it is not for anyone who would pay from an emergency fund. Pricing is not published; it is discussed on the strategy call, which is a real drawback if you want a number before you talk to anyone.

How to decide

  1. Verify before you compare. For TaxFreeCEO, and for every program on this page, get the current syllabus, the support model, the term, and the refund policy in an email.

  2. Decide whether you are buying knowledge or labour. A course gives you knowledge; a firm gives you labour. If you are busy and the money is there, labour is often the better buy. If you want to understand your own return, buy knowledge first.

  3. Check what you own. Most of these strategies need a business or a rental. With neither, the honest list is short: max the 401(k) and HSA, use a taxable account well, then decide whether to build a business or buy property.

  4. Fix the books before you buy strategy. If you own a business and the bookkeeping is a mess, an accounting firm solves that first. Strategy on top of bad records is guesswork.

  5. Ask who actually answers you. A published call schedule or a dedicated guide is not the same as a forum. Then ask for five numbers in writing: enrollment fee, monthly fee, term length, renewal price, and refund window. A seller who will not put them in an email has told you something.

  6. Run the S-corp and W-2 employee guides against your own numbers before any call, so you know which strategies are even on the table.

Questions people ask

What is TaxFreeCEO?

A tax-planning education brand aimed at business owners, sold under the name of Brandon A. Madden, MBA, EA, and sharing its name with his book. We could not load the site when we checked in September 2026, so verify the current offering, format, and terms directly with them before you pay.

What is the best TaxFreeCEO alternative for a W-2 earner?

Most business-owner programs assume an entity. Legacy Wealth Blueprint covers W-2 strategies, tax-advantaged accounts, and real estate inside one plan, which is why it fits salaried earners better. If you own nothing yet, start with the free W-2 guides and max your accounts first.

Which options do the work for me instead of teaching me?

Tax Alchemy, Tax Goddess, WCG, LYFE Accounting, and a local CPA do the work. WealthAbility matches you with a firm that does. Tax Strategy Accelerator, the Navi Maraj course, Business Owner HQ, Anderson Platinum, and Legacy Wealth Blueprint teach or coach; the Blueprint adds a written plan and a brief for your own CPA, but you still carry it to them.

Which alternative actually files my tax return?

WCG and a local CPA file. Tax Alchemy offers preparation to advisory clients. WealthAbility's matched advisor firms file. Legacy Wealth Blueprint does not; it gives you a CPA Implementation Brief for your own CPA.

What is the cheapest alternative to TaxFreeCEO?

Free content and the retirement accounts you already have access to. After that, an education membership such as Tax Strategy Accelerator, then a fixed-scope self-paced course. We do not quote prices here because they change; read each seller's own page.

Is a local CPA a real alternative to a tax program?

Yes, if they plan rather than only prepare. Ask what strategies they proposed to clients last year, how many S-corp and rental returns they handle, and whether they will meet mid-year. A proactive local CPA plus free education beats an expensive program you never open.

How should I compare two programs fairly?

Same five numbers from each, in writing: enrollment fee, monthly fee, term length, renewal price, refund window. Then the same three questions: who answers me, do you file, and what do I hold in writing when the term ends. Compare deliverables before headline cost.

Does Legacy Wealth Blueprint cover real estate tax strategies?

Yes. Real estate is taught step by step in the Investment Vehicles pillar, and the site's guides on the STR loophole, cost segregation, and REPS are part of the same material. The program teaches when those strategies apply; specialists you hire do the studies and the filing.

How much do these alternatives cost?

We do not publish pricing for Legacy Wealth Blueprint, and we do not quote other providers' prices on this page because they change. Get each provider's current terms in writing and compare what is delivered: who answers you, who files, and what you hold at the end.
