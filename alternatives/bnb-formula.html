Quick verdict

BNB Formula is the safe, well-known choice if you want a big, long-running arbitrage brand and are happy to learn the price and coaching details on a call. It is a weaker fit if you want one coach who reviews your specific lease, if you need a published price and refund policy first, or if you have realised you want to own the property rather than lease it.

Airbnb Ascension is ours and we flag it throughout. It suits the buyer who wants one property finished with one coach and done-for-you setup. If cost transparency is why you are leaving, Sean Rakidzich's published, refundable courses solve that better than we do. If taxes are why, read the tax section: the short-term rental loophole belongs to owners, not tenants.

What BNB Formula is

Brian Page's Airbnb rental arbitrage coaching program: lease a property long-term, get written permission to sublet, furnish it and rent it by the night. His page claims more than 25,000 students in 47 countries, which makes it one of the most established brands in the category.

Format. The public entry offer is a free book that routes you to a call. Price is not published on the pages we could open, and format details (how much is video, how much is live, who coaches) are thin. Verify the current offering with his team before buying.

Known for

  • One of the earliest and most heavily marketed arbitrage brands
  • Large student numbers and wide international reach
  • A free-book funnel that lets you read the framework before a sales call

Best for

  • Buyers who value a long track record and a big alumni base over hands-on coaching
  • People who want an arbitrage-only curriculum with nothing else bolted on
  • Readers who want the book before deciding whether to pay for more

Not ideal for

  • People who want a named coach reviewing their lease and deal numbers
  • Buyers who need a price and a written refund policy before a call
  • Anyone whose real goal is equity, depreciation or the STR tax rules, which require ownership

Why people look for BNB Formula alternatives

  • No price on the site; the entry path is a free book, then a call, so comparison shopping is slow.
  • Public pages say little about who coaches you and how often, and nothing about a landlord reference.
  • No guarantee published on the pages we could open.
  • They want to know what arbitrage does to their taxes, or they have a down payment and want to buy, and an arbitrage-only program covers neither.

BNB Formula alternatives, one by one

1. Airbnb Ascension (Preston Seo)

Type: Coaching program. Best for: One property, one dedicated coach, done-for-you setup, and a tax and entity session built in

Strengths

  • Dedicated 1-on-1 coach: onboarding call, day-to-day messaging for deal reviews, ad-hoc calls, weekly progress reports on a 6-month plan
  • Property Profitability Reports (market, cash flow, ROI, tax impact) before you sign, a Market Matrix of the top 500 U.S. markets, landlord scripts and sublease templates, and coaches who act as your reference
  • Done-for-you listing, furnishing guidance, messaging automation and pricing setup, a 45 to 60 minute entity, credit and tax session, and 7+ hours of Airbnb Arbitrage Roadmap curriculum for life

Tradeoffs

  • Standard 6-month term is one property; the 12-month Scale version covers more with no count guaranteed
  • No published price or money-back guarantee, so it does not solve the transparency complaint; you still make the landlord calls
  • Bring roughly $10,000 to $25,000 of your own working capital for deposits and furniture, per the program's guidance

Read more about Airbnb Ascension (Preston Seo)

2. 10XBNB (Shaun Ghavami and Ari Rahmanian)

Type: Course. Best for: Arbitrage and co-hosting taught together in a self-paced library with a big group

Strengths

  • Covers arbitrage, bed flipping and co-listing, so you can start with co-hosting if you lack deposit money
  • Self-paced modules, an active Facebook group and live group calls

Tradeoffs

  • Price on application; group coaching rather than a named coach
  • No landlord reference or guarantee found on the pages we checked

Read more about 10XBNB (Shaun Ghavami and Ari Rahmanian)

3. Sean Rakidzich's courses (Airbnb Automated)

Type: Course. Best for: A published price and a 30-day refund before spending anything on coaching

Strengths

  • Prices and a 30-day money-back guarantee are on his compare page
  • Short separate courses on pricing, market data and search ranking; buy one, not a bundle

Tradeoffs

  • No lease review or landlord reference; coaching is application-only
  • No done-for-you setup; you build the listing and systems yourself

Read more about Sean Rakidzich's courses (Airbnb Automated)

4. STR University-style acquisition courses

Type: Course. Best for: People with a down payment who want the tax benefits of owning a short-term rental

Strengths

  • Teach underwriting, financing and buying, so you build equity and can depreciate the building
  • Ownership is what unlocks the STR loophole and cost segregation; leasing does not

Tradeoffs

  • Needs a down payment, closing costs and reserves; slower to the first dollar
  • Programs vary widely; verify the instructor and the curriculum date

Read more about STR University-style acquisition courses

5. BiggerPockets short-term rental resources

Type: Community. Best for: Self-directed readers who want free forums, calculators and podcasts first

Strengths

  • Large free forum with short-term rental threads, plus podcasts and books
  • Many voices rather than one seller

Tradeoffs

  • Arbitrage is a minority topic and owner-investors often argue against it
  • No coaching, deal review or accountability

6. Free YouTube

Type: Free resource. Best for: Deciding whether you want this business before you pay for a book, a course or a coach

Strengths

  • Zero cost, and several operators, Preston and Rakidzich included, walk through real deals
  • You learn the vocabulary before any sales call, so you ask better questions

Tradeoffs

  • No lease review, accountability or landlord reference
  • Hard to tell what is current; rules change city by city

Airbnb Ascension fits when your problem is execution, not information. The 6-month program is scoped to one property: a dedicated coach, a Market Matrix to pick the city, a Property Profitability Report on each deal, landlord scripts with roleplay and a coach as your reference, then done-for-you listing, furnishing, automation and pricing setup. Where Legacy Wealth Blueprint does not fit

If you left BNB Formula for price transparency or a refund guarantee, we do not fix that: Ascension pricing is discussed on the call and there is no published money-back guarantee. If you want more than one property inside six months, ask about the 12-month Scale version and understand it guarantees no count. If your reserve for deposits and furniture is thin, wait.

Owning gives him depreciation and the STR rules; a tenant in an arbitrage unit gets neither. Read the short-term rental loophole page before choosing a model.

How to decide

  1. Confirm short-term rentals are legal in the zone you can afford in your target city. If not, no program helps.

  2. Decide lease or buy. Arbitrage starts faster and cheaper and produces ordinary business income. Owning needs a down payment and is the only model that gets depreciation and the STR loophole.

  3. If you lease, decide coach or library. Ascension is a coach; BNB Formula and 10XBNB lean library plus group; Rakidzich is library only.

  4. Ask every seller in writing: the price, the refund policy, who coaches you and how often, and whether they review your specific lease.

  5. Set up the entity and the books before the first booking. The tax outcome depends on records you keep from month one. Our Airbnb host tax page lists what to track.

Questions people ask

Is BNB Formula legit?

It is a long-running, widely marketed coaching brand with a large claimed student base. Whether it is the right purchase depends on answers its public pages do not give: the price, the refund terms, who coaches you and how often, and whether anyone reviews your lease. Ask for all four in writing, and apply the same test to us.

How much does BNB Formula cost?

Brian Page does not publish a price on the pages we could open; the entry offer is a free book and the site routes you to a call. Airbnb Ascension does not publish a price either; it is discussed on the strategy call. The only option here with a published price is Sean Rakidzich's courses.

What is the best alternative to BNB Formula?

A coach who reviews your lease and vouches for you with landlords: Airbnb Ascension. A published price and a 30-day refund: Sean Rakidzich. Co-hosting as a starting point: 10XBNB. Ownership and its tax benefits: an acquisition course plus the short-term rental loophole. Free: YouTube and BiggerPockets.

How is Airbnb arbitrage income taxed?

Arbitrage is a business you operate, not a property you own. Nightly revenue is business income; rent, furniture, supplies, cleaning, software and platform fees are business expenses. Because you do not own the building, there is nothing to depreciate and no cost segregation to run. This is education; confirm your facts with a tax professional.

Does the short-term rental loophole apply to Airbnb arbitrage?

Generally no. The loophole lets an owner who materially participates treat rental losses, usually created by depreciation, as non-passive so they can offset income such as W-2 wages. An arbitrage operator does not own the property, so there is no depreciation to create that loss. If that outcome is your goal, study ownership, not arbitrage.

Should I lease or buy my first short-term rental?

Lease if you want cash flow quickly with less capital and can stomach owing rent in a slow month. Buy if you have a down payment, want equity, and want the depreciation and STR tax rules that only owners get. Our students have done each: Tom and Kiana leased, Jeff bought.
