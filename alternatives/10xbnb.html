10XBNB Alternatives: Six Airbnb Arbitrage Programs Compared

It is a weaker fit if you want one coach who reads your lease and runs the numbers before you sign, or if you want to test the model cheaply first. Those two reasons drive most searches for alternatives, and they point to different programs.

Quick verdict

10XBNB is a fair buy if you want arbitrage and co-hosting taught together in a self-paced library with a big group and live calls. It is a weaker fit if you want one coach who reads your lease and runs the numbers before you sign, or if you want to test the model cheaply first. Those two reasons drive most searches for alternatives, and they point to different programs.

Airbnb Ascension is our program and we flag it wherever it appears. It suits the one-coach, one-property, done-with-you buyer. If you are self-directed and want a refundable first step, Sean Rakidzich's courses beat both of us. If you want to buy rather than lease, skip every arbitrage program here. Our three-way comparison has the sourced detail.

What 10XBNB is

An Airbnb coaching brand from Shaun Ghavami and Ari Rahmanian that teaches three ways to earn without owning: rental arbitrage (lease, furnish, sublet nightly), bed flipping (rent by the room), and co-listing, its term for co-hosting someone else's listing for a share of revenue.

Format. Self-paced modules, an active private Facebook group and live group coaching calls. No price on the program, FAQ or reviews pages we checked; the FAQ routes you to a contact page. Verify the current offering on its site before buying.

Known for

  • Arbitrage and co-hosting in one program
  • A large social footprint and a busy student community
  • Co-listing as a low-capital entry for people without deposit money

Best for

  • Starting with co-hosting and moving into arbitrage later without a second purchase
  • Learners who thrive in a big group and will post questions publicly
  • People who want a library to revisit at their own pace

Not ideal for

  • Anyone who wants one named coach reviewing their exact lease and numbers
  • Buyers who need the price and refund terms in writing before a call
  • People who want to own the property; equity and depreciation are not in the model

Why people look for 10XBNB alternatives

  • No price on the site, so you cannot compare it without booking a call.
  • Group coaching: your question competes with everyone else's, and nobody is accountable for your progress.
  • No landlord reference. Written sublet permission is the hardest step in arbitrage, and a group cannot vouch for you.
  • No published money-back guarantee on the pages we checked.

10XBNB alternatives, one by one

1. Airbnb Ascension (Preston Seo)

Type: Coaching program. Best for: One property, one dedicated coach, done-with-you setup from signed lease to first guest

Strengths

  • Dedicated 1-on-1 coach: onboarding call, day-to-day messaging for deal reviews, ad-hoc calls, weekly progress reports on a 6-month plan
  • Property Profitability Reports (market, cash flow, ROI, tax impact) before you sign, a Market Matrix of the top 500 U.S. markets, and coaches who vouch for you with landlords
  • Done-for-you listing, furnishing guidance, messaging automation and pricing setup, a 45 to 60 minute entity, credit and tax session, and 7+ hours of Airbnb Arbitrage Roadmap curriculum for life

Tradeoffs

  • Standard term is 6 months and one property; the 12-month Scale version covers more, with no count guaranteed
  • No published price or money-back guarantee; you still make the landlord calls, with scripts and roleplay
  • Bring roughly $10,000 to $25,000 of your own working capital for deposits and furniture, per the program's guidance

Read more about Airbnb Ascension (Preston Seo)

2. BNB Formula (Brian Page)

Type: Coaching program. Best for: The largest, longest-running arbitrage brand, if you will ask for the price on a call

Strengths

  • His page claims 25,000+ students in 47 countries; a free book is the entry offer
  • Arbitrage-only, so nothing is wasted if that is all you want

Tradeoffs

  • Price not published; coaching format is thin on the public pages
  • No guarantee on the pages we could open; verify the current offering

Read more about BNB Formula (Brian Page)

3. Sean Rakidzich's courses (Airbnb Automated)

Type: Course. Best for: A refundable, low-commitment first step from an active operator

Strengths

  • Publishes his prices and a 30-day money-back guarantee on his compare page
  • Short separate courses on pricing, market data and search ranking, so you buy one piece

Tradeoffs

  • No lease review or landlord reference; his coaching tier is application-only
  • You supply your own sequencing and accountability

Read more about Sean Rakidzich's courses (Airbnb Automated)

4. STR University-style acquisition courses

Type: Course. Best for: People with a down payment who want to buy and hold rather than lease

Strengths

  • Teach underwriting, financing and buying, so equity and depreciation are part of the return
  • Only ownership unlocks the short-term rental tax rules

Tradeoffs

  • Needs a down payment and reserves; slower to the first dollar
  • Curricula vary widely; verify the instructor and the date

Read more about STR University-style acquisition courses

5. BiggerPockets short-term rental resources

Type: Community. Best for: Free forums, calculators and podcasts before paying anyone

Strengths

  • A large free forum with short-term rental threads, plus podcasts and books
  • Many voices rather than one seller

Tradeoffs

  • Arbitrage is a minority topic there and owner-investors often argue against it
  • No coaching, deal review or accountability

6. Free YouTube

Type: Free resource. Best for: Deciding whether you want this business at all

Strengths

  • Zero cost; Preston, Rakidzich and many operators walk through real deals on camera
  • You see the daily work before any sales call

Tradeoffs

  • No lease review, accountability or landlord reference
  • Hard to tell what is current; rules change city by city

Which option fits which situation

No deposit money yet; you want to start with co-hosting 10XBNB Co-listing is core to its curriculum. Ascension is built around leasing a unit, which needs capital; its co-hosting masterclass is a scaling resource, not the entry point.

Where Legacy Wealth Blueprint fits

Airbnb Ascension is the pick when you want one person accountable to you and one property finished: a dedicated coach, a Market Matrix to choose the city, a Property Profitability Report before you sign, landlord scripts with roleplay and a coach who vouches for you, then done-for-you listing, furnishing, automation and pricing setup. The Airbnb Arbitrage Roadmap curriculum underneath is about 7 hours of video with lifetime access; the rest is people and process.

The results we cite are on our reviews page. Tom, after a corporate layoff, booked $25,000 in reservations in his first two weeks and reports $5,000 to $6,000 a month from his Chesapeake Bay unit. Kiana left a ten-year finance career, runs two Scottsdale units at about $3,200 a month, and quit her job three months in. Individual outcomes, not a promise. Our Trustpilot profile shows a 4.2 TrustScore from 66 reviews as of September 2026.

Where Legacy Wealth Blueprint does not fit

Ascension is the wrong first step if you want to test the idea cheaply: pricing is not published, there is no published money-back guarantee, and you bring your own capital for deposits and furniture. It is also wrong if you want more than one property in six months on the standard term, or if co-hosting is your starting point.

It is not for buyers. Our student Jeff chose to buy his Galveston property after 28 years in banking and reports $7,000 to $8,000 a month after his mortgage. Different model, different tax picture. If ownership is your goal, read the short-term rental loophole page before talking to us or to 10XBNB.

How to decide

  1. Check your target city's short-term rental rules. If stays under 30 days are banned where you can afford to lease, no program helps.

  2. Decide lease or buy, then co-hosting or arbitrage. Buying points to an acquisition course; co-hosting to 10XBNB; arbitrage with a coach to Ascension; arbitrage alone to Rakidzich.

  3. Count your reserve. If a program fee would eat the money you need for deposits and furniture, buy the cheap course and keep the cash.

  4. Ask every seller the same four things in writing: the price, the refund policy, who coaches you, and whether they review your specific lease before you sign.

  5. Run one deal through our arbitrage ROI and break-even occupancy calculators. If it fails in a spreadsheet, no program fixes it.

Questions people ask

Is 10XBNB legit?

It is an operating coaching company with public program pages, an active community and live group calls. Whether it is the right buy for you is a separate question. Ask for the price and refund terms in writing, who coaches you and how often, and whether anyone reviews your specific lease. Judge every program, including ours, by those answers.

How much does 10XBNB cost?

10XBNB does not publish a price; its FAQ sends you to a contact page. Airbnb Ascension does not publish one either; it is discussed on the strategy call. Sean Rakidzich's courses are the option here with a published price.

What is the difference between 10XBNB and Airbnb Ascension?

10XBNB teaches arbitrage, bed flipping and co-listing through self-paced modules, a Facebook group and live group calls. Airbnb Ascension is a 6-month, one-property arbitrage program with a dedicated 1-on-1 coach, a Property Profitability Report on each deal, a coach who acts as a landlord reference, and done-for-you setup after the lease. One is a broader library; the other is narrower and hands-on.

What is the best alternative to 10XBNB?

It depends on why you are leaving. A coach who reviews your lease and vouches for you: Airbnb Ascension. A published price and a 30-day refund: Sean Rakidzich. Buying instead of leasing: an acquisition course plus the short-term rental tax rules. Free: BiggerPockets and YouTube.

Do 10XBNB or Airbnb Ascension offer a money-back guarantee?

We found none on the 10XBNB pages we checked, and Airbnb Ascension does not publish one. Rakidzich publishes a 30-day money-back guarantee. Whatever you buy, get the refund policy in writing first.

How much money do I need to start Airbnb arbitrage?

Separate from any program cost, our guidance is roughly $10,000 to $25,000 of working capital for a first unit: deposit, first month's rent, furniture and a reserve for a slow first quarter. Some markets need less, some more. Run your target unit through our arbitrage ROI calculator before you commit.
