Tax Alchemy alternatives: ten options, who fits each, and when Karlton Dennis is the right call

Tax Alchemy sells labour. Decide first whether you want the work done, the material taught, or a coached plan that covers more than tax; that choice removes most of the list.

Quick verdict

Tax Alchemy is a done-for-you tax planning firm. If you want a team to build the plan, set up the entities, run the cost segregation, and prepare the return, and you are comfortable being quoted on a call, it is a legitimate way to hand the whole job off. Most people searching for an alternative are not rejecting the firm; they are discovering that they wanted something cheaper, more educational, more licensed, or more focused on the W-2 side of their life.

Sort by what you want to hand off. Want the labour done: compare Tax Alchemy with Tax Goddess, a fee-only planner, and a firm like AE Tax, and ask each who holds the licence. Want to learn first: Tax Strategy Accelerator from the same founder, or a fixed-scope course. Want ongoing answers for a multi-entity setup: Anderson Platinum. Want a licensed CPA and attorney teaching: Mark J. Kohler. Want a vetted advisor matched to you: WealthAbility. Want to learn and be coached through a written plan that covers more than tax: Legacy Wealth Blueprint. We publish this page and offer the Blueprint, and our earlier three-way comparison, Karlton Dennis vs Mark Kohler vs Legacy Wealth Blueprint, reaches the same conclusions.

What Tax Alchemy is

Two things share the Karlton Dennis name, and people mix them up. Tax Alchemy is the firm: its site lists Karlton Dennis as CEO and describes tax strategy analysis, entity structuring, implementation help, real estate tax planning, cost segregation, and 1031 exchange work, with tax preparation offered to advisory clients. Tax Strategy Accelerator is the education membership from the same founder: video courses, a private community, recordings of weekly client calls, and AMA sessions with tax advisors. Karlton Dennis built the audience on YouTube, and his public bio describes him as a tax strategist without listing a CPA or EA licence; Tax Alchemy describes its team as licensed tax strategists.

Format. The firm is a 1-to-1 advisory engagement quoted on a call. The membership is self-paced video plus community and call recordings. Neither publishes a fixed scope for every client, so ask exactly what is included before you sign.

Known for

  • High-production YouTube content on write-offs, entity structure, and real estate tax moves
  • A done-for-you model where planning, entity work, and preparation sit with one team
  • Real estate tax planning, cost segregation, and 1031 exchange work for investor clients
  • A lower-commitment education membership for people who want to learn first

Best for

  • Business owners and investors who want the plan built and implemented for them
  • People who already have a business or property and a tax bill large enough for a firm engagement to pay back
  • Self-starters who want a large video library and community through the membership

Not ideal for

  • W-2 earners with no business and no property; no firm can invent deductions you do not have
  • People who want the person on the sales call to be the person who does the work
  • Anyone who wants debt, credit, retirement accounts, and protection covered alongside tax
  • Buyers who want a fixed, published scope before the call

Why people look for Tax Alchemy alternatives

  • They were quoted for the firm and wanted the education membership, or the other way round, and want to know what else sits in between.
  • They want to know who holds the licence. The founder is the face; the team does the work. That is normal for a firm, but people want it stated plainly.
  • They are W-2 earners, and every strategy in the videos assumes a business or a rental they do not yet have.
  • They want coaching on the whole money picture, not only the tax return: debt, accounts, protection, and the order to do things in.
  • They want a CPA and an attorney in the room, and would rather buy from a licensed professional directly.
  • They tried to hire a firm and wanted the plan explained to them well enough to run it with their own CPA next year.
  • They already own several entities and want ongoing legal and tax answers on demand, which is a membership job rather than a firm job.

Tax Alchemy alternatives, one by one

1. Legacy Wealth Blueprint

Type: Coaching program. Best for: People earning roughly six figures and up, W-2 or business owner, who want tax strategy coordinated with debt, income, investing, and protection in one written plan, with a guide checking in.

Strengths

  • A custom 12-month Wealth Strategy Snapshot and an optional Prior Year Tax Return Diagnostic in weeks two to three that lists the strategies your last return missed
  • Weekly live coaching calls with replays, a 1-on-1 Legacy Wealth Guide with bi-weekly accountability check-ins, and 12 months in the private Wealth Circle community
  • Five pillars, not one: tax optimisation, debt and credit leverage, income diversification, investment vehicles, and protection
  • A CPA Implementation Brief around months ten to eleven that you hand to your own CPA, so you keep your CPA and the strategies still land on the return
  • Partner network with a tax strategist, estate attorney, credit advisor, and entity setup partner, including a private strategy session

Tradeoffs

  • It does not file returns and the team does not contact your CPA; if you want the work done for you, Tax Alchemy is the closer match
  • Preston Seo is a personal finance educator, not a CPA or attorney; licensed advice comes through the partner network
  • Real estate is one pillar. If cost segregation and 1031 work are the whole point, a real estate tax specialist may fit better
  • Pricing is not published and is discussed on the strategy call; that is a tradeoff for anyone who wants a number up front

2. Tax Strategy Accelerator (Karlton Dennis)

Type: Course. Best for: Self-starters who want a large video library and a community before they hire anyone.

Strengths

  • Video courses, a private community, recordings of the weekly calls with the firm's private client group, and AMA sessions with tax advisors
  • The same founder and the same thinking as Tax Alchemy, at a membership commitment rather than a firm engagement
  • A fair way to learn what a business or a rental would actually change on your return before you commit to either

Tradeoffs

  • A membership does not know your name; nobody checks whether you implemented anything
  • It teaches; it does not file, structure entities, or run cost segregation for you
  • If you have only a W-2 and no plans to change that, the library will be mostly aspirational

3. WealthAbility (Tom Wheelwright, CPA)

Type: Firm or professional service. Best for: People who want a vetted tax advisor matched to them, trained in one consistent philosophy, rather than a course.

Strengths

  • Founded by Tom Wheelwright, a CPA and the author of Tax-Free Wealth, so the philosophy is documented in a book you can read before you commit
  • A network of advisor firms trained in the same approach, which solves the problem of finding a proactive CPA in your area
  • Education, live trainings, and advisory services under one brand

Tradeoffs

  • The advisor you are matched with is a separate firm with its own fees and capacity; the experience varies by advisor
  • Pricing is on application, so plan on a call before you know the cost
  • The emphasis is tax and wealth; if you need coaching on debt or income first, that is not the focus

4. Mark J. Kohler (Business Owner HQ, KKOS Lawyers, Directed IRA)

Type: Coaching program. Best for: Small business owners who want a licensed CPA and attorney teaching, with legal and entity services available under the same roof.

Strengths

  • Mark J. Kohler is a licensed attorney and CPA, and Business Owner HQ includes biweekly live masterminds with him, an app, on-demand videos, an AI assistant, and a forum
  • KKOS Lawyers and Directed IRA mean the education, the entity work, and the self-directed retirement accounts can all be done inside his group of companies
  • His trifecta structure (operating entity, holding entity, trust) is a clear framework for owners with assets to protect

Tradeoffs

  • Built for business owners; a W-2 earner with no entity gets less from it
  • The education naturally points toward his firms; get legal and membership quotes separately
  • Group calls and a forum, not a dedicated 1-on-1 guide; see our Mark J. Kohler alternatives guide for the full fit

Visit Mark J. Kohler (Business Owner HQ, KKOS Lawyers, Directed IRA) (opens their site)

5. Anderson Business Advisors Platinum

Type: Community. Best for: Investors with several entities who want ongoing legal and tax answers on demand rather than a course or a one-off plan.

Strengths

  • A membership built around questions: strategy sessions, document review, a forms library, and a weekday Q&A room
  • Legal, tax, and asset protection under one brand, which suits people with multiple LLCs and rentals
  • A published support schedule, so you know when a person is available

Tradeoffs

  • Answers, not a plan; you bring the situation and get a response, which only works if you already know your direction
  • Heavier on entity and asset protection than most W-2 earners need
  • An enrollment plus ongoing membership model; confirm the current terms and what stops when you leave

6. Tax Goddess

Type: Firm or professional service. Best for: High-revenue business owners who want a firm to run strategic tax planning as an ongoing engagement.

Strengths

  • A done-for-you firm model with 1-to-1 engagement, aimed at owners who would rather pay for labour than a classroom
  • Describes its clients in revenue tiers, which tells you quickly whether you are the intended buyer

Tradeoffs

  • Priced on application, so expect a call before you know the cost
  • Built for businesses with meaningful revenue; a W-2 earner or a new side business is not the target
  • A firm, not a coaching program: nothing on debt, income, or protection outside the tax engagement

Visit Tax Goddess (opens their site)

7. A fee-only tax planner (CPA, EA, or CFP)

Type: Firm or professional service. Best for: Someone with a clear question who wants a licensed professional paid a flat fee for a planning engagement, without a program wrapped around it.

Strengths

  • You know who is doing the work, what they are licensed as, and what the engagement covers
  • No products sold and no membership; you pay for the plan and leave
  • Often the cheapest way to get a specific strategy modelled with your actual numbers

Tradeoffs

  • Finding one who does proactive planning rather than compliance takes real searching; ask what strategies they proposed to clients last year
  • No community, no curriculum, and no accountability between meetings
  • A one-time plan goes stale; you will need to re-engage each year

8. AE Tax

Type: Firm or professional service. Best for: People shopping for done-for-you tax planning who want a second firm to interview against Tax Alchemy.

Strengths

  • It shows up in the same searches as Tax Alchemy for done-for-you planning, so it is a natural second quote
  • A second quote is the fastest way to learn whether the first one was reasonable

Tradeoffs

  • We have not reviewed its offering firsthand, so treat it as a firm to interview, not a recommendation from us
  • Ask the same questions you would ask any firm: who holds the licence and in which state, what the engagement includes, whether they file, and what the exit looks like when the term ends

9. Navi Maraj CPA small business tax course

Type: Course. Best for: Owners who want the S-corp and deduction basics as a fixed-scope, self-paced course with no calls.

Strengths

  • A self-paced course with modules and software walkthroughs, taught by a CPA
  • Fixed scope, so you know exactly what you are buying before you buy it
  • Covers the questions new owners actually have: entity choice, reasonable salary, bookkeeping basics

Tradeoffs

  • No live support and no community to speak of; it is a course
  • Small-business focus; real estate and W-2 strategies are not the point
  • You still need a CPA to file and a plan to know what to do after the course ends

Visit Navi Maraj CPA small business tax course (opens their site)

10. Do it yourself with free content and your current CPA

Type: Free resource. Best for: W-2 earners with no business or property yet, and owners whose books are not clean enough for strategy to matter.

Strengths

  • Our tax strategy library and S-corp guide cover the same ground the videos do, and the calculators let you test a strategy before paying anyone
  • If you have only a W-2, the honest list of moves is short: max the retirement accounts and the HSA, then decide whether to build a business or buy property
  • Costs nothing and tells you which paid option you actually need

Tradeoffs

  • Your current CPA may be a preparer, not a planner; free content will not make them proactive
  • No accountability; most people read and do nothing
  • You can misjudge whether you qualify for a strategy and find out at filing time

Where Legacy Wealth Blueprint fits

Legacy Wealth Blueprint is education plus coaching, not a firm. It fits people earning roughly six figures and up who want to optimise what they already earn: taxes first, then debt and credit, income, investment vehicles, and protection. You get lifetime access to the 100+ video curriculum, 12 months of weekly live coaching with replays, a custom 12-month Wealth Strategy Snapshot, an optional Prior Year Tax Return Diagnostic in the first weeks, a 1-on-1 Legacy Wealth Guide with bi-weekly check-ins, an AI advisor trained on the system, templates such as a CPA vetting checklist and a tax planning timeline, and access to a partner network with a tax strategist, estate attorney, credit advisor, and entity setup partner.

The clearest difference from Tax Alchemy is the CPA Implementation Brief. Around months ten to eleven you receive a written summary of the strategies you implemented, and you hand it to your own CPA. You keep your CPA; the team does not contact them. Shawn, a business owner, came to the program for tax knowledge after struggling to get proactive answers from CPAs. Abigail, a former oil and gas engineer, calculated just under a hundred thousand dollars in first-year return by her own maths. Individual, self-reported results; not typical and not a promise. Third-party reviews sit at a 4.2 TrustScore from 66 Trustpilot reviews, with 77 percent five-star and 9 percent one-star, on the reviews page.

Where Legacy Wealth Blueprint does not fit

If you want the work done for you, the Blueprint is the wrong buy and Tax Alchemy or a fee-only planner is the right one. If your business is large enough that entity restructuring, cost segregation, and 1031 work are the whole agenda, a real estate tax specialist or a firm will go deeper on those specific moves. If you want a cheap video library and nothing else, Tax Strategy Accelerator does that job well. If paying would come from an emergency fund or a card you cannot clear, clear the debt first. And if you want a number before a call, we do not publish one; pricing is discussed on the strategy call.

How to decide

  1. Decide whether you are buying knowledge, labour, or a coached plan. Tax Alchemy, Tax Goddess, and AE Tax sell labour. Tax Strategy Accelerator and the Navi Maraj course sell knowledge. Legacy Wealth Blueprint and Business Owner HQ sell a coached plan. Anderson Platinum sells answers on demand.

  2. Check what you own. Entity strategies need a business. Depreciation needs property. If you have only a W-2, a program cannot invent deductions for you; it can only help you build the thing that creates them. Start with the accounts and the high-earner deductions guide.

  3. Decide whether you need answers or a plan. Anderson Platinum and Business Owner HQ are built for questions: bring a situation, get an answer. A written plan is someone looking at the whole picture once and telling you the order of operations.

  4. Ask every seller who holds the licence, in which state, and whether that person will work on your file. Write the answer down.

  5. Get the total in writing before the call: full price, payment terms, refund window, and what happens when the term ends. If the number only appears after a long pitch, that is information too. Then get a second quote for any firm engagement.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between Tax Alchemy and Tax Strategy Accelerator?

Tax Alchemy is the done-for-you firm: advisory, entity structuring, implementation, real estate tax planning, cost segregation, 1031 work, and preparation for advisory clients. Tax Strategy Accelerator is the education membership from the same founder: video courses, a community, weekly call recordings, and AMA sessions. Decide which one you were shopping for before you compare alternatives.

Is Karlton Dennis a CPA?

His public bio describes him as a tax strategist and does not list a CPA or EA licence. Tax Alchemy describes its team as licensed tax strategists. If licensure matters to you, ask which person on the team holds the licence and in which state, and whether that person works on your file.

Do I need a business or rental property for these programs to help?

For most of the strategies they teach, yes. Entity planning needs a business and depreciation needs property. A W-2 earner with neither should max the retirement accounts and the HSA first, then decide whether to build a business or buy property. Legacy Wealth Blueprint treats that decision as part of a wider plan; a firm engagement assumes you already made it.

What is the best Tax Alchemy alternative for a W-2 earner?

None of the firms can create deductions for someone with no business and no property. Start with retirement accounts and an HSA, then decide whether to build a business or buy property. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is built for optimising the income you already have and treats that decision as part of a wider plan, which is why it tends to fit W-2 earners better than a firm engagement.

What is the cheapest real alternative to Tax Alchemy?

Free content and your current CPA, then the education membership from the same founder, then a fixed-scope self-paced course such as Navi Maraj's. None of those does the work for you; they are cheap because you do the work. We do not quote prices on this page because they change; check each seller's own site.

Does any program on this list file my tax return?

Tax Alchemy prepares returns for its advisory clients. Tax Goddess and a fee-only planner may, depending on the engagement. Mark J. Kohler's firms provide professional services, quoted separately. Anderson Platinum is a membership, not a preparer. Legacy Wealth Blueprint does not file; you get a CPA Implementation Brief for your own CPA.

Which alternative includes a licensed CPA or attorney?

Mark J. Kohler is a licensed CPA and attorney and teaches on his own live calls. WealthAbility matches you with an advisor firm. A fee-only planner is by definition the licensed professional. Legacy Wealth Blueprint's coaches are educators, with licensed professionals available through the partner network.

Does Legacy Wealth Blueprint do cost segregation or 1031 exchanges for me?

No. It teaches when those strategies apply and how to prepare for them, and the partner network includes a tax strategist, but the studies and the exchange work are done by the specialists you hire. Tax Alchemy lists that work as part of its firm offering, which is a real advantage if that is your main need.

How do I check the real cost before I buy?

Find the price on the seller's own page, not on a review site. Ask what happens at the end of the term, who answers your questions, whether filing is included, and what the refund policy is. Ask for all of it in one email before the call. For Legacy Wealth Blueprint, pricing is discussed on the strategy call rather than published, so ask the same questions there.
