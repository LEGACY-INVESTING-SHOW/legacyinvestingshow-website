### S-Corporation Election Strategy

When STR profits exceed $60,000 annually, converting from LLC to S-Corp status creates significant self-employment tax savings. The strategy splits business income between W-2 salary (subject to FICA taxes of 15.3%) and distributions (not subject to FICA).

**Example Calculation:**
- Projected business profit: $100,000
- Reasonable W-2 salary: $90,000 (FICA: $13,770)
- S-Corp distributions: $10,000 (FICA: $0)
- **Total FICA Savings: $15,240**

The election requires filing Form 2553 by March 15, 2026, for retroactive 2025 treatment.

### Short-Term Rental Tax Loophole Implementation

The STR loophole allows offsetting W-2 income with short-term rental losses when material participation requirements are met:

**Material Participation Test:**
- Work more than 100 hours in the activity during the year, AND
- Work more hours than anyone else (including contractors)
- Document ALL hours with time tracking app

This strategy, combined with cost segregation studies, can unlock $15,000-$40,000 in passive losses that directly reduce taxable W-2 income.

## Advanced STR Market Analysis: Las Vegas Dynamics

### Understanding the Las Vegas STR Ecosystem

Las Vegas presents a unique STR market with distinct characteristics that differ significantly from traditional residential real estate investments:

**Demand Drivers and Seasonal Patterns:**
- **Convention Season (January-February, October-November):** CES, MAGIC, SEMA, and other major shows drive occupancy rates above 85%
- **Entertainment Calendar:** residencies by major artists (Adele, U2, etc.) create micro-booms
- **Sports Events:** Golden Knights (NHL), Raiders (NFL), and UNLV games drive weekend demand
- **Holiday Peaks:** New Year's Eve can command 3-5x normal ADRs
- **Summer Slump:** July-August sees 20-30% ADR reductions due to extreme heat

**Regulatory Environment Deep Dive:**

Trent must navigate a complex and evolving regulatory landscape:

1. **City of Las Vegas Short-Term Rental Ordinance:**
   - Registration required ($100 annual fee)
   - 1,000-foot distance requirement from other registered STRs
   - Primary residence exemption no longer available for pure arbitrage
   - 24/7 local contact requirement

2. **Clark County Regulations (Unincorporated Areas):**
   - Different rules apply outside city limits
   - Some Henderson and North Las Vegas areas offer regulatory advantages
   - Ongoing legal challenges create uncertainty

3. **HOA and Building Restrictions:**
   - Many condominium buildings have STR prohibitions
   - Single-family home HOAs increasingly restrict short-term rentals
   - Due diligence on CC&Rs is essential before lease signing

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the core strategy for building a $20K/month STR cash flow?

The strategy focuses on acquiring multiple short-term rental units through arbitrage, maximizing tax benefits through bonus depreciation and cost segregation, and scaling systematically while maintaining quality operations. Each unit targets $1,500-$2,500 in monthly net profit, with the path to $20,000 requiring 4-6 optimized units plus potential consulting income.

### How does bonus depreciation create $60,000-$90,000 in tax refunds?

With 100% bonus depreciation available in 2025, furnishing and equipment costs for STR units can be fully deducted in year one. On a $150,000-$200,000 investment in furnishings across multiple units, this creates massive first-year deductions that generate substantial tax refunds. This requires proper documentation of placed-in-service dates and cost segregation studies for owned properties.

### What is the difference between IMST and MSTY for income generation?

IMST offers higher yield (~162%) with newer fund status and thinner liquidity, making it suitable for tax-advantaged accounts where trading is limited. MSTY provides slightly lower but more stable yields (~135-160%) with deeper liquidity and tighter spreads, making it better for larger positions where exit flexibility matters.

### Why is the S-Corp election important for this strategy?

When STR net profits exceed $60,000 annually, S-Corp election can save $2,000-$3,000+ in self-employment taxes by splitting income between salary (subject to FICA) and distributions (not subject to FICA). The election requires filing Form 2553 by March 15 for the target tax year.

### What makes Las Vegas an attractive STR market?

Las Vegas offers strong year-round demand from tourism, conventions, and events. However, regulations are tightening, requiring proper permitting and compliance. The market supports higher ADRs (Average Daily Rates) during peak seasons with strong occupancy potential, making it suitable for experienced operators who can navigate regulatory complexity.
