## Introduction: Building Wealth in the Bay Area

This comprehensive wealth plan from Morgan Hill, California demonstrates how to navigate high-cost, high-tax environments while building substantial real estate wealth. The strategy focuses on leveraging California's robust real estate appreciation potential while aggressively optimizing tax exposure in one of the nation's highest-tax jurisdictions.

### California-Specific Tax Considerations

**State Income Tax Impact:**
California's progressive income tax reaches 13.3% at higher income levels, making every deduction significantly more valuable than in no-tax states.

**Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET) Election:**
For multi-member LLCs, the PTET election allows the partnership to pay California tax at the entity level (deductible for federal purposes) rather than at the individual level. This bypasses the $10,000 SALT deduction cap and creates substantial federal tax savings.

**Real Estate Tax Benefits:**
- Property tax limited to 1% of assessed value plus local bonds (Prop 13)
- Depreciation deductions (residential: 27.5 years, commercial: 39 years)
- Cost segregation for accelerated depreciation on short-term rentals
- 1031 exchanges for tax-deferred property upgrades
- Step-up in basis at death for inherited properties

### California Capital Gains Considerations

California taxes capital gains as ordinary income at full rates—unlike federal preferential rates. This makes tax-deferral strategies particularly valuable:

**Primary Residence Exclusion:**
California follows federal rules allowing $250,000 ($500,000 married) exclusion on primary residence sales if you meet ownership and use tests. Planning move-in and move-out dates can maximize this benefit.

**Investment Property Strategies:**
- 1031 exchanges allow upgrading properties without immediate tax hit
- Opportunity Zone investments defer and potentially eliminate capital gains
- Installment sales spread gain recognition across multiple years
- Charitable remainder trusts provide income while avoiding immediate capital gains

**Depreciation Recapture Planning:**
When selling depreciated properties, accumulated depreciation is recaptured at 25% federal plus full California rates. Strategic timing of sales and use of 1031 exchanges can manage this liability.

## Core Investment Strategy Framework

### The California House Hack Strategy

House hacking—living in one unit while renting others—addresses California's high living costs while building equity:

**Strategy Mechanics:**
1. Purchase a duplex, triplex, or fourplex using FHA or conventional financing with low down payment (3.5-5%)
2. Live in one unit while renting the others
3. Rental income from other units covers most or all of the mortgage
4. Build equity through appreciation and principal paydown while living nearly rent-free
5. After one year, repeat the process with another property

**California-Specific Considerations:**
- Rent control: Morgan Hill currently has no rent control, but monitor local regulations
- House hacking math works best with 3-4 unit properties
- FHA loan limits in Santa Clara County: $1,149,825 (2024)
- Owner-occupancy requirements: Must live in property for at least one year

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Living expenses reduced by 50-100% through rental income
- Equity building through appreciation (historically strong in Bay Area)
- Loan paydown by tenants effectively saving for you
- Foundation for scaling to additional properties

### Short-Term Rental Arbitrage Opportunities

**Strategy Implementation:**

2. Furnish professionally for short-term rental appeal with local character
3. List on Airbnb, VRBO, and Booking.com with professional photography

**Tax Advantages of STR Arbitrage:**
- Material participation allows active loss treatment against other income
- 100% bonus depreciation on furnishings and equipment in year one
- Augusta Rule allows 14 days of tax-free rental income from your primary residence to the business
- Business mileage deductions for all property-related travel
- QBI deduction applies to profitable STR operations

## Tax Optimization for California Investors

### Multi-Member LLC Structure

**California LLC Requirements:**
- $800 annual franchise tax (minimum, regardless of income)
- $70 filing fee for Articles of Organization
- Statement of Information filed within 90 days, then every 2 years

**Despite the costs, LLC benefits justify the structure:**
- Liability protection separating personal and business assets
- Pass-through taxation avoiding entity-level federal tax
- Flexible management and ownership structures
- Professional credibility with lenders and partners

**Multi-Member Advantages:**
- PTET election eligibility (huge in California)
- Multiple retirement accounts (one per member)
- Income splitting among family members
- Succession and estate planning flexibility

### The Pass-Through Entity Tax (PTET) Election

**Why It Matters in California:**
The $10,000 SALT deduction cap significantly hurts California taxpayers. The PTET election effectively restores deductibility of California taxes.

**How It Works:**
1. Multi-member LLC elects to pay California income tax at the entity level
2. LLC deducts these payments on its federal partnership return
3. Members receive credit on their California returns for taxes paid by the LLC
4. Result: Full federal deduction of California taxes (bypassing $10k cap)

**Expected Value:**
For an LLC generating $100,000 in California-taxable income:
- California tax at ~9.3%: $9,300
- Federal tax savings from deduction (24% bracket): $2,232
- Net benefit: ~$2,200 annually

The value increases proportionally with income and California tax liability.

### Cost Segregation and Accelerated Depreciation

**For Short-Term Rental Properties:**
Cost segregation studies identify building components that can be depreciated over shorter periods (5, 7, or 15 years instead of 27.5 years).

**Typical Breakdown:**
- Personal property (furniture, appliances): 5-year depreciation
- Land improvements (landscaping, parking): 15-year depreciation
- Building structure: 27.5-year depreciation

**Impact:**
For a $100,000 property furnishing package:
- Standard depreciation (27.5 years): ~$3,600/year
- Cost segregation (5-year): $20,000/year first five years
- Additional first-year deduction: ~$16,400
- At 32% combined tax rate: $5,250 in tax savings

**Bonus Depreciation (2024):**
60% bonus depreciation allows immediate expensing of qualifying property in year one, further accelerating deductions.

## Building a California-Compliant Wealth System

### Regulatory Navigation

**Rent Control Awareness:**
While Morgan Hill currently has no rent control, California's statewide AB 1482 imposes annual rent increase caps (5% + CPI, max 10%) on many properties over 15 years old. New construction is exempt for 15 years.

**Just Cause Eviction Requirements:**
California requires specific reasons for evicting tenants who have occupied a property for over one year. Plan for longer holding periods and more careful tenant selection.

**Security Deposit Limits:**
California limits security deposits to two months' rent for unfurnished, three months for furnished properties. Return within 21 days of move-out with itemized deductions.
