Legacy Wealth Blueprint, $9,800

For you if you earn $100K to $200K or more, want to keep more of it, and are not ready to buy property or start a side business.

You get lifetime access to the 100+ video curriculum across five pillars: tax, debt and credit, income, investing, and protection. Then 12 months of live support:

  • Weekly live coaching calls, with replays and every past recording
  • A custom 12-month Wealth Strategy Snapshot built around your numbers
  • A review of last year's tax return, delivered in weeks 2 to 3
  • A CPA implementation brief around months 10 to 11, which you hand to your own CPA
  • A one-to-one Legacy Wealth Guide with an onboarding call and bi-weekly check-ins
  • The Legacy Wealth AI Advisor, trained on the full system
  • The Wealth Circle community for 12 months
  • The SOP, template, and checklist library
  • Partner network access, including a 45 to 60 minute private strategy session
  • A free second membership for a spouse or family member

LWB Course + AI, $1,500

For you if you earn under $75K. This is the version offered to applicants at that income level.

You get the video curriculum and the Legacy Wealth AI Advisor. There are no coaching calls, no custom plan, and no one-to-one guide.

Airbnb Ascension, $9,800, and Ascension Scale, $18,000

For you if you want cash flow from a side business next to your job, and you have $10,000 to $25,000 to put in. Ascension runs 6 months and covers one property. Scale runs 12 months with no property cap.

This is the arbitrage model. You sign a long-term lease, furnish the place, and rent it by the night. You do the market research and call the landlords, using our scripts and coaching. We handle the rest.

You get:

  • A dedicated one-to-one STR coach, with same-day access for questions
  • 7+ hours of arbitrage curriculum and lifetime community access
  • Weekly live Zoom calls plus 18+ months of recordings
  • Property profitability reports, so deals get analysed before you sign
  • The Market Matrix, with the top 500 markets ranked
  • The STR AI Advisor
  • Done for you listing design, furnishing, operations setup, messaging automation, and pricing setup
  • Weekly progress reports against a 6-month plan
  • A 45 to 60 minute session on entity setup, business credit, and tax
  • Landlord scripts, sublease templates, furniture lists, and deal spreadsheets

The capital you need is separate from the program fee. Budget for the deposit, the first month's rent, and furniture.

STR Concierge, $16,000, and Concierge Portfolio, $30,000

For you if you earn $150K or more, have $120,000 or more in capital, want to buy within about four months, and would rather pay for the work than learn it. Concierge runs 6 to 7 months for one property. Portfolio runs 12 months with no property cap. Concierge is capped at 20 spots because of the done for you work.

You get:

  • We source and underwrite 5 to 10 deals for you
  • A full underwriting package per property: revenue projection, expenses, cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash, and a cost segregation estimate
  • Short-term rental rule checks, neighbourhood scoring, and offer negotiation support
  • Due diligence, appraisal, title, and closing coordination
  • Entity and tax structure set up with CPAs, and cost segregation coordinated
  • Listings live on Airbnb, VRBO, and Booking.com within 2 to 3 weeks of closing
  • A direct booking website
  • Automated guest messaging and the full operations playbook
  • A vetted referral network: DSCR lenders, cost segregation firms, STR CPAs, agents, designers, and property managers
  • 30-day and 90-day performance reviews
  • A free second membership for a spouse or family member

The $120,000 is your own capital for the down payment, furniture, and the program fee. It is not included in the price above.

Financing and refunds

Financing costs more

The Legacy Wealth Blueprint is $9,800 paid by cash or card. Through Splitit financing it is $10,800. That $1,000 is the cost of spreading the payments. Ask for the total you will pay, in writing, before you agree to anything.

Payment plans are available on the other programs too. Promotions run at some points in the year, so a price you are quoted may be lower than the list price above. Get any quote in writing and take it away with you. Nothing here needs to be decided on one call.

Refunds

Refund terms are in the enrollment agreement you sign before payment.

Tax treatment

The fee may qualify as a deductible business expense in some situations. It depends on your own facts, so ask your CPA before you count on it.

Pricing questions

What does Legacy Investing Show cost?

Programs run from $1,500 to $30,000. The Legacy Wealth Blueprint is $9,800. LWB Course + AI is $1,500. Airbnb Ascension is $9,800 and Airbnb Ascension Scale is $18,000. STR Concierge is $16,000 and STR Concierge Portfolio is $30,000. Bundles run from $17,000 to $34,000.

Is there financing or a payment plan?

Yes. Payment plans are available. Financing costs more than paying outright. The Legacy Wealth Blueprint is $9,800 paid by cash or card and $10,800 through Splitit financing. Ask for the total amount you will pay, in writing, before you agree to anything.

What is included in the Legacy Wealth Blueprint?

Lifetime access to a 100+ video curriculum across five pillars, 12 months in the Wealth Circle community, weekly live coaching calls with replays, a custom 12-month Wealth Strategy Snapshot, a prior year tax return diagnostic, a CPA implementation brief, a one-to-one Legacy Wealth Guide with bi-weekly check-ins, the Legacy Wealth AI Advisor, the SOP and template library, partner network access, and a free second membership for a spouse or family member.

What is the refund policy?

Refund terms are in the enrollment agreement you sign before payment.

Who is each program for?

The Legacy Wealth Blueprint is for people earning $100K to $200K or more who want to optimise the income they already have. Airbnb Ascension is for people who want side income from rental arbitrage and have $10K to $25K to deploy. STR Concierge is for people earning $150K or more with $120K or more in capital who want the buying done for them.

Is there a cheaper option?

Yes. LWB Course + AI is $1,500 and is offered to applicants earning under $75K. It is the video curriculum plus the Legacy Wealth AI Advisor, with no coaching calls. The blog, the tax strategy guides, and the calculators on this site are free.

Are the program fees tax deductible?

The fee may qualify as a deductible business expense in some situations. Whether it does depends on your own facts, so ask your CPA before you count on it. This page is education, not tax advice.
