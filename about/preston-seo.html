Preston Seo

Founder of Legacy Investing Show. He teaches tax strategy, investment planning, business structures, and retirement accounts.

Preston started Legacy Investing Show in 2020, after paying off $80,000 of his own debt. He and his wife built a $15M+ real estate portfolio along the way. He now teaches the same decisions to professionals, investors, and founders who earn good money and want to keep more of it.

How he got here

  1. 2017

    $80,000 in debt

    Employed full time, with no system for taxes, cash flow, or investing.

  2. 2018

    First experiments

    Income, ownership structures, tax moves, and real estate. Some worked. Others were expensive lessons.

  3. 2019

    Debt cleared

    The $80,000 was paid off.

  4. 2020

    Legacy Investing Show founded

    Built to teach the frameworks rather than single tactics.

  5. 2024

    Past 2,000 students

    Guided through the same frameworks, one decision at a time.

What he teaches

Tax strategy
Which deductions, structures, and timing decisions apply to a situation, and in what order.
Investment planning
How to choose between assets on the numbers rather than on the story attached to them.
Business structures
Entity choice, ownership, and how the structure changes what you owe.
Retirement accounts
Solo 401(k)s, IRAs, conversions, and the trade-offs between them.
Debt management
Paying down debt as one option among several, compared against investing the same dollar.
Alternative income
Airbnb arbitrage and other income streams that do not depend on a salary.
Worth knowing

Airbnb arbitrage is about 20% of the work, and it is the part people know him for. The other 80% is tax strategy, investment planning, structures, and retirement.

Students typically earn $75K+. Most are high earners paying more tax than they expected, business owners deciding how to pay themselves, real estate investors sizing up depreciation and entity choices, or professionals building a first wealth system.
