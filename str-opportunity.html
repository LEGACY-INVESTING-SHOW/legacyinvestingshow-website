Frequently asked questions

I have a full-time job. Will I have time for this?
We built these services for people who already have a job and do not have time to do all the work themselves. With STR Concierge, our team handles most of the setup while you choose the property and make the key decisions. With Airbnb Ascension, you make the landlord calls and we work through the setup with you. We explain the time and work involved before you join.
I already paid for Legacy Wealth Blueprint. What would I be paying for here?
Legacy Wealth Blueprint is a broad personal finance program. These separate paid services focus on building a short-term rental business, either by buying a property or leasing one from an owner. You are paying for done-for-you services and consulting to help put that business in place. We explain what each program includes and what it costs on the call.
Do I need to own a property to get started?
No. You can lease a property with the owner's permission, furnish it, and rent it to short-term guests. Airbnb Ascension helps you with that process. If you want to buy a property, STR Concierge supports the purchase and setup.
