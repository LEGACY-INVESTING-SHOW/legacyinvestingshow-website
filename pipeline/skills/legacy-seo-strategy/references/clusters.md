# Clusters

Ship new pages only inside these clusters. Anything else is refuse or a human-named exception.

- Tax strategy (cost segregation, Augusta, bonus depreciation, QBI, 1031, STR)
- Entity choice (S-corp, LLC, payroll, reasonable salary)
- Retirement (solo 401k, backdoor Roth, HSA)
- Insurance and protection (renters, auto, disability) when a calculator already exists
- Airbnb operations only as a supporting cluster, not the brand
- Wealth systems / high-earner cash-flow decisions

Branded queries (`preston seo`, `legacy investing show`) belong to `/` or `/blog/preston-seo-review`.
