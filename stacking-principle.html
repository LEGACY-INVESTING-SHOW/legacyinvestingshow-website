The Short-Term Rental Loophole

Here's where it gets really interesting—especially if you have W-2 income.

The Best Tax Strategy for W-2 Earners

The STR loophole allows you to deduct losses from your short-term rental against your ordinary W-2 income—something you normally can't do with passive real estate investments.

📉
Cost Segregation
Accelerate depreciation on building components
🏗️
Bonus Depreciation
Write off 60-80% of property value in year one
💰
Cash Flow

📈

With the STR loophole, you can generate $30K-50K in tax deductions while also generating $20K-40K in cash flow—all from a single property.
