What is Financial Stacking?

Instead of one strategy, you layer complementary strategies on top of each other.

Each layer doesn't just add — it multiplies the impact of the others.

💰 Optimize taxes on your current income
📉 Pay off debt in the right order (save on interest)
📈 Invest savings in tax-free growth accounts
🏠 Generate tax-free cash flow (e.g., Airbnb)

Step 1: Optimize What You Have

Before chasing new income, maximize what you're already earning.

  • Restructure your tax situation
  • Optimize your investment portfolio structure
  • Fix the wellness/benefits structure
  • Ensure you're not leaking money on inefficiencies

This alone can save or create tens of thousands per year.

Step 2: The Cash Flow Layer

Once your foundation is optimized, add Airbnb as a cash flow engine.

Two goals working together:

  • More income — Monthly cash flow from short-term rentals
  • Tax advantages — Real estate's biggest loophole: depreciation, deductions, 1031 exchanges

This isn't just extra money — it's strategically structured money.

Two Paths, Same Destination
