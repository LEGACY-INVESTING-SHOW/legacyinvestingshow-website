What is Financial Stacking?

Instead of relying on one strategy, you layer complementary strategies on top of each other. Each layer doesn't just add — it multiplies the impact of the others.

💰 Optimize taxes on your current income
📉 Pay off debt in the right order (save on interest)
📈 Invest savings in tax-free growth accounts
🏠 Generate tax-free cash flow with Airbnb

Step 1: Optimize What You Have

Before chasing new income, you must maximize what you're already earning. Most people are leaking thousands without realizing it.

  • Restructure your tax situation for maximum savings
  • Optimize your investment portfolio structure
  • Fix your wellness and benefits structure
  • Eliminate hidden inefficiencies draining your wealth

This foundational step alone can save or create tens of thousands per year.

Step 2: The Cash Flow Layer

Once your foundation is optimized, add Airbnb as a cash flow engine. This serves two strategic purposes simultaneously:

  • Immediate Income — Monthly cash flow from short-term rentals
  • Tax Advantages — Depreciation, deductions, 1031 exchanges

This isn't just "extra money" — it's strategically structured money that compounds your wealth building.
