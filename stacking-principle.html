Phase 2

Add the Cash Flow Engine

Once your foundation is optimized, add Airbnb as a wealth accelerator. This isn't "side income" — it's a strategic cash flow layer.

Airbnb is uniquely powerful because it delivers two things simultaneously: immediate cash flow you can use today, and tax advantages that make the IRS work for you.

  • ✓ Monthly Cash Flow — $2,000-$10,000+ per property in net income, deposited to your account.
  • ✓ Depreciation — Write off the property value against your income. Massive tax savings.
  • ✓ Expense Deductions — Everything from furniture to cleaning supplies reduces taxable income.
  • ✓ 1031 Exchanges — Defer capital gains indefinitely as you scale.
