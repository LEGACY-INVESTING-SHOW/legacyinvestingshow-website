WealthAbility and Tax-Free Wealth alternatives

A CPA class teaches the map. It does not sign your return.

WealthAbility is Tom Wheelwright's tax education company. He also wrote Tax-Free Wealth, the book many Rich Dad readers buy next. WealthAbility Live listed $197 a month on September 15, 2026. The Tax-Free Formula sales page we opened that day did not print a checkout price. If you want a class, that can be enough. If you want a signed return, hire a CPA. If you want documents and attorneys on tap, that is a membership like Anderson Platinum, not a video. We sell Legacy Wealth Blueprint. It is a written plan plus coaching. We do not file your return.

How we chose

We grouped options by job: class, licensed filer, attorney membership, or a written wealth plan. We skipped lookalikes whose only door was a webinar with no public price.

  • The option teaches tax, files tax, or sells legal support we could describe from a live page.
  • Price is copied from a public page, or we say it was not printed.
  • We do not reuse Wheelwright's marketing averages as our facts.
  • We include our own program and say so.

What WealthAbility sells

WealthAbility Live is a monthly subscription. The page we opened listed $197 a month, two live sessions a month, a forum, and archives. It is hosted by Tom Wheelwright, CPA. Treat the lesson list as seller copy. We did not sit in the sessions.

The Tax-Free Formula page describes a digital course with modules on deductions, conversion, brackets, credits, and deferral. On September 15, 2026 that page did not print a checkout price in the text we could copy. If you saw $497 on an older screenshot, confirm the live offer. We will not invent it.

Tax-Free Wealth is the book. A book is not a filing position. Mentorship and done-for-you pages we opened did not publish a price. If someone quotes a five-figure WealthAbility coaching fee, ask for the live invoice.

Pick the next tool by the next job

These are not 'better WealthAbility.' They do different work.

  • Stay with WealthAbility Live

    Use it if you already own a business or property and you want CPA-led class time at a printed monthly price. Skip it if you only have wages and no rental. A class cannot invent a deduction you do not have. See tax strategies for W-2 employees.

    Job: tax school with a published $197 a month sticker

  • A CPA or enrolled agent

    A certified public accountant or an enrolled agent (EA) can prepare and sign Form 1040. An EA is a tax pro licensed by the IRS to represent taxpayers. A course cannot. See tax course vs hiring a CPA.

    Job: the actual tax return

  • Anderson Platinum

    Attorney and document membership, not a video class. Public price on September 15, 2026: $3,495 to enroll plus $75 a month. Their FAQ said tax filing packages are not included. Full split: Anderson Platinum vs a tax course.

    Job: legal and entity questions on tap

  • Named tax programs we already compared

    If you want a vendor table with other public class prices, use best tax strategy programs for W-2 earners over $250k. This page will not clone that table.

    Job: shop tax classes by published price

  • Legacy Wealth Blueprint

    Our 12-month plan for tax, debt, income, investing, and protection. We publish $9,800 cash or card. We still tell you to keep a CPA. Cost briefing: Legacy Wealth Blueprint cost.

    Job: one written plan plus coaching. We sell this.

W-2 limit in one line

After the 401(k) and HSA, most large tax ideas need a business or a rental. A $197 class that talks about entities will not lower a pure wage bill by itself. Fill the IRS buckets first. 2026 employee 401(k) deferral is $24,500 (IRS IR-2025-111).

If you only read Tax-Free Wealth

The book is a companion to Rich Dad, not a substitute for a preparer. Next mechanical steps: Rich Dad alternatives if you still need a how-to, or a CPA meeting with last year's return in hand.

If a sales page promises you will cut tax by a round percent with no look at your file, walk. The FTC coaching page flags easy-profit pitches. We do not call WealthAbility a scam. We also do not repeat their student stories as our proof.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • Will this purchase file my return, or only teach me?
  • Do I already have a business or a rental, or only wages?
  • Is the live Formula checkout printed, or am I guessing from an old screenshot?
  • If I keep my CPA, what artifact do I hand them?
  • Have I already maxed the 401(k) and HSA the IRS already allows?
