W-2 limit in one line

After the 401(k) and HSA, most large tax ideas need a business or a rental. A $197 class that talks about entities will not lower a pure wage bill by itself. Fill the IRS buckets first. 2026 employee 401(k) deferral is $24,500 (IRS IR-2025-111).
