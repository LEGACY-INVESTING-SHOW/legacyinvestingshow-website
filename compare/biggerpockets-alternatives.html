BiggerPockets vs our wealth plan

Forums teach deals. Our plan writes 12 months from your tax file.

BiggerPockets is a real estate site with free forums, free podcasts, and a paid Pro membership. It helps you study deals. It is not a full money plan. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is what Preston Seo teaches after a corporate job, a house-hacked duplex, and a $15M+ portfolio with his wife: a written 12-month order for tax, debt, income, investing, and protection, plus a guide and a CPA brief. Stay on BiggerPockets if the job is listings. Use us if the job is one written order. Cheap rental memberships exist if you need neither. We do not publish our tuition here.

The portfolio is the result. The system is Legacy Wealth Blueprint: a Wealth Strategy Snapshot from your numbers, a dedicated guide, weekly coaching, and a CPA Implementation Brief you hand to your own preparer. You keep your CPA. You do not have to buy a rental on day one.

What BiggerPockets is

BiggerPockets is a real estate media and community company. You can read forums, listen to podcasts, and use deal tools. A staff post on March 3, 2026 said Pro costs $32.50 a month when billed once a year. A membership comparison page also lists Pro at $39 a month, or $32.50 a month billed yearly.

The bootcamps URL we opened on September 15, 2026 showed free guides and a prompt to upgrade to Pro. It did not show a live checkout price. An older membership table lists online bootcamps at $199 each. Confirm the current class on their site.

You do not need us if the free forums already answer your questions. You need a different product when you want a written plan for tax, debt, and investing, not just rentals.

If you need neither product

These are cheaper doors, not a bake-off between other brands.

  • Stay free: forums and podcasts

    Keep using BiggerPockets forums plus the BiggerPockets Real Estate Podcast. Reddit threads are free too, but quality varies.

    Job: learn the basics at $0

  • KenPro (Ken McElroy)

    A paid membership around rental and apartment education. Ken's store page lists Ken Pro at $39.99 a month or $399 a year. Older pages still show $29.99. Use the live store price.

    Job: cheap paid lessons without a high-ticket call

  • A fee-only planner or CPA

    If you need advice on your file, buy hours from a licensed person, not a forum. The FTC warns that coaching programs which promise easy real estate profits are often a scam.

    Job: advice on your numbers. Fee varies.

Watch for this

Many 'BiggerPockets alternatives' articles rank software: PropStream, DealMachine, Roofstock. Those help you find a property. They do not replace education, and they are not our wealth plan. If a page ranks 11 tools and never says who wrote it, treat it as a lead list.

Questions people ask

Is there a free BiggerPockets alternative?

Yes. The free BiggerPockets forums and podcasts are the first alternative to paying. Paid options start when you want structure, a calculator pack, or a written wealth plan.

How much is BiggerPockets Pro?

A March 3, 2026 BiggerPockets staff post said $32.50 a month billed yearly. A membership table also shows $39 a month. Check biggerpockets.com before you buy.

Is Legacy Wealth Blueprint a BiggerPockets alternative?

Only if your job is a full money plan. It is not a deal marketplace or a landlord forum. Read what's in Blueprint.

Should I buy PropStream instead?

PropStream is a data tool, not a class. Buy data when you are already looking at markets. Do not buy it to learn what a rental is.
