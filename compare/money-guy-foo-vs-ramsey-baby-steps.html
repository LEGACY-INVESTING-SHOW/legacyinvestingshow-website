FOO or Ramsey vs a written wealth plan

Those maps tell the next dollar where to go. Our plan writes 12 months from your return.

Dave Ramsey's Baby Steps and the Money Guy Financial Order of Operations, or FOO, are free maps for the next dollar. Ramsey pauses extra investing until consumer debt is gone. FOO takes the employer 401(k) match earlier. Both are useful. Neither is a 12-month wealth plan. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is what Preston Seo built after a corporate job, a house-hacked duplex, and a $15M+ portfolio with his wife: one written order for tax, debt, income, investing, and protection, plus a coach and a CPA brief. Use Ramsey or FOO if cards are the fire. Use us when the leftover leak is a high W-2 bill and a full file.

What those two maps actually are

The FOO guide we opened was marked last updated July 29, 2026. The nine steps are: deductibles, employer match, high-interest debt, emergency fund, Roth IRA and HSA, max employer plans, hyperaccumulation, prepay future expenses, then prepay low-interest debt. An HSA is a health savings account you can use with a high-deductible health plan.

The Baby Steps page lists: $1,000 starter fund; pay off all debt except the house with the debt snowball; three to six months of expenses; invest 15 percent for retirement; kids' college; pay the home off early; then build wealth and give. A debt snowball means you list debts from smallest balance to largest.

Financial Peace University is the nine-week class that teaches Ramsey's order. The class page said $99.99 per household, spouse included, on September 15, 2026. We are not ranking FPU against the FOO course. We are saying both are maps, and Blueprint is a plan.

If you already earn a lot

A high W-2 wage does not cancel 22 percent credit cards. If those are still on, stay with Ramsey or FOO until the expensive debt is gone. Do not buy a wealth plan to skip that work.

If cards are gone and the leftover fight is a cheap mortgage versus a 401(k), that is pay off the mortgage vs invest, not a guru fight. If the leftover fight is tax on wages, that is W-2 tax strategies and, if you want a written order, what's in Blueprint.

How to read seller claims

Ramsey Solutions advertises average class results, such as debt paid in 90 days. Those are their marketing lines. We did not audit them. Money Guy's learn page called $49 a sale from $249. Confirm the live checkout. We do not promise any plan will make you rich.

Questions people ask

Should a high earner follow Ramsey or FOO?

Follow Ramsey's pause if lifestyle debt is still the fire. Take the 401(k) match either way. After cards are gone, neither map replaces a tax pro or a written wealth plan.

Is Blueprint a Ramsey alternative?

Only after expensive debt is gone. Blueprint is a 12-month plan for tax, debt, and investing together. It is not a $99.99 spending class.

Is this the same as pay off the mortgage vs invest?

No. That scorecard is mortgage math. This page is a cheap dollar-order map vs our written plan.
