BiggerPockets or Rich Dad vs a written wealth plan

A forum and a book are starting tools. Our plan is a 12-month order for your file.

BiggerPockets is a real estate community with free podcasts and a paid Pro plan for tools. Rich Dad is a brand around Robert Kiyosaki's book. Both can start you. Neither writes a 12-month plan from your tax return. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is the plan Preston Seo teaches: tax, debt, income, investing, and protection in one order, plus a coach and a CPA brief. He built that after a corporate job, a house-hacked duplex, and a $15M+ portfolio with his wife. Use the book or the forum first. Use us when you want one written order, not another story.

How to pick this month

If you still believe your salary is the only asset you will ever have, read Rich Dad Poor Dad once. Then stop. Do not buy a stack of sequels until you can explain one local rental in numbers.

If you can already explain cap rate in a sentence (yearly net rent divided by price), use free BiggerPockets content or our rental cash flow tool. Pay for Pro only when a calculator or form is in your way every week. See what to know before Pro.

If the leak is a W-2 tax bill, debt, and investing at once, that is our lane. Read what's in Blueprint.

We sell the third job

We are not a BiggerPockets killer. We are not a Rich Dad sequel. If you only need deal math, stay free. If you want Preston's 12-month order, that is Blueprint.

Questions people ask

Should I pick BiggerPockets or Rich Dad?

Use the book once if you need a mindset jolt. Use BiggerPockets for models and math. That choice is not the same as choosing a wealth plan.

Is Blueprint a BiggerPockets alternative?

Only if your next job is tax, debt, and investing in one order. It will not replace forums for underwriting listings.

Does any of this file my taxes?

No. See tax course vs a CPA. Blueprint still sends you back to your CPA.
