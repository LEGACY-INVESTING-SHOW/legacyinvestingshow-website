BiggerPockets vs Rich Dad

One is a toolbox. One is a story. Buy the one that matches the week you are in.

BiggerPockets is a real estate community with free podcasts and a paid Pro plan for tools. Rich Dad is a brand around Robert Kiyosaki's book and later classes. Use Rich Dad when you still think a paycheck is the only path. Use BiggerPockets when you are ready to study a real listing. Most people need the book once, then a how-to. You rarely need to pay both at once.

By Preston Seo, founder of Legacy Investing Show. Updated September 15, 2026. First published September 15, 2026.

How to pick this month

If you still believe your salary is the only asset you will ever have, read the book. Then stop. Do not buy a stack of Rich Dad sequels until you can explain one local rental in numbers.

If you can already explain cap rate in a sentence (that is yearly net rent divided by price), skip the parable and go to BiggerPockets or a calculator. Try our rental cash flow tool.

If your problem is a W-2 tax bill, neither brand is the center. Use tax strategies for high-income earners.

We sell a third thing

Legacy Wealth Blueprint is not a BiggerPockets killer and not a Rich Dad sequel. It is a written plan plus a year of coaching. Use it only if you want tax, debt, and investing in one order. See Legacy Wealth Blueprint.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • Am I still stuck on mindset, or do I need a spreadsheet?
  • Have I used the free layer of both brands?
  • If a sales call starts, what is the full price in writing?

Questions people ask

Which one is better for beginners?

The book is the easier first night. BiggerPockets is the better second month. Paying either brand before you use the free layer is usually a waste.

Can I use both?

Yes. Read the book once. Then use BiggerPockets for models and math. You do not need two paid memberships to do that.

Does either one file my taxes?

No. See tax course vs a CPA.
