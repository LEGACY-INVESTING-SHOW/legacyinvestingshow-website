QCD First vs Roth Conversion First for Retirees Near IRMAA Cliffs

A sequence-aware retirement tax comparison focused on Medicare surcharge thresholds and giving goals.

Quick Verdict: Depends on bracket room and IRMAA headroom. For retirees near IRMAA thresholds, sequence can matter more than strategy selection itself. The same two actions can produce different Medicare premium outcomes depending on order and timing.

When QCD First Usually Wins

Best when managing MAGI exposure is primary and charitable intent is already committed.

When Roth Conversion First Usually Wins

Best when conversion window is narrow and long-term tax-rate risk is primary concern.

Most Common Mistake

Optimizing for the biggest deduction headline instead of the most reliable after-tax outcome for your actual facts.

Worked Example (Scenario Model)

Profile: Retired couple near IRMAA threshold with planned annual charitable giving and IRA assets.

  • Charitable intent is fixed regardless of sequence
  • Conversion amount can be staged
  • IRMAA threshold proximity is within narrow range

QCD First Outcome

QCD-first sequence preserves MAGI headroom and avoids surcharge in base scenario.

Roth Conversion First Outcome

Conversion-first sequence increases long-term Roth assets but can trigger higher Medicare premiums in near-threshold year.

Scenario takeaway: Sequence should be tested with 2-3 year horizon models, not a single-year tax view.

Edge Cases That Change the Decision

  • Unexpected capital gains push MAGI above cliff after conversion.
  • Giving schedule changes late year and breaks planning assumptions.
  • RMD timing and custodian processing create sequencing errors.
  • One-time income event invalidates planned bracket room.

When Not to Use This Strategy

Avoid QCD First If...

  • Charitable giving is uncertain and may not occur.
  • Conversion window is unusually temporary and should be captured first.
  • Your MAGI headroom is already large and IRMAA risk is low.

Avoid Roth Conversion First If...

  • You are near a surcharge threshold with little buffer.
  • Medicare premium increase would materially strain cash flow.
  • You lack a sequence monitoring process during the year.

30-Day Implementation Checklist

  • Map current MAGI headroom to next IRMAA tier.
  • Set conversion range with cliff guardrails.
  • Lock charitable timing and custodian workflow early.
  • Run mid-year and Q4 sequence recalculations.
  • Document final sequence rationale and assumptions.

Questions to Ask Your CPA/Advisor

  • What conversion range keeps us below the next IRMAA tier?
  • How should QCD timing integrate with our RMD workflow?
  • What non-IRA income risks could break our sequence plan?
  • How should we adjust sequence if markets decline sharply?

Frequently Asked Questions

Should retirees always do QCD first?

Not always. It is often compelling near IRMAA cliffs, but sequence depends on conversion goals and MAGI headroom.

Can conversion-first still be right near a cliff?

Yes, if long-term strategy warrants it and you can absorb potential premium impact.

Is one-year optimization enough?

Usually no. A multi-year sequence view is more reliable.
