Real estate coaching vs a self-paced course

Buy a course for a map. Buy coaching when a real decision is costing you money. Our plan is both, for 12 months.

A course is recorded lessons you play on your own. Coaching is a person who answers you and, if it is real, looks at your deal. Most busy people should buy a course, or use free podcasts, until they have a listing or a lease in front of them. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is Preston Seo's 12-month wealth plan: curriculum plus a dedicated guide and weekly calls, built from a corporate job, a house-hacked duplex, and a $15M+ portfolio with his wife. It is coaching plus a written order for tax, debt, and investing, not a deal-finding concierge. We do not publish our tuition here. If a seller will not say their price until a call, assume it is high. The FTC warns that real estate coaching pitches often use fake wins and rush.

What the FTC says to watch

The FTC page on coaching scams says promoters often start with a free event, talk up student wins, claim a proven system, and rush the buy. It also says to be ready for the upsell. That pattern is common in real estate seminars. It does not mean every coach is a scam. It means you should slow down.

A second FTC note, How to spot business coaching scams (dated May 18, 2026 on the page we opened), repeats the same idea: guaranteed income and 'proven systems' are warning signs.

A simple decision order

  1. Use free podcasts for two weeks. If you cannot name a model, do not buy coaching.

  2. If you want structure, buy a course with a published price and a refund window.

  3. Buy coaching only when a live listing, lease, or partner dispute is on the table, or when you want a 12-month written wealth order.

  4. Get the total price, refund rules, and who your coach is, in writing, before a card is charged.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • Is there a live deal, or am I buying motivation?
  • Do I need deal coaching, or a 12-month wealth order?
  • Who is the coach by name, and how many hours do I get?
  • What is the refund rule if they go quiet?
  • Will I still need a local attorney after I pay you?

Questions people ask

Is coaching worth it for a first rental?

Only if you have a live listing and you need a second set of eyes this month. For the first 20 hours of learning, a course or free podcast is enough.

Why do coaches hide the price?

High-ticket sales often use a call. That can be honest. It can also hide the number until you are tired. Ask for the total in the first ten minutes. We do the same: confirm Blueprint on a strategy call.
