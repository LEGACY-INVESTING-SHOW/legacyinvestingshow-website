What to know before you join BiggerPockets Pro

Start free. Pay for Pro when the calculators or forms would save you hours.

This is not a shopper review. We did not join Pro for this page. BiggerPockets is a real estate media site. Forums and podcasts are free. Pro is the paid tier for more calculator use, forms, and extra content. A March 3, 2026 staff post said Pro is $32.50 a month billed yearly. A membership table also lists $39 a month. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is Preston Seo's 12-month wealth plan: a written order for tax, debt, and investing, plus a guide and a CPA brief, built after a corporate job, a house-hacked duplex, and a $15M+ portfolio with his wife. Use Pro if you are already looking at deals. Use us if you want one written order. Skip both if you have not picked a model yet. We do not publish our tuition here.

Free vs Pro

Free: forums, many articles, and the main podcasts. That is enough to learn BRRRR (buy, rehab, rent, refinance, repeat), house hacking, and why a deal dies.

Pro: a membership comparison page lists extra calculator reports, state lease packages, webinar replays, and bootcamps. The same table shows some reports capped on cheaper tiers. We could not re-check a live logged-in dashboard, so treat feature lists as 'what they advertise,' then confirm at checkout.

The bootcamps URL on September 15, 2026 was a free resource library plus a Pro upgrade prompt. If you were quoted $199 for a bootcamp, that figure appears on an older membership table. Ask them to show the live offer.

Who should pay, who should wait

Pay when you are underwriting real listings most weeks and you want their calculators or forms in one login. Wait if you are still choosing between a rental, a short-term rental, and doing nothing. That choice is a short-term vs long-term rental problem, not a membership problem.

Pro will not build you a tax plan. If that is the pain, that is our lane. Read what's in Blueprint or tax strategies for real estate investors.

A 15-minute checklist

  • I can name the model I want (long-term, short-term, flip, or none).
  • I have used the free forums or podcasts for at least two weeks.
  • I know whether I need calculators, forms, or a class.
  • I have written down the monthly and yearly Pro prices from their live page, not from memory.
  • I am not paying with high-interest debt.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • What exact feature am I paying for in month one?
  • Is there a trial, and what is the cancel path?
  • Do I need state lease forms, or will an attorney do that cheaper on one property?
  • Will I still need Pro if I only buy one house this year?

Questions people ask

Is BiggerPockets legit?

It is a long-running real estate media company with a large public forum. That is not a promise that every post is good advice. Treat forum tips as leads, then check numbers and local law.

Do I need Pro to start?

No. Start free. Pay when a listed Pro feature is in your way every week.

Is this a BiggerPockets review?
