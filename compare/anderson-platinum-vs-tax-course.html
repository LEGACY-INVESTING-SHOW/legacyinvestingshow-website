Anderson Platinum vs a tax course

One is attorney access. The other is lessons. Tax filing is a third bill.

Anderson Platinum is a legal, tax, and asset protection membership. Their Platinum page listed $3,495 to enroll plus $75 a month on September 15, 2026. It sells attorney access, document review, and a Q and A room. Their own FAQ said tax filing packages are not included. A tax course sells lessons. It does not replace an attorney, and it does not sign your return. Use Platinum when you already have entities and constant legal questions. Use a course when you need vocabulary. Use a CPA when you need a signed Form 1040.

What Anderson Platinum is

The Platinum membership page we opened on September 15, 2026 listed $3,495 enrollment plus $75 a month. It advertises unlimited strategy sessions, attorney-reviewed documents (they say up to two contracts a month on that page), a template library, and a live Q and A room. Treat feature lists as seller copy. We are not members for this briefing.

Their FAQ on that same page said tax packages are not included in Platinum. You can still ask tax questions in office hours. Filing is a separate product. That is the line people miss.

What a tax course is

A tax course is lessons. Cheap public examples: WealthAbility Live at $197 a month. Our own Blueprint at $9,800 is a written plan plus coaching, still education. We do not file. See Legacy Wealth Blueprint cost.

If you want a named list of other classes, use best tax strategy programs for W-2 earners over $250k and TaxFreeCEO alternatives. Those posts already include Platinum as a row. This URL will not paste that table again.

Three bills, not one

People buy Platinum and think the return is filed. People buy a class and think an attorney will review the lease. Budget the membership, the class, and the preparer as separate lines, then drop the ones you do not need.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • Do I need an attorney this quarter, or do I need vocabulary?
  • Who will sign Form 1040, and is that fee extra?
  • How many entities and contracts do I actually have?
  • If I buy Platinum, what tax package is still extra?
  • Am I duplicating a class I already own?

Questions people ask

Does Anderson Platinum include tax filing?

The Platinum page FAQ we opened said tax packages are not included. Confirm on their live page before you pay.

Is Platinum cheaper than a $9,800 plan?

Year one at their printed numbers is $3,495 plus twelve times $75, which is $4,395 if the monthly fee runs the whole year. That is still a different product from a wealth plan. Cheaper is not the same job.

Can a tax course replace Platinum?

Not for contract review or attorney access. A course can replace Platinum if you only needed lessons.
