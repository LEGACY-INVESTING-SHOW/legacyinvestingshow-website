What Anderson Platinum is

The Platinum membership page we opened on September 15, 2026 listed $3,495 enrollment plus $75 a month. It advertises unlimited strategy sessions, attorney-reviewed documents, a template library, and a live Q and A room. Treat feature lists as seller copy. We are not members for this briefing.

Their FAQ on that same page said tax packages are not included in Platinum. You can still ask tax questions in office hours. Filing is a separate product.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • Do I need an attorney this quarter, or a written money order?
  • Who will sign Form 1040, and is that fee extra?
  • How many entities and contracts do I actually have?
  • Am I duplicating a class I already own?
