Start with accounts, not a guru

For 2026 the IRS set the employee 401(k) deferral at $24,500. Age 50 catch-up is $8,000. Ages 60 to 63 can use $11,250 instead. Source: IRS IR-2025-111.

For 2026 HSA limits are $4,400 self-only and $8,750 family. Source: Rev. Proc. 2025-19. An HSA is a health savings account you can use with a high-deductible health plan.

If those are empty, a high-ticket class is the wrong first buy. Fill the cheap, legal buckets. Then pick education for what is left.

What we left out, on purpose

We left out brands whose only public door is a webinar funnel with no curriculum list we could verify. SubTo and some masterminds may be real. Their model is narrow. They belong on a creative-finance list, not a 'best wealth education' list for a physician or engineer.

We also left out Dave Ramsey as a 'best for high earners' crown. FPU at $99.99 can still be the right class if lifestyle debt is the leak. It is not the best tax school. See Dave Ramsey vs our wealth plan.

FTC bar

The FTC says a coaching pitch that promises easy real estate or business profits is a warning sign. Anyone who guarantees a return is selling a story. Walk.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • Which leak is largest this year: tax, debt, or idle cash?
  • Have I maxed the 401(k) and HSA I already have?
  • Does this teacher publish a price before a call?
  • Will a CPA still have to file, and did I budget for that fee too?

Questions people ask

What is the best wealth program for high earners?

There is no single best. Fill IRS accounts first. Then buy the class that matches the leftover leak. We sell Blueprint as a written 12-month plan. It is not the right buy if you still have unpaid credit cards or empty 401(k) space.

Is a cheap monthly tax class better than a written wealth plan?

They are different products. A cheap class is content. A plan with a coach is time and a written order. Compare hours, access, and whether anyone looks at your return. See our Karlton Dennis vs Mark Kohler vs Blueprint post for three named vendors.

Can education replace a 401(k) match?

No. A match is part of pay. Do not drop it to afford a course.
