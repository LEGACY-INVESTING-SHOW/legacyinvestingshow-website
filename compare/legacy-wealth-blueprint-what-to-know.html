What's in Legacy Wealth Blueprint

A 12-month written plan for the income you already earn. Tuition stays off this page.

Who built this

The portfolio is the result. The system underneath it is the thing he teaches: tax, debt, income, investing, and protection in one order, not one tactic.

What the 12 months include

From the Blueprint page: a custom Wealth Strategy Snapshot, a dedicated Legacy Wealth Guide with an onboarding call and check-ins, weekly live coaching with replays, the Wealth Circle community for 12 months, an optional prior-year tax return review plus a CPA Implementation Brief, 100+ lessons for life with templates and the Legacy Wealth AI Advisor, a partner network at client pricing (separate fees, confirm before you hire), and a second seat for a spouse or direct family member.

You keep your CPA. We do not sign Form 1040. The brief is homework you hand to the person who files.

The program is built for people earning $100K or more, whether that is a salary, self-employment, or a business. You do not have to buy a rental or start a company on day one. Which moves apply depends on your file.

Buyer scorecard

Print this. If you cannot check most boxes, do not book a call this week.

  • I can pay for a year of coaching without new credit-card debt.
  • I have last year's tax return, or an IRS transcript, in a folder.
  • I want a written 12-month order, not a one-off rental tactic.
  • I already have, or I will hire, a CPA or enrolled agent to file.
  • My 401(k) match is on, or I can explain why it is not. 2026 employee deferral limit is $24,500 (IRS IR-2025-111).
  • I can name the leak: tax, debt, idle cash, or a rental.
  • I read the FTC coaching page and I will not be rushed on a call.
  • I looked at reviews for named client work, not star widgets on this URL.
