Tax strategy course vs hiring a CPA

A course teaches the map. A CPA signs the return. Our plan writes the order you hand them.

A tax strategy course sells lessons. A CPA, a certified public accountant, is a licensed pro who can prepare and sign a return. A course cannot file for you. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is Preston Seo's 12-month wealth plan: a written snapshot from your numbers, a dedicated guide, weekly coaching, and a CPA Implementation Brief you hand to your own preparer. He built that after a corporate job, a house-hacked duplex, and a $15M+ portfolio with his wife. We do not file. We do not publish our tuition here. If you have a simple W-2 and no rental, a class is optional. If you own a business or a short-term rental, budget for a CPA even if you buy a class or a plan.

When a course is enough

You want vocabulary before a CPA meeting. You will still pay someone to file. You can do homework. You will not treat a module as a filing position.

Cheap examples we could copy: WealthAbility Live at $197 a month. Our tax program roundup lists other public prices, including classes that hide the number.

When you need a CPA or EA this year

You own a rental, a partnership, an S corp, or you sold a business. You got a notice from the IRS. You want someone who can represent you.

A CPA's fee is often not on a homepage. That does not make a $50,000 'done for you' firm the default. Ask for an engagement letter: what they will file, what they will not, and whether planning hours are extra.

W-2 limit in one line

A course cannot invent a deduction you do not have. After the 401(k) and HSA, most large ideas need a business or a rental. Read tax strategies for W-2 employees.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • Will this purchase file my return, or only teach me?
  • If I keep my CPA, what artifact do I hand them?
  • Is the teacher a CPA, EA, or neither?
  • Have I already maxed the 401(k) and HSA the IRS already allows?

Questions people ask

Can a tax course replace my CPA?

No. A course does not sign your return. Some firms sell both class and prep. Ask which hat they are wearing on your file.

Does Blueprint replace a CPA?

No. We write a plan and a brief. They sign Form 1040. Read what's in Blueprint.

What is an enrolled agent?

An EA is a tax pro licensed by the IRS to represent taxpayers. A CPA is licensed by a state. Either can be the right preparer. Ask about real estate or S corp experience if that is your fact pattern.
