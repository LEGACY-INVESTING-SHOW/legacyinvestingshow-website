Alternatives

SubTo alternatives, by the job you need done

SubTo is creative-finance school at a high-ticket price. Buy it only if that is the job.

SubTo is Pace Morby's creative-finance community. Creative finance here means buying a house in a way that is not a normal new bank loan. One method they teach is buying 'subject to' the seller's existing loan: the loan stays in the seller's name and you take over the payments. That can be legal in some cases. It also carries due-on-sale and title risk, so it is not a beginner toy. The join page we opened on September 15, 2026 listed the SubTo Accelerator at $9,800, or four payments of $2,450. If you do not want that tactic, pick a cheaper teacher or a different job: rental math, tax, or a written money plan.

What SubTo is

SubTo's homepage describes a community and training around creative financing. It does not print a checkout price. The buy.subto.com page we opened listed 'SubTo Accelerator' at $9,800 in full, or four monthly payments of $2,450. It also lists daily Zoom calls, recorded training, documents, and a member-success advisor. Treat those as seller claims. We did not sit in the calls.

The same homepage carries a disclaimer: figures are personal or from clients, results are not typical, and the average person who buys 'how to' information gets little or no result. That is their language, dated to the day we opened the page. It is a reason to slow down, not a reason to copy a testimonial.

You do not need an alternative if you already close normal financed deals and you specifically want this community. You need a different product if you have never underwritten a rental, if you still carry credit-card debt, or if the real pain is tax, not creative paper.

Alternatives by job

Start with the job. Then look at price.

  • Stay free: forums and podcasts

    Use BiggerPockets forums and podcasts to learn long-term rentals, house hacking, and basic underwriting at $0. This does not teach subject-to paperwork. It does teach whether a normal rental even makes sense.

    Job: learn rental math before any high-ticket call

  • BiggerPockets Pro

    Paid tools and forms on top of the free site. A March 3, 2026 staff post said Pro is $32.50 a month billed yearly. See what to know before Pro.

    Job: calculators and forms, not a mentor

  • KenPro (Ken McElroy)

    Rental and apartment lessons with a public price. Ken's store lists $39.99 a month or $399 a year. Closer to a course library than a creative-finance mastermind.

    Job: cheap paid lessons on conventional rentals

  • BetterLife (Brandon Turner)

    Coaching and community from a former BiggerPockets host. BetterLife did not publish a checkout price on the pages we opened. The Tribe page says it is for people who already did a deal this year.

    Job: coaching after you already own a deal. Price on request.

  • A local real estate attorney plus a normal lender

    If the job is 'can I take over this loan,' that is legal work, not a Zoom room. A lawyer in the property's state can read the note, the due-on-sale clause, and the title. A lender can tell you whether a new loan is even possible. Fees vary and are rarely on a homepage. This is the adult path when a live contract is on the table.

    Job: advice on one file. Not a course.

  • A fee-only planner or CPA

    Creative finance does not fix a W-2 tax bill. If the leak is payroll tax or an empty 401(k) match, buy hours from a licensed person. The FTC warns that coaching pitches which promise easy real estate profits are often a scam.

    Job: your tax file or household plan

  • Legacy Wealth Blueprint

    Our 12-month plan for tax, debt, income, investing, and protection. We publish $9,800 cash or card. Same sticker as SubTo Accelerator on the day we checked. Different job. Skip Blueprint if you only want subject-to documents. Details: Legacy Wealth Blueprint.

    Job: one written money plan. We sell this.

Watch for this

Subject-to and seller-finance deals can trip due-on-sale clauses, insurance, and servicing. A community screenshot is not a legal opinion. If a seller rushes you on a recorded call, read the FTC coaching page before you pay. Get the total price, refund rule, and who your advisor is, in writing.

How to choose in 20 minutes

Write one sentence: 'I need help with ___ this quarter.' If the blank is 'my first rental numbers,' stay free or buy KenPro. If the blank is 'this seller will not wait for a new loan,' that is lawyer-plus-lender work, and a $9,800 community may still not close that file. If the blank is 'my tax bill and my debt,' SubTo is the wrong aisle.

  • Do not buy SubTo to learn what a cap rate is. Free podcasts cover that.
  • Do not put a $9,800 coaching bill on a card you cannot pay. The FTC flags that pattern.
  • Read coaching vs a course before any high-ticket call.
  • If you work full time, see real estate courses for people with a day job.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • What job will this purchase do in the next 90 days that a free podcast will not?
  • Is the live price still $9,800, and is there a second payment or upsell after I join?
  • Who reviews my actual contract, and are they a licensed attorney in that state?
  • What happens to the seller's loan if the due-on-sale clause is enforced?
  • Send me the refund terms in writing before the call ends.

Questions people ask

How much does SubTo cost?

The buy.subto.com page we opened on September 15, 2026 listed $9,800, or four payments of $2,450. The main homepage did not show that number. Check the live checkout. Prices change.

Is SubTo a scam?

We do not call living brands scams from a homepage. SubTo itself says results are not typical. The FTC says real-estate coaching pitches that promise easy profits and rush the buy are a warning sign. Verify the legal work locally. Do not take our word, or theirs, as a verdict.

What is a cheaper SubTo alternative?

Free BiggerPockets content, then KenPro at $39.99 a month if you want lessons with a published price. Those will not replace a subject-to document set. They cost two orders of magnitude less while you learn whether you even want that tactic.

Is Legacy Wealth Blueprint a SubTo alternative?

Only if your job is a full money plan. It is not a creative-finance community. The cash price we publish is also $9,800. Same sticker, different syllabus. See Legacy Wealth Blueprint and reviews.
