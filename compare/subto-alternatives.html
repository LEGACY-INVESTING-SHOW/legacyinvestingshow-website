SubTo vs our wealth plan

SubTo is creative-finance school. Ours is a 12-month order for the income you already earn.

SubTo is Pace Morby's creative-finance community. One method they teach is buying 'subject to' the seller's existing loan: the loan stays in the seller's name and you take over the payments. That can be legal in some cases. It also carries due-on-sale and title risk. The join page we opened on September 15, 2026 listed the SubTo Accelerator at $9,800, or four payments of $2,450. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is Preston Seo's 12-month wealth plan: tax, debt, income, investing, and protection in one written order, plus a guide and a CPA brief. He built that after a corporate job, a house-hacked duplex, and a $15M+ portfolio with his wife. Use SubTo only if that tactic is the job. Use us if you want one written order. We do not publish our tuition here.

Preston Seo started in debt in 2017, left a corporate path, and with his wife built a $15M+ portfolio. He did not get there by taking over other people's loans as the whole plan. He got there by putting tax, cash flow, and ownership in one system. Creative finance can be a tool later. It is a poor first class if you have never underwritten a normal rental, or if the leak is a W-2 tax bill.

What SubTo is

SubTo's homepage describes a community and training around creative financing. It does not print a checkout price. The buy.subto.com page we opened listed 'SubTo Accelerator' at $9,800 in full, or four monthly payments of $2,450. It also lists daily Zoom calls, recorded training, documents, and a member-success advisor. Treat those as seller claims. We did not sit in the calls.

The same homepage carries a disclaimer: figures are personal or from clients, results are not typical, and the average person who buys 'how to' information gets little or no result. That is their language, dated to the day we opened the page.

You do not need an alternative if you already close normal financed deals and you specifically want this community. You need a different product if you have never underwritten a rental, if you still carry credit-card debt, or if the real pain is tax, not creative paper.

If you need neither product

Learn a normal rental first. These are not a guru ranking.

  • Stay free: forums and podcasts

    Use BiggerPockets forums and podcasts to learn long-term rentals and basic underwriting at $0. This does not teach subject-to paperwork.

    Job: learn rental math before any high-ticket call

  • A local real estate attorney plus a normal lender

    If the job is 'can I take over this loan,' that is legal work, not a Zoom room. A lawyer in the property's state can read the note, the due-on-sale clause, and the title.

    Job: advice on one file. Not a course.

Watch for this

Subject-to and seller-finance deals can trip due-on-sale clauses, insurance, and servicing. A community screenshot is not a legal opinion. If a seller rushes you on a recorded call, read the FTC coaching page before you pay. Get the total price, refund rule, and who your advisor is, in writing.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • Is the job this seller's loan, or a 12-month money order?
  • Is the live SubTo price still $9,800, and is there a second payment after I join?
  • Who reviews my actual contract, and are they a licensed attorney in that state?
  • What happens to the seller's loan if the due-on-sale clause is enforced?
