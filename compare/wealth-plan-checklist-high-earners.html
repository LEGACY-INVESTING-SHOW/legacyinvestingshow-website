Week one: export the facts

Put these in one folder. That folder is the plan's raw material.

  • Last two pay stubs, with 401(k), HSA, and match lines highlighted.
  • Last year's federal return, or a transcript from IRS.gov if you cannot find it.
  • A list of debts: balance, rate, and minimum, from each statement.
  • Cash accounts: checking, savings, money market. Note any emergency fund in months of spend. Use the emergency fund tool if you need a target.
  • Investment accounts: 401(k), IRA, brokerage. Write the balance only. Do not hunt for last year's return yet.
  • Insurance: health plan type (HSA-eligible or not), term life, disability if you have it.

Score the leaks

Circle the largest leak. For many W-2 households it is tax plus lifestyle debt. For others it is cash sitting idle after accounts are full. Do not start a rental module to fix a 22% card.

2026 room you can measure: employee 401(k) deferral $24,500, extra $8,000 at age 50, extra $11,250 instead at ages 60 to 63 (IRS IR-2025-111). HSA $4,400 or $8,750 (Rev. Proc. 2025-19).

A default order (change it with a CPA)

  1. High-interest consumer debt and a basic cash buffer. Budget: monthly budget planner.

  2. 401(k) at least to the match. Then HSA if you qualify.

  3. Remaining 401(k) room vs taxable investing vs a rental. See 401(k) vs rental.

  4. Tax ideas that need a business or property only after the above is in writing. Start at W-2 tax strategies.

  5. Estate and insurance questions last, with the right licensed person. A course is not a will.
