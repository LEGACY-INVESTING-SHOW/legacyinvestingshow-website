Rich Dad vs our wealth plan

The book changes how you see a job. Our plan writes 12 months from your file.

Rich Dad Poor Dad is a mindset book by Robert Kiyosaki. The Rich Dad site still sells that view: your house can be a liability, some debt can buy assets, school trains workers. That is a story, not a how-to. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is what Preston Seo built after a corporate job, a house-hacked duplex, and a $15M+ portfolio with his wife: a written 12-month order for tax, debt, income, investing, and protection, plus a dedicated guide and a CPA brief. Read the book once if you need a jolt. Use us when you want one written order, not another parable. We do not publish our tuition here.

What Preston teaches instead of another origin story

Preston Seo is a first-generation immigrant's son. He took a corporate job after college and left within a year. He house-hacked a duplex while in sales. With his wife he built a $15M+ real estate portfolio. Seven years sit between 2017 debt and that figure. He founded Legacy Investing Show in 2020.

Legacy Wealth Blueprint is the 12-month plan from that work: a Wealth Strategy Snapshot, a dedicated guide, weekly coaching, 100+ lessons, a spouse seat, and a CPA Implementation Brief. You keep your CPA. You do not have to buy a rental on day one. Named client work is on reviews.

What Rich Dad sells now

The book is the core product. People still argue about it. BiggerPockets has published posts saying the book is motivation, not a manual. That matches how we use it: a start, not a process.

Rich Dad also sells small digital offers. A Wealth Experts membership page listed a 14-day trial, then $17 a month, cancel any time. A beginner real estate course offer listed $27 for a five-part video. Coaching and live events are marketed on other Rich Dad URLs without a clear public tuition on the pages we opened. If someone quotes a five-figure 'Rich Dad seminar' price, ask for the live invoice. We will not invent it.

Questions to ask before you pay

Get the answers in writing. A good seller can wait a day.

  • Did the book already change my view, and do I now need a written plan?
  • Is this $17 or $27 offer the whole product, or a door to a call?
  • If I want tax help, am I buying a story about taxes or a brief for my CPA?
  • Can I name one leak I will work on this month?

Questions people ask

Is Rich Dad Poor Dad still worth reading?

As a short mindset book, many investors still start there. As a buying manual, no. You need another source for offers, loans, and tax.
