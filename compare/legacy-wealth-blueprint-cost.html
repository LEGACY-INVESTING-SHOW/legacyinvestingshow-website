Current sticker is $9,800 cash or card. $2,497 is not the list price.

Legacy Wealth Blueprint is our 12-month wealth plan. On September 15, 2026 we published $9,800 if you pay by cash or card, and $10,800 if you use Splitit financing. About $2,497

A student named Amanda is quoted in an older roundup saying the community alone is worth $2,497. That line is her value comment. It is not what Blueprint costs now. If you see $2,497 on an old tab, treat it as stale. The live cash price we publish is $9,800.

What the $9,800 is for

Blueprint is a written wealth plan plus a year of coaching. It covers tax, debt, income, investing, and protection. We do not file your Form 1040. You keep a CPA. We are not a law firm.

LWB Course plus AI at $1,500 is the lighter offer on the same FAQ. If you only want lessons and tools, that is the cheaper door. If you want a written plan and a coach on the calendar, that is the $9,800 door.

On the homepage and about page we say a $15M+ real estate portfolio. We do not use a higher figure here. Proof of client work is on reviews, with named interviews. This page will not paste those stories as a substitute for a price.

Coaching buyer scorecard

Print this. If you cannot check most boxes, do not buy this week.

  • I can pay $9,800 (or $1,500 for Course plus AI) without new credit-card debt.
  • I have last year's tax return, or an IRS transcript, in a folder.
  • I know whether I am buying lessons ($1,500) or a written plan plus coaching ($9,800).
  • I already have, or I will hire, a CPA or enrolled agent to file. Blueprint does not sign the return.
  • My 401(k) match is on, or I can explain why it is not. 2026 employee deferral limit is $24,500 (IRS IR-2025-111).
  • I can name the leak: tax, debt, idle cash, or a rental. I am not buying to 'figure it out later.'
  • I read the FTC coaching page and I will not be rushed on a call.
  • I looked at reviews for named client work, not star widgets on this URL.
