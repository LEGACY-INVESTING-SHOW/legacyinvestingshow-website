Alternatives

Dave Ramsey alternatives after the Baby Steps

Ramsey is strong on consumer debt. It is not the last word on tax or rentals.

Dave Ramsey's company, Ramsey Solutions, teaches a simple order: get out of consumer debt, then save, then invest. Financial Peace University is their nine-week class. Their class page listed $99.99 for a household on September 15, 2026, and a spouse is included. If you already live that plan, you may want a next step: a different dollar order, a tax class, or real estate school. Those are not 'better Ramsey.' They do a different job.

By Preston Seo, founder of Legacy Investing Show. Updated September 15, 2026. First published September 15, 2026.

How we chose

We compared Ramsey to other live education brands that high earners actually search after they finish the Baby Steps. We did not treat church budget classes as competitors unless they publish a national offer we could open.

  • The option is a money education brand or a licensed professional service, not a random app.
  • We can describe the method in plain words from a public page.
  • We do not repeat Ramsey marketing averages as our own facts.
  • We include our own program and say so.

What Ramsey Solutions sells

Financial Peace University is nine video lessons, a workbook, and three months of the EveryDollar budget app. You can take it in a group or on your own. The class finder said 'Only $99.99 to start FPU' when we opened it on September 15, 2026. The main FPU page also uses '$100' and '$80' in ads, so treat $99.99 as the class-page figure and confirm at checkout.

Ramsey Solutions also publishes the Baby Steps, a free order for debt and saving. The Ramsey Show is free to hear. You do not need to pay to learn the basic plan.

Ramsey is clear that almost all debt is bad, including most mortgages you could invest around. That view helps people who are drowning in credit cards. It clashes with people who already have a paid-off emergency fund and want a rental or a tax plan. Neither view is a moral test. It is a job fit.

If you already did the hard debt work

Pick the next teacher by the next problem.

  • The Money Guy Show (FOO)

    Brian Preston and Bo Hanson publish a nine-step Financial Order of Operations. The guide we opened was marked last updated July 29, 2026. The order is: cover insurance deductibles, take the employer 401(k) match, kill high-interest debt, build cash, then Roth and HSA, then max work plans. The guide and learn page listed the FOO course at $49 (the learn page called it a sale from $249). Full split: FOO vs Baby Steps.

    Job: a dollar order that invests earlier than Ramsey

  • A fee-only planner

    Pay a person who is paid only by you. Ask if they are a fiduciary, which means they must put your interest first. This is advice on your file, not a class. Fees vary and are often not on a homepage.

    Job: a plan for your household

  • A CPA for tax

    A certified public accountant is a licensed tax pro. If your pain is a six-figure W-2 bill, a class about envelopes will not change the return. See tax course vs hiring a CPA.

    Job: the actual tax return

  • BiggerPockets

    Use this only if you want to study rentals. Free podcasts first. Paid Pro is for calculators and forms. See what to know before BiggerPockets Pro.

    Job: rental education, not debt payoff

  • Legacy Wealth Blueprint

    Our 12-month plan for tax, debt, income, investing, and protection. We publish $9,800 cash or card. It is a poor fit if you still carry credit-card balances you cannot pay. Finish expensive debt first. Details: Legacy Wealth Blueprint.

    Job: one written plan after the basics. We sell this.

How to read Ramsey results

Ramsey Solutions advertises average class results, such as debt paid in 90 days. Those are their marketing claims. We did not audit them. Use them as a seller's statement, not as a promise to you.

When to stay with Ramsey

Stay if credit cards, car loans, or chaos around spending are still the main fire. A $99.99 class that makes two spouses talk once a week can be cheaper than a $9,800 plan you will not use.

Leave the Ramsey lane when the debt is gone, the 401(k) match is on, and the next question is tax, equity, or a business. That is a different library. Our wealth plan checklist is built for that stage.

Who it fits, and who should skip it

This page is useful if

  • You finished, or almost finished, consumer debt payoff and you want a next teacher.
  • You like Ramsey on spending, but you need tax or rental help he does not teach in depth.

Skip this path if

  • You still have high-interest credit-card debt and no budget. Start with Ramsey's free show or FPU.
  • You want us to dunk on Dave. We will not. The method works for a lot of households in debt.

Questions people ask

Is Financial Peace University worth $99.99?

It can be if you need a nine-week group to change spending. It will not lower a high W-2 tax bill by itself. Confirm the live checkout price. The main marketing page uses more than one number.

What is a fair Ramsey alternative for investing?

The Money Guy FOO is the closest free map that still puts high-interest debt early, then invests in Roth, HSA, and work plans. It is not anti-Ramsey. It just invests sooner than 'all debt including the house.' See FOO vs Baby Steps.

Should a high earner skip Ramsey?

Not if lifestyle debt is still running. A high salary does not cancel 22% credit cards. Skip Ramsey as your only teacher once that debt is gone and tax is the larger leak.
