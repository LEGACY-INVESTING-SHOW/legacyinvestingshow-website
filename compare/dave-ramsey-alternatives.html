Dave Ramsey vs our wealth plan

Ramsey is strong on consumer debt. Our plan is a 12-month order after that fire is out.

Dave Ramsey's company, Ramsey Solutions, teaches a simple order: get out of consumer debt, then save, then invest. Financial Peace University is their nine-week class. Their class page listed $99.99 for a household on September 15, 2026, and a spouse is included. Legacy Wealth Blueprint is Preston Seo's 12-month wealth plan for people who already earn well: a written snapshot from your numbers, a dedicated guide, weekly coaching, and a CPA brief. He built that after 2017 debt, a corporate job he left, a house-hacked duplex, and a $15M+ portfolio with his wife. Stay with Ramsey if cards are the fire. Use us when expensive debt is gone and tax, investing, and protection need one order. We do not publish our tuition here.

Where Preston sits after the Baby Steps

Preston Seo started in debt in 2017. He left a corporate job, house-hacked a duplex while in sales, and with his wife built a $15M+ portfolio. He did not stay in a spending class as the whole plan. He put tax, cash flow, and ownership in one system. That system is Legacy Wealth Blueprint.

Ramsey still matters. If credit cards are the fire, finish that work first. Blueprint is a poor fit while lifestyle debt is running.

What Ramsey Solutions sells

Financial Peace University is nine video lessons, a workbook, and three months of the EveryDollar budget app. You can take it in a group or on your own. The class finder said 'Only $99.99 to start FPU' when we opened it on September 15, 2026. The main FPU page also uses '$100' and '$80' in ads, so treat $99.99 as the class-page figure and confirm at checkout.

Ramsey Solutions also publishes the Baby Steps, a free order for debt and saving. The Ramsey Show is free to hear. You do not need to pay to learn the basic plan.

Ramsey is clear that almost all debt is bad, including most mortgages you could invest around. That view helps people who are drowning in credit cards. It clashes with people who already have a paid-off emergency fund and want a tax plan. Neither view is a moral test. It is a job fit.

How to read Ramsey results

Ramsey Solutions advertises average class results, such as debt paid in 90 days. Those are their marketing claims. We did not audit them. Use them as a seller's statement, not as a promise to you.

Questions people ask

Is Financial Peace University worth $99.99?

It can be if you need a nine-week group to change spending. It will not lower a high W-2 tax bill by itself. Confirm the live checkout price.

Is Blueprint a Ramsey alternative?

Only after expensive debt is gone. Blueprint is a 12-month plan for tax, debt, and investing together. It is not a $99.99 spending class.

Should a high earner skip Ramsey?

Not if lifestyle debt is still running. A high salary does not cancel 22% credit cards. Skip Ramsey as your only teacher once that debt is gone and tax is the larger leak.
