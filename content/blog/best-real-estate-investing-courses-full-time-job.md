If you have a full-time job, the best first step is free. Read the BiggerPockets forums and listen to the free podcasts until you can pick one model and one market. Pay for a program after that. Published 2026 prices in this list run from $399 a year for a membership up to $10,000 for a recorded masterclass. Two of the eight do not publish a price at all.

Below is what each one is, what it costs if the price is public, and who it fits. Every price here comes from a page we opened on the seller's own site. Where a price is not public, we say so instead of guessing.

## Do the free stuff first

Reddit threads about real estate coaching almost always have the same top comment. It says the information is free. That comment is partly right. The information is free. Structure, feedback, and other people's mistakes are not.

So start here, at no cost:

- **BiggerPockets forums and podcasts.** The [BiggerPockets Real Estate Podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/biggerpockets-real-estate-podcast/id594419649) is free, hosted by Dave Meyer and Henry Washington, with more than 1,400 episodes and new ones twice a week. [Real Estate Rookie](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/real-estate-rookie/id1499646507) is also free, hosted by Ashley Kehr and Tony J. Robinson, with around two episodes a week.
- **Ken McElroy's free channels.** His podcast and YouTube channel are linked from [kenmcelroy.com](https://kenmcelroy.com/), along with a free multifamily deal checklist.
- **Free live sessions.** [Wealthy Investor runs a free Cashflow 2.0 masterclass](https://wealthyinvestor.com/cashflow2o). [Infinity Investing runs free virtual workshops](https://infinityinvesting.com/).
- **Pace Morby's free content.** SubTo links its public YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram channels from [subto.com](https://subto.com/).

Give this 30 to 60 days. If you still cannot say which model you want and which city you want it in, a paid program will not fix that. It will just cost you money while you keep deciding.

## How to choose when your time is the scarce part

Money is not your tightest limit. Hours are. Use these four filters in order.

**1. Pick the model your calendar can carry.** Buy-and-hold rentals need a few hard weeks and then light upkeep. Flipping and creative finance need constant calls. Short-term rentals need daily attention at launch and then systems. If you have 5 hours a week, do not pick the model that needs 20.

**2. Match the format to how you actually learn.** Live weekly calls only help if you attend. If your job runs late, a self-paced library with an active chat beats a call you keep missing. Wealthy Investor's value is the call volume. KenPro's value is that you can watch at 11pm.

**3. Decide what you are buying: information or review.** A $399 membership gives you information. A high-ticket program should give you someone who reads your actual lease, your actual numbers, and tells you no. If it does not include that, you are paying course prices for a course.

**4. Ask what happens when you stall.** Everyone with a job stalls. Ask the seller what the program does in month three when you have not made an offer. If the answer is "watch module 4 again," keep looking.

## Legacy Wealth Blueprint: the honest version

What you invest depends on your situation and how you choose to pay, with payment plans and financing so the starting check is not what keeps a busy W-2 earner stuck. That covers lifetime access to the curriculum and 12 months of community and live support. You get weekly live coaching calls with replays, a custom 12-month Wealth Strategy Snapshot, a review of your prior year tax return, a CPA implementation brief near tax season, and a 1-on-1 guide with bi-weekly check-ins. A lighter curriculum-only path exists for applicants earning under $75,000, with the video curriculum plus the AI advisor and no calls.

There is no published money-back guarantee. If that matters to you, ask for the refund terms in writing before you pay.

**Who Legacy Wealth Blueprint is not for:**

- **People who want a deal-finding course.** It is a wealth program covering taxes, debt and credit, income, investing, and protection. If you only want to learn how to find and underwrite rentals, a BiggerPockets bootcamp or KenPro is a better fit and far cheaper.
- **People earning under about $75,000.** The math on tax strategy at that income usually calls for a lighter path, not the full coaching program.
- **People who would borrow to pay for it.** If the fee needs a card you cannot clear, the program is not the problem you should be solving first.
- **People who want short-term rental operations.** That is a different program. Airbnb Ascension is about acquiring and running the property, not about your whole balance sheet, and it is also matched to your situation rather than sold off a public menu.

## Before you pay anyone

Ask these five questions and get the answers in writing:

1. What is the total price, including any second payment or upsell?
2. What is the refund policy?
3. How long is my access, and what happens when it ends?
4. Who exactly reviews my deals, and how fast do they reply?
5. Can I speak to two people who joined in the last six months and are not in your ads?

A seller who answers all five plainly is worth a second call. A seller who gets vague on price or refunds has told you something useful.
