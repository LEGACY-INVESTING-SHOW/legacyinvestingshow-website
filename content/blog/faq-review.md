## The Business Model

### 16. Is rental arbitrage legal?

Yes, in most places.

Some cities have restrictions:
- San Francisco: 90-day annual cap
- New York: Strict regulations
- Some HOAs prohibit short-term rentals

We teach you how to research local laws before you start.

### 17. What if the landlord says no?

Most will.

That's normal.

You might message 20 landlords to get 1 yes. That's a 5% success rate.

Our scripts improve your odds, but rejection is part of the game.

### 18. How do I find landlords who allow Airbnb?

You don't find them. You convince them.

Most landlords have never considered Airbnb. You educate them on the benefits:
- Guaranteed rent (you pay even if unit is empty)
- Professional management (you treat it like a business)
- Higher quality tenants (you have incentive to maintain the unit)

### 19. What about damages and problems?

Part of the business.

Airbnb has host protection insurance.
You collect security deposits.
You screen guests.
You have cleaning protocols.

Most issues are minor. Broken glass. Stained carpet. Normal wear and tear.

### 20. How much time does this take?

Per unit, per week:

**Setup phase:** 10-15 hours
**Operational phase:** 2-3 hours

With systems and automation, you can manage multiple units in 10 hours/week total.
