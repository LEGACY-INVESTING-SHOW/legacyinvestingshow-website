## What Tax Alchemy actually is

Two different things share the Karlton Dennis name, and people mix them up.

[Tax Alchemy](https://taxalchemy.com/) is the firm. Its site says Karlton Dennis is CEO and lists tax strategy analysis, entity structuring, implementation help, real estate tax planning, cost segregation, and 1031 exchange work. It also offers tax preparation, but only to advisory clients. On pricing the site says the flat fee "can range from as little as $32k up to $50k."

[Tax Strategy Accelerator](https://www.taxstrategyaccelerator.com/) is the education product. The page lists video courses, a community, recordings of weekly calls with the private client group, and AMA sessions with tax advisors. The price on the page is "$97 Per Month" with a 14 day risk-free window.

So if you saw a $32,000 quote and went looking for options, you may only need the $97 membership. And if you wanted someone to do the work, a $97 membership will not do it. Get clear on which one you were shopping for first.

## How to choose

Answer four questions in order. They knock out most of the list fast.

**1. Do you want to learn it or hand it off?**

If you want to hand it off, you are shopping for a firm. That means Tax Alchemy, Tax Goddess, or a local CPA who does planning. Expect four or five figures and expect to be quoted on a call.

If you want to learn it, you are shopping for education. Tax Strategy Accelerator, Navi Maraj's course, Business Owner HQ, and Legacy Wealth Blueprint sit here.

**2. Do you already have a business or a rental?**

This is the question that decides how much any program can do for you. Entity planning needs a business. Depreciation and cost segregation need property. If you are a W-2 earner with neither, a program cannot invent deductions for you. It can only help you build the thing that creates them.

**3. Do you need answers or do you need a plan?**

Anderson Platinum and Business Owner HQ are built for questions. You bring a situation, you get an answer, you move on. That works when you already know your direction.

A plan is different. You want someone to look at your whole picture once and tell you the order of operations. That is what a written wealth plan or a paid planning engagement gives you.

**4. What is the total, in writing, before the call?**

Every seller in this category sells on a call. Ask for the full price, the payment terms, the refund window, and what happens when the term ends. Get it in an email. If the number only appears after a 45 minute pitch, that is information too.

## What to check before you pay anyone

1. Find the price on the seller's own page, not on a review site.
2. Ask what happens at the end of the term. Does access stop?
3. Ask who answers your questions. The founder, a team, or a forum?
4. Ask whether tax filing is included. Usually it is not.
5. Read the refund policy before the call, not after.
6. Search the name plus "refund" and read what comes back with a cool head.

A program is worth its price when it changes what you actually do. Nothing on this list works if you buy it and never open it.
