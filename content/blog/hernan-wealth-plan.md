**2. Cost Segregation Study Implementation:**

A cost segregation study accelerates depreciation by identifying building components with shorter depreciable lives:

*Typical Segregation Results:*
- 5-year property: Personal property, furniture, fixtures (15-20% of building value)
- 7-year property: Specialty electrical, plumbing (5-10%)
- 15-year property: Land improvements, parking, landscaping (10-15%)
- 27.5-year property: Building structure (55-70%)

*Study Investment and ROI:*
- Professional study cost: $3,000-$7,000 per property
- Present value of accelerated deductions: $15,000-$35,000
- ROI on study: 300-500%
- Recommended for properties $200,000+

**3. Opportunity Zone Investment Strategy:**

Opportunity Zones offer three tax benefits for capital gains reinvestment:

*Benefit 1: Deferral*
- Reinvest capital gains into Qualified Opportunity Fund (QOF) within 180 days
- Defer tax on original gain until 2026 or earlier sale

*Benefit 2: Reduction*
- 5-year holding: 10% of deferred gain eliminated
- 7-year holding: Additional 5% eliminated (total 15%)
- Note: 2026 deadline means 7-year benefit no longer available for new investments

*Benefit 3: Exclusion*
- 10+ year holding: 100% of appreciation on QOF investment excluded from tax

*Hernan's Application:*
- Realize gain from asset sale: $50,000
- Reinvest in QOF within 180 days
- Defer $50,000 gain taxation
- If held 10+ years: Full appreciation on QOF investment tax-free

**4. 1031 Exchange Planning for Portfolio Scaling:**

Tax-deferred exchanges enable portfolio growth without capital gains tax erosion:

*Exchange Requirements:*
- Like-kind property (real estate for real estate)
- 45-day identification period (3 property rule)
- 180-day closing deadline
- Qualified Intermediary required
- Same or greater debt and equity in replacement property

*Strategic Application:*
- Year 5-7: Exchange Property 1 (appreciated) for Properties 4 and 5
- Defer $75,000-$150,000 in capital gains
- Upgrade to larger or multiple properties
- Continue exchange chain indefinitely

*Estate Planning Integration:*
- Step-up in basis at death eliminates all deferred gains
- "Swap until you drop" strategy eliminates tax permanently
- Generational wealth transfer opportunity

**5. Qualified Business Income (QBI) Deduction:**

Section 199A provides 20% deduction of qualified business income:

*Eligibility:*
- Pass-through entity income (LLC, S-Corp, partnership)
- Not a specified service trade or business (SSTB) at high income levels
- W-2 wages or property basis limitations at high income

*Application:*
- Annual rental net income: $24,000
- QBI deduction: $4,800 (20%)
- Tax savings at 24%: $1,152
- Phase-out consideration: Begins at $170,000 single / $340,000 MFJ (2025)

**6. Installment Sale Strategy:**

Spread gain recognition over multiple years for bracket management:

*Application:*
- Sell appreciated property: $100,000 gain
- Receive payment over 5 years: $20,000/year
- Stay in 12% bracket vs. jumping to 22%
- Potential tax savings: $10,000+

*Risks:*
- Buyer default risk
- Time value of money consideration
- Legislative risk (rate changes)

**7. Short-Term Rental (STR) Tax Loophole Evaluation:**

If considering STR conversion:

*Material Participation Requirements:*
- Substantial involvement in operations (not passive)
- Self-management vs. property management distinction
- 7-day average stay (vs. 30-day long-term rental)

*Benefits:*
- Losses potentially deductible against ordinary income
- Business expense deductions (travel, education, home office)
- Augusta Rule (rent home to business 14 days tax-free)
- Bonus depreciation on furnishings (100% in some years)

*Worcester STR Considerations:*
- Local registration requirements
- State room occupancy tax: 5.7%
- Local excise tax: up to 6%
- Operational intensity vs. tax benefits trade-off
