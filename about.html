My Story

In 2017, I was living what many would call a "normal" life. I had a steady job, but I also had $80,000 in debt and a growing realization that trading my time for money wasn't going to get me where I wanted to be.

I stumbled upon Airbnb arbitrage almost by accident. A friend mentioned they were renting out a spare room, and something clicked. What if I could do this at scale? What if I could build a business around short-term rentals without owning property?

I started with one unit. Then two. Then five. Within 18 months, I had paid off all my debt and replaced my corporate income. But more importantly, I had discovered something that could change lives.

That's why I created Legacy Investing Show. Not just to teach people about Airbnb arbitrage, but to show them that financial freedom isn't reserved for the wealthy or the lucky. It's available to anyone willing to learn and take action.
