My Story

In 2017, I was living what many would call a normal life. I had income, but I also had $80,000 in debt and a growing realization that earning more without a system for taxes, cash flow, and investing was never going to create real freedom.

Real estate and Airbnb were part of the breakthrough, but they were not the whole answer. The bigger shift came from learning how taxes, ownership, cash flow, and decision-making all fit together.

I started applying the same principle everywhere: make the next money decision with a clear framework, not a guess. That meant better tax moves, better asset choices, better income design, and better execution.

That is why I created Legacy Investing Show. Not to sell one tactic, but to teach the broader wealth system that helps professionals, investors, and founders keep more of what they earn and build lasting optionality.

The Journey

From debt to financial freedom, here's how the journey unfolded.

2017

The Wake-Up Call

$80,000 in debt, working a 9-5, and realizing the traditional path wasn't going to build the life I wanted. Something had to change.

2018

First Real Money Experiments

I started testing the ideas in real life: income experiments, ownership decisions, tax moves, and real estate. Some worked immediately. Others taught expensive lessons.

2019

Debt Free

I paid off all $80K in debt and kept stacking better financial decisions. That was the point where the system started to feel real instead of theoretical.

2020

Legacy Investing Show Born

I started teaching the frameworks that changed my life. The response was immediate because people were tired of generic advice and wanted something they could actually execute.

2024

2,000+ Students Guided

Thousands of students have used the content, tools, and frameworks to improve taxes, cash flow, investing decisions, and long-term wealth. The system keeps compounding.

What I Believe

These principles guide everything I teach and how I run Legacy Investing Show.

Action Over Perfection

The best time to start was yesterday. The second best time is today. Don't wait for perfect conditions - they don't exist.

Systems Over Hacks

One clever move is not enough. The goal is to build a repeatable system for taxes, cash flow, investing, and execution.

Actionable Over Abstract

I care less about sounding smart and more about helping you make the next right move with enough clarity to actually execute it.

Long-Term Thinking

Building wealth isn't about get-rich-quick schemes. It's about consistent action over time to create lasting financial freedom.

Taxes Are A Wealth Lever

High earners often focus on earning more and ignore the leak on the back end. Better tax decisions can change the entire compounding curve.

Legacy Mindset

This isn't just about making money. It's about building something that lasts - for your family and future generations.
