What drives Colorado renters insurance costs

Colorado renters paid about $147 a year on average in the NAIC 2021 baseline, about $23 less than the $170 US average.

Weather exposure is a major driver here: hail, wildfires, and winter storms all contribute to loss patterns.

Claim frequency and litigation also matter: claim frequency runs moderate, with hailstorms the most common trigger.

Replacement costs and carrier competition round out the picture: replacement values along the Front Range are above the national norm, and many carriers compete, especially in the Denver market.
