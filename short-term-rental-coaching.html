The best short-term rental coaching programs, by model and by fit

Pick the model before the coach. Arbitrage, ownership and co-hosting are three different businesses, and most bad purchases come from buying a program for the wrong one.

What STR coaching actually teaches

'Short-term rental coaching' covers four different curricula. Knowing which one you are buying is most of the decision.

Acquisition: you buy the property

Market selection, underwriting from comparable listings, financing (often DSCR loans), design and furnishing, operations, and the tax structure only owners get: depreciation, cost segregation and the short-term rental rules. Highest capital, slowest start, and the only model that builds equity.

Arbitrage: you lease, then sublet nightly

Landlord outreach and the pitch, lease clauses that permit subletting, market selection where short stays are legal, furnishing on a budget, pricing and guest operations. Lower capital, faster start, no equity, and the whole business rests on written landlord consent. Our arbitrage training guide goes deeper.

Co-hosting: you run someone else's listing

Finding owners, the management agreement, the revenue share, guest communication and turnover systems. Almost no capital. Your hard part is winning clients and keeping them.

The tax angle

A short-term rental you own can, if the average stay is seven days or less and you materially participate, produce a loss that is not passive and can offset W-2 income, especially after a cost segregation study. Some programs are sold on this angle alone. It applies to owners, not to arbitrage operators, and it depends on your hours and your records. See the short-term rental loophole guide.

The first question

Do you want equity and the tax benefits (buy), cash flow with less capital (arbitrage), or income with almost no capital (co-host)? Answer that before you compare coaches.

The ranked list

Ordered for a working professional who wants monthly cash flow from a first short-term rental within a few months and wants a person to check the deal before committing. If you are buying rather than leasing, or learning for free first, the fit table below reorders it.

1. Airbnb Ascension, built on the Airbnb Arbitrage Roadmap (Preston Seo)

Format: 6-month 1-on-1 coaching program scoped to one arbitrage property; a 12-month Scale version repeats the model for as many properties as you can execute · Program page

Rental arbitrage with a dedicated coach. You lease a property in a market where short stays are allowed, sign a 12-month lease, furnish it, list on Airbnb, VRBO and Booking.com, and target cash flow of roughly two to three times the monthly lease cost. The Airbnb Arbitrage Roadmap is the 7+ hour self-paced curriculum inside the program, with lifetime access to the classroom and community; it is not sold on its own.

The people and the process are the product: a 1-on-1 STR coach (a 30 to 45 minute onboarding call, day-to-day access for questions and deal reviews, ad-hoc calls), Property Profitability Reports on deals before you sign (market analysis, cash-flow projection, ROI, tax impact), a Market Matrix ranking the top 500 markets, weekly live Zoom calls plus 18+ months of recordings, the Airbnb Association community with messaging access to Preston and the coaches, weekly progress reports against a 6-month implementation plan, an STR AI advisor, and a business setup, credit and tax concierge session. Coaches vouch for students as references with landlords. Done-for-you pieces cover listing design, furnishing optimization, operations setup, messaging automation and dynamic pricing configuration; you do the market research and the landlord calls, with scripts, roleplay and coaching.

Who it fits

  • Someone with a job who wants cash flow from a side business, has startup capital for a deposit and furnishing, and wants to be earning within the first one to two months
  • People whose blocker is confidence on the landlord call or fear of a bad lease, not a lack of information
  • People who want the lease and the numbers reviewed by someone before they sign

Who it does not fit

  • People testing the idea; a cheap self-paced course and 20 landlord calls will tell you whether you like the work
  • People who want equity or the owner-only tax benefits; arbitrage builds neither
  • People in cities with primary-residence rules or bans on stays under 30 days
  • People who want a published refund guarantee; none is published

What to verify before buying

  • Ask what a Property Profitability Report looks like on a real deal and who reviews it
  • Ask how the 6-month plan is paced and what weeks one to four look like
  • Confirm your target city allows short stays before the call, and get term and refund terms in writing

2. STR Concierge (Preston Seo)

Format: Done-for-you acquisition of an owned short-term rental, about 6 to 7 months for one property; a 12-month Portfolio version repeats the model · Program page

The ownership and tax-angle option. The team sources and underwrites five to ten deals, sets up operations two to three weeks after closing, and builds a tax-optimized structure from day one (entity, cost segregation and bonus depreciation planning coordinated with CPAs). Each property comes with a full underwriting package (projected gross revenue from comps, operating expenses, monthly and annual net cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, a cost segregation estimate and year-one tax impact), a direct-booking website, a vetted referral network (DSCR lenders, cost segregation firms, STR-specialist CPAs, investor-focused agents, designers, property managers), 30-day and 90-day performance reviews, the full STR video curriculum, and a second membership for a spouse or family member.

Who it fits

  • W-2 earners or business owners with substantial investable capital who want cash flow, tax savings and equity from a property they own, plan to buy within about four months, and value time over learning to do it themselves
  • Households that can put in the hours to meet a material participation test and keep the log

Who it does not fit

  • Anyone without the capital to buy; this is not an arbitrage program
  • Anyone who wants to learn underwriting themselves rather than have it done
  • Anyone who assumes the tax result is automatic; STR losses offset W-2 income only when the owner qualifies under the rules and materially participates, and the final treatment is for your CPA

What to verify before buying

  • Ask to see a sample underwriting package and how the year-one tax impact is calculated
  • Ask how the coordination with your own CPA works and who signs the position
  • Ask what happens if no deal passes underwriting inside the term

Disclosure

STR Concierge is our program. Pricing is not published. The tax angle is stated by the program as a possibility that depends on your facts, not a promise.

6. STR University (STR Uni)

Format: Ownership-focused short-term rental education: buying, designing and operating properties you own

Education for people who intend to buy: market selection, underwriting, design and the guest experience, with a strong emphasis on standing out through the property itself. It is not an arbitrage program. Confirm the current course lineup, format and terms on its own site, because catalogues in this category change often.

Who it fits

  • Buyers with capital who want to learn acquisition and design themselves rather than have it done
  • Owners who want to improve an existing property's design and revenue

Who it does not fit

  • Anyone without the capital to buy
  • Anyone who wants arbitrage or co-hosting

What to verify before buying

  • Confirm what is self-paced versus coached, and who the coaches are
  • Ask whether underwriting is taught with a template you keep
  • Get refund terms in writing

7. BiggerPockets STR content

Format: Forums, podcasts, books (including a widely read STR title) and bootcamps; a Pro membership with calculators; pricing published · Our alternatives guide

The cheapest serious way to learn ownership fundamentals: how to underwrite, finance and operate, plus thousands of public member stories in the forums. The STR material is ownership-focused. Coaching is limited to cohort bootcamps; there is no dedicated coach.

Who it fits

  • Anyone learning the fundamentals before paying for coaching
  • Buyers who want to underwrite their own first deal

Who it does not fit

  • Arbitrage operators; the model is barely covered
  • People who want a named person reviewing their deal

What to verify before buying

  • Decide whether the Pro tools are worth it to you versus the free forums
  • Check the bootcamp format and cohort size before buying one

Enough to learn the vocabulary, hear real operators describe a normal month, and decide which model you want. Red flags to check in any STR coaching program

Run every program, including ours, through these.

  • No regulation module. If the program does not make you check your city's short-term rental rules before anything else, it is selling a fantasy. Primary-residence rules and 30-day minimums end the arbitrage model outright.
  • No written landlord-consent step. For arbitrage, permission to sublet must be in the lease. A program that treats it as a conversation rather than a clause is teaching you to build on sand.
  • Income claims without named people. 'Students make X a month' with no names, dates or interviews is marketing. Ask for people you can watch or speak to.
  • 2021 assumptions. Underwriting taught at 80% occupancy and peak-year rates. Any honest model runs at 50% occupancy first.
  • No deal review before you sign. The fee for a coached program buys people. If nobody reads your lease and your numbers before you commit, you have bought videos at a coaching price.
  • Guarantees of results. Nobody can promise occupancy, a property count or a dollar figure. A program that does is telling you how it sells.
  • Coaches who no longer operate. Ask when your coach last signed a lease or closed on a property, and in which market.
  • Terms only on a call. Common, including with us, and not a red flag by itself. It becomes one if the seller will not put total, term and refund terms in an email when you ask.
  • The fee eats your working capital. If paying for the program leaves nothing for deposits, furniture and a few months of rent, you have bought a course instead of a business.

The STR tax angle: who it applies to and who it does not

The reason some programs sell on tax: a rental you own is normally passive, so its losses cannot offset W-2 income. If the average guest stay is seven days or less, the activity is not a rental activity under the passive loss rules, and if you also pass a material participation test (the realistic one for someone with a job is more than 100 hours and more hours than anyone else), the loss becomes non-passive. A cost segregation study then front-loads depreciation into year one. The full walkthrough is in the short-term rental loophole guide.

Three things to know before you buy a program on this angle. First, it is for owners; an arbitrage operator does not own the building and has nothing to depreciate beyond furniture and setup costs. Second, a full-service property manager usually logs more hours than you, which ends the 100-hour test; the cost segregation versus STR loophole comparison explains why a usable loss matters more than a headline deduction. Third, the REPS versus STR loophole comparison covers why real estate professional status is unrealistic with a full-time job while the STR path is a property and a log. Depreciation comes back at sale as recapture; see depreciation recapture on a short-term rental.

Any program that mentions the tax angle should tell you all three things without being pushed. If it does not, that is a red flag for its underwriting too.

Who should not buy any STR program

Six readers who should keep their money for now.

  • Your city does not allow it. Check zoning and licensing for the addresses you can afford before anything else. Our guide on whether Airbnb arbitrage is legal shows what to check.
  • You have no reserve. Deposits, furniture and a few months of rent, or a down payment and closing costs, come before any program fee. If the fee is your capital, wait.
  • You cannot give it real hours for the first 60 days. Launch is front-loaded: calls, setup, first guests. After that it calms down.
  • You want it passive from day one. The operators in the interviews above got to two to four hours a week after systems were built, not before.
  • You have not decided the model. Buy, lease or co-host. A program for one is the wrong program for the other two.
  • You want a guaranteed outcome. No program can promise one, and ours does not.

Questions people ask

What is the best short-term rental coaching program?

It depends on the model. For arbitrage with a dedicated coach and deal review before you sign, Airbnb Ascension, which is our program. For buying a property with the tax angle done for you, STR Concierge, also ours. For a cheap, refundable self-paced start, Sean Rakidzich's courses. For ownership fundamentals at the lowest cost, BiggerPockets. Pick the model first.

What does short-term rental coaching actually teach?

Four different things depending on the program: acquisition (buying, financing, underwriting, design), arbitrage (the landlord pitch, lease clauses, furnishing, pricing, guest operations), co-hosting (winning owner clients and running their listings), and the tax angle for owners (the seven-day average stay test, material participation, cost segregation).

Is Airbnb Ascension the same as the Airbnb Arbitrage Roadmap?

The Airbnb Arbitrage Roadmap is the 7+ hour self-paced curriculum inside Airbnb Ascension. It is not sold on its own. Ascension adds the dedicated 1-on-1 coach, Property Profitability Reports before you sign, weekly live calls, the community and the done-for-you setup pieces.

How much does Airbnb Ascension cost?

Pricing is discussed on the strategy call and is not published. What is published is the scope: a 6-month program for one property with a dedicated coach, or a 12-month Scale version for as many properties as you can execute, with no property count or financial result guaranteed.

Can STR coaching help me cut my W-2 taxes?

Only the ownership path can. A short-term rental you own with an average stay of seven days or less, where you materially participate, can produce a non-passive loss that offsets W-2 income, especially after cost segregation. Arbitrage does not, because you do not own the building. Read the STR loophole guide and confirm your facts with a CPA.

What are the red flags in an STR coaching program?

No regulation check, no written landlord-consent step, income claims without named people, underwriting at 2021 occupancy, no deal review before you sign, guarantees of results, and coaches who no longer operate. Price only on a call is common and not a red flag by itself, unless the seller will not put terms in writing when asked.

How long does it take to get a first short-term rental live?

In the documented client interviews on this site, arbitrage clients went from joining to a signed lease in roughly four to six weeks and from lease to first guest within a few more; the fastest booked $25,000 in their first two weeks live. Others took six months of research first. Your city, your capital and how many landlord calls you make decide it.

Sources to check

Check primary guidance and your own records before you treat any page as a final answer.

  • IRS Publication 946 and depreciation guidance
  • IRS passive activity rules (Publication 925)
  • Current IRS forms, instructions, and publications for the relevant tax year
  • Your actual account statements, payroll reports, entity records, and advisor memos

  • Check the short-term rental rules for three zip codes you can afford.
  • Run one deal at 50% occupancy in the arbitrage ROI calculator or the break-even occupancy calculator.
  • Email any program you shortlist for term, refund terms and who reviews your deal.
